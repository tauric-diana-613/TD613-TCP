# The 8-Stage Dollhouse Design Authoring Cycle

The `/dollhouse-design` engine implements an 8-stage operational sequence to prevent premature formal closure and template clichés.

---

## 1. Commission (Ingress)
A commission enters as an intellectual seed $s$, accompanied by an optional medium $m$ and the six Human Coordinates:
$$\mathcal{H}_q = (\mathrm{WHAT}, \mathrm{WHO}, \mathrm{WHERE}, \mathrm{HOW}, \mathrm{WHEN}, \mathrm{WHY})$$

These coordinates are evaluated as three fundamental couplings:
- **$\mathrm{WHAT} \leftrightarrow \mathrm{WHO}$:** The object created vs. the human observer/actor whose encounter gives it consequence.
- **$\mathrm{WHERE} \leftrightarrow \mathrm{HOW}$:** The material medium/viewport vs. the operational arrangement enabling legibility.
- **$\mathrm{WHEN} \leftrightarrow \mathrm{WHY}$:** The temporal cadence/sequence of transformation vs. the teleological purpose.

---

## 2. Select Instruments
The Conductor evaluates which formal tradition best serves the inquiry:
- For multi-trajectory or transmedia translation: **`PHASONIC_COUNTERPOINT`**
- For bureaucratic, spatial, or institutional friction: **`CARO_INSTITUTIONAL_RECONSTRUCTION`**
- For iterative convention, subversion, or normative drift: **`BUTLER_PERFORMATIVE_ITERATION`**
- For textural vulnerability, care, and anti-paranoid survival: **`SEDGWICK_REPARATIVE_TEXTURE`**
- For unresolvable philosophical or structural disputes: **`DIALECTICAL_TENSION`**

Instruments may be paired, with one acting as scaffold and the second as counterlayer.

---

## 3. Generate Alternatives (Propagation)
Before drafting or coding, the seed may be studied through structurally non-equivalent internal formal propositions. These are design studies, not file deliverables. 
- *Candidate A* might prioritize temporal suspense and retrospective revelation.
- *Candidate B* might prioritize immediate consequence and procedural friction.

---

## 4. Compose
The Conductor applies the remaining generative verbs:
- **Compression:** Stripping away decorative ornament while strictly retaining declared relational invariants ($R(s) \subseteq R(c)$).
- **Coupling:** Ensuring that changes in one dimension (e.g. typography) alter another (e.g. motion pacing or argumentative gravity).
- **Transition:** Projecting the relational seed into the material constraints of the target medium ($\Pi_m(s)$).
- **Stabilization:** Allowing the work to settle into a definite, inspectable boundary.

---

## 5. Materialize
The engine produces the operator-requested number of finished working artifacts (default: exactly one): complete standalone HTML5 code, a fully articulated scholarly essay, a rigorous theoretical proof, or another commissioned medium. Outlines and promissory descriptions are rejected. A receipt is not itself a finished artifact.

---

## 6. Refract
The engine mutates exactly one governing formal parameter:
- Inverting the chronological sequence.
- Replacing the primary typeface with an antithetical family.
- Removing an interactive affordance to test if the meaning survives.
- Exaggerating an interference peak.

This produces an **internal counterfactual design study**, not an additional deliverable. It may strengthen the one finished artifact; external materialization requires an explicit operator request.

---

## 7. Compare
The engine analyzes the primary approach and internal counterfactual:
- What survived the refraction?
- What was destroyed by the change?
- Which formal choice was truly consequential versus merely decorative?

---

## 8. Return (Human Rest)
The engine returns **exactly the artifacts explicitly commissioned**, one by default. Neither the counterfactual study nor the comparison requires a second file, variant, or visual mode. Experimental A/B paired-output requirements apply only inside an explicitly commissioned controlled comparison; they never override ordinary commissions. The system rests; human selection and closure remain required.

---

## 9. The Five Epistemic Registers of Authorship

To preserve scientific and artistic integrity, the system distinguishes five operational registers:
1. **Deterministic Commission Analysis:** Heuristic keyword and cluster matching (`analyzeSeedSemantics`) identifying semantic tensions in the input seed.
2. **Structural Composition Receipt:** Conductor output (`runDollhouseDesignCycle`) declaring formal propositions, human coordinate pairings, and explicit causal contracts.
3. **Creative Agent Materialization:** The downstream implementation of executable code (HTML, CSS, JS) or prose that embodies the structural receipt.
4. **Executed Causal Mechanisms:** Operative mathematical parameters that measurably alter layout, geometry, typography, or grammar, falsifiable by perturbation probes (distinguished from decorative telemetry).
5. **Experimental Observations & Human Artistic Judgments:** Empirical inspection of browser execution and sovereign human choice (Palate remains unborn).
