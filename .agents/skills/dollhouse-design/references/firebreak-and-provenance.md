# Scientific Firebreak & Instrument Provenance

The integrity of TD613 depends on maintaining an impenetrable firebreak between creative generative synthesis and empirical scientific audit.

---

## 1. The Constitutional Firebreak

```text
[CREATIVE GENERATIVE DOMAIN]             [SCIENTIFIC VERIFICATION DOMAIN]
/.agents/skills/dollhouse-design/   <--|  /.agents/skills/dollhouse-audit/
                                       |
- OPEN_FIELD_CREATIVE_SYNTHESIS        |  - FAIL-CLOSED SCIENTIFIC AUDIT
- Operates via creative verbs          |  - Operates via restored domain engines
- Proposes aesthetic forms             |  - Audits conditioning, fibers, non-retroactivity
- Palate remains UNBORN                |  - Orchestrator preserves specialist vetoes
- release_authority: false             |  - release_authority: false
- scientific_proof: false              |  - scientific_proof: verified-head evidence only
```

- **One-Way Advisory Calling:** `/dollhouse-design` may invoke `/dollhouse-audit` to perform an advisory check on whether a mathematical equation, temporal timestamp sequence, or coordinate mapping contains internal contradictions.
- **Zero Evidence Credit:** Calling the scientific audit from a creative run never generates empirical evidence credit (`EMPIRICAL_CREDIT_0`). Creative synthesis can never cite its own generated outputs as scientific proof.
- **No Conflation of Roles:** The creative Design Conductor is strictly separate from the scientific Orchestrator. The Conductor cannot issue an Orchestration decision code (`SUPPORTED`, `HELD`).

---

## 2. Instrument Provenance Tracking

Every commissioned artifact can carry or expose an **Instrument Provenance Ledger** within that artifact or its internal receipt; the ledger never authorizes an additional externally delivered file:
- Which specific instrument was selected?
- Which mathematical formula or theoretical concept was adapted?
- What was genuinely computed by code versus what was authored as a creative prompt?
- Which formal feature of the finished artifact was directly shaped by that operation?

---

## 3. The Unborn Specialist (`Palate`)

The proposed sixth specialist, `Palate` (Aesthetic Judgment), remains constitutionally **unborn**. 
- Aesthetic merit cannot be reduced to a scalar score, a pass/fail classifier, or an algorithmic threshold.
- Instead of an automated aesthetic judge, `/dollhouse-design` supports **internal Refractive Comparison**: examining a counterfactual design choice without obligating the author to create or deliver a second work. A paired visual comparison is produced only if the operator requests it.
- Final aesthetic judgment, selection, and closure belong exclusively to the human operator.
