In a declared linear reconstruction, rank below latent dimension leaves unresolved directions; full rank does not ensure numerical stability. Read the originals before converting a formulation into an assay. Source-defined mathematics, declared speculation, finite synthetic witnesses, browser liveness and empirical acquisition remain different evidence classes.

## When to reach for Aperture

Reach for Aperture when work materially involves one or more of:

- admissibility, narrowing, capacity, projection, or controlled-surface analysis;
- observability versus identifiability;
- reconstruction from multiple declared observations;
- operator rank, nullity, conditioning, or numerical fragility;
- uncertainty, covariance, correlated noise, missing reliability, or invalid noise geometry;
- widening / additional-observation proposals;
- abstention, rejection, null results, or unresolved alternatives;
- source drift, signed residue, held-out validation, replay, or tamper posture;
- route-memory consequences that depend on what survived the observation aperture;
- a Pedagogue-proposed question that needs a reconstruction/uncertainty audit before it deserves to be asked.

Ordinary copy edits, styling, and non-semantic mechanical repairs do not need Aperture merely because the repository contains telemetry.

## No-crown / authority boundary

Aperture may recommend, reconstruct, compare, abstain, reject, preserve residue, and replay within its declared jurisdiction.

It may not by itself:

- take Ash custody;
- execute a sensor or physical experiment;
- authorize release/export;
- mutate a consequential route;
- promote a research refinement into production;
- infer hidden intent or external-world truth from numerical closure;
- convert route order into connection, curvature, or holonomy;
- convert an analogue mapping into quantum identity.

The bridge carries receipts, not power.

## Dollhouse falsifier-driven witness-plan companion

`app/engine/dollhouse-witness-plan.js` supplies the external, declaration-only
`auditDollhouseWitnessPlan(plan)` adapter. Its versioned input schema is
`td613.dollhouse.witness-plan/v0.1`; its output is
`td613.dollhouse.witness-plan-receipt/v0.1`. This bounded companion does **not**
promote a standalone candidate, change the installed Aperture identity/tool,
or replace the v3.2 numerical deficit/uncertainty audit.

Each claim declares its finite observation scope, instrument, evidence class,
positive witness, hostile counterexample, unresolved alternatives and required
next observation (or explicit `null`). The plan requires `execution: false`.
Any proposed next observation retains `execution: false` and
`authority: HUMAN_APPROVAL_REQUIRED`; the adapter performs no tool/provider calls.

The supported bounded claim classes cover finite literal exclusion, signed
predecessor continuity, scoped behavior, scoped policy enforcement and empirical
episode observations. Unsupported claims, malformed declarations and widened
authority return `REJECT`. A declared discriminating observation returns
`PROPOSE`; missing references or unresolved alternatives without a next
observation return `ABSTAIN`. `ASK_NOTHING` means only that this bounded plan
declares no further question and contains witness/falsifier references. It
earns no claim verification, source authentication or empirical credit.

Evidence classes remain distinct: `DECLARATION`, `OFFLINE_TEST`,
`BROWSER_WITNESS`, `PROVIDER_RESPONSE`, `EMPIRICAL_ACQUISITION`. Caller-provided
references remain unauthenticated even when labelled empirical. The adapter
retains an explicit ceiling for each class rather than promoting one into
another. It also retains these counterclaims independently of plan completion:

- A signed predecessor alone leaves global latest state, replay resistance and
  signer/source authenticity unresolved.
- Excluding a finite literal bank leaves universal secrecy and untested
  encoding/projection leakage unresolved.
- Provider acknowledgment alone supplies insufficient evidence of enforcement.
- An empirical reference still needs independent episode, custody, instrument
  and comparison-frame qualification.

Run the independent synthetic route-continuity and archive-projection proving
cases, together with malformed/sparse/authority-widening controls:

```bash
node --test tests/dollhouse-witness-plan.test.mjs
```

This is a plan audit with per-claim findings, no universal score, hidden-state
detector, automatic observation, release permission or majority-vote closure.
