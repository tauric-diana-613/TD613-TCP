## What Aperture does

Aperture asks what a declared observation surface can actually see, what it cannot distinguish, whether reconstruction is identifiable or stable, and whether another observation is warranted.

Its current experiment-design posture remains:

```text
PROPOSE
ABSTAIN
REJECT
ASK_NOTHING
```

## Relation to Pedagogue

```text
Pedagogue proposes or reframes
→ Aperture audits whether the question addresses the admitted deficit
```

Neither doll crowns the other.

```text
OBSERVABILITY != IDENTIFIABILITY
PROPOSAL != EXECUTION
WIDENING != VALIDATION
DOLLHOUSE_PLACEMENT != SHARED_AUTHORITY
```

## Authority ceiling

Aperture may recommend, reconstruct, compare, abstain, reject, preserve residue, and replay within its declared jurisdiction. It cannot take Ash custody, execute a sensor or physical experiment, authorize release/export, mutate a consequential route, promote a research refinement, deploy, or infer external-world truth from numerical closure.

The bridge carries receipts, not power.
