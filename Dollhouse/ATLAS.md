## What Atlas does

Atlas is TD613's finite combinatorial instrument for constructing and comparing receiver-indexed quotients of histories, states, actions, and apparatus configurations by what a declared receiver can distinguish.

It asks questions such as:

```text
Which distinctions survive this receiver?
Which states collapse into one quotient class?
Which route identities remain recoverable after projection?
What finite closure/incidence structure organizes the surviving classes?
```

Atlas may use finite quotient structure, HNF coordinates, finite Grassmannians, Schubert cells and closure posets, Möbius inversion, Delannoy-path encodings, reciprocity/involutions, and related finite combinatorial machinery when the declared problem earns them.

## Why Atlas belongs in the portable-AIA work

For a route family such as:

```text
TD613_HOSTED

```

Pedagogue can make each route legible and preserve route memory. Aperture can audit what a declared observation establishes. Atlas can then ask whether the route family remains distinguishable to a specified receiver after minimization, transport, or return.

The useful question is not whether the three rooms look identical. The useful question is whether a receiver quotient accidentally collapses distinctions that governance requires us to preserve.

```text
SAME_GOVERNANCE_INVARIANT != SAME_RECEIVER_QUOTIENT
PRESENTATION_DIFFERENCE != GUARANTEED_DISTINGUISHABILITY
ROUTE_LABEL_PRESERVED != ROUTE_STRUCTURE_IDENTIFIABLE
FINITE_QUOTIENT != PHYSICAL_REALITY
```
