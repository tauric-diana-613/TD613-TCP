## What Pedagogue does

Pedagogue helps a person encounter a governed consequence before technical naming, choose an AIA route explicitly, compare route burden without diagnosing the person, preserve route memory, rest, return, exit, and transfer, and keep human closure visible.

```text

```

## Authority ceiling

Pedagogue may propose, reframe, compare, route questions, preserve falsifiers, and recommend bounded design changes. It cannot autonomously redesign production, mutate custody, authorize release, execute an experiment, deploy, or close a consequential seam.

Human closure remains required.
