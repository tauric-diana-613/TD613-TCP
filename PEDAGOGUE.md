## Reach rule

Reach for Pedagogue when work materially changes:

- consequence order, terminology, rest, or exit;
- AIA route structure or child/custodian/auditor/technical projections;
- route burden, dependencies, projection crossings, gluing obstruction, or route memory;
- custody, release, refusal, or human-closure sequencing;
- a high-consequence mutation path or Cistern Law boundary;
- a shared pattern that may deserve promotion from one context into reusable infrastructure;
- a consequential workspace that needs a harmless practice case;
- a research claim that needs a falsifier, negative control, model-scope boundary, or explicit forbidden inference.

If the question is specifically about **observability, identifiability, reconstruction, conditioning, uncertainty geometry, widening, abstention, rejection, or replay**, also reach for [`APERTURE.md`](APERTURE.md).

Default joint route:

```text
Pedagogue proposes or reframes
→ Aperture audits the admitted observation / reconstruction geometry
→ Dome-World hosts any warranted research assay
→ human closure remains required
```

Neither instrument automatically executes the other's recommendation.

## Canonical Practice Fixture

A Canonical Practice Fixture is manifestly fictional and authority-closed. It declares the expected route through a real workspace without fabricating evidence or creating a second fake product route.

Loading one must not by itself retrieve records, create receipts, write custody, mutate a domain system, or grant authority. Explicit later gestures may exercise only what the fixture separately declares safe.

Example:

```bash
npm run pedagogue:design -- tests/fixtures/pedagogue/giving-bikini-bottom-practice.json
```
