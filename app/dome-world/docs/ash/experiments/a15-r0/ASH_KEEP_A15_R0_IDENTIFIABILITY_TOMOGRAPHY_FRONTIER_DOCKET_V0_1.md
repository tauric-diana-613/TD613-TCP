## 3. Identifiability and stability

### 3.1 Identifiability

For a declared forward family `F`, require:

```text
F(S1) = F(S2)  =>  S1 ~ S2
```

where `~` contains only explicitly permitted equivalences such as gauge choice, irrelevant coordinate relabeling, or another declared symmetry.

If materially different latent states repeatedly produce indistinguishable receipts, the instrument contains an identifiability hole.

### 3.2 Stability

Exact uniqueness is insufficient when noise makes materially different states nearly indistinguishable.

A candidate forward map must also preserve usable separation between materially different states.

If a differentiable local model becomes justified, inspect the informative Jacobian after removing admitted equivalence directions.

Candidate diagnostics include:

```text
rank(J_F)
sigma_min(J_F)
condition_number(J_F)
posterior / feasible-set ambiguity volume
held-out reconstruction error
```

No Jacobian, singular-value, or conditioning language is admitted until the declared forward operator earns the assumptions required to compute it.

## 6. New hypothesis cards

Every hypothesis below remains noncanonical and phase-free. None unlocks another.

### H_TRIPLE_IDENTIFIABILITY_SYNERGY

**Question:** Does the intact `D3 + Q + M` architecture make materially different latent states more uniquely and stably distinguishable than matched constituent and pair-wise controls?

**Required comparison:**

```text
D
Q
M
D+Q
D+M
Q+M
D+Q+M
relationship-shuffled(D+Q+M)
```

under matched observation, noise, and reconstruction budgets.

**Falsifier:** The intact triple does not outperform the best matched pair, or any apparent gain survives destruction of cross-regime relationships.

**Important correction:**

```text
I(S; D,Q,M) > I(S; D)
```

alone is insufficient evidence of emergence because joint observations ordinarily cannot contain less mutual information than a subset.

Candidate evidence must instead isolate information or identifiability gain attributable to intact cross-regime relations. Partial-information-decomposition-style synergy is one candidate analysis, not a constitutional estimator.

**Status:** OPEN_UNMEASURED  
**Claim ceiling:** SYNTHETIC_MATCHED_CONTROL_IDENTIFIABILITY_ONLY

### H_QUASIPERIODIC_REFERENCE_REDUCES_ALIASING

**Question:** Under matched observation budgets, does the structured-nonclosure reference reduce ambiguous reconstruction relative to a commensurate periodic reference?

Candidate conditions:


Candidate diagnostics:

```text

sigma_min_P vs sigma_min_Q (only if justified)
```

**Falsifier:** The quasiperiodic condition produces no reproducible improvement under matched controls, or observed improvement is explained by unequal coverage, feature density, amplitude, noise, or computational budget.

**Status:** OPEN_UNMEASURED  
**Claim ceiling:** SYNTHETIC_ANTI_ALIASING_HYPOTHESIS_ONLY

### H_PROBE_DIVERSITY_OUTPERFORMS_REPEAT_VOLUME
