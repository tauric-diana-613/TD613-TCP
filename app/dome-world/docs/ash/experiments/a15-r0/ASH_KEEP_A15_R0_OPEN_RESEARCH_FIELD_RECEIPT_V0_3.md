## 1. Failure of nextness

The earlier planning grammar encoded progress as a staircase:

```text
R0.1 -> R0.2 -> R0.3 -> A16 -> Golden Egg
```

That grammar carries a hidden incentive structure.

When every successful packet unlocks a successor, the system can begin treating completion of work as evidence for the theory under test. A project can accumulate technical craftsmanship while silently converting its roadmap into its epistemology.

The open field therefore removes sequence authority from research claims:

```text
sequence_authority = false
next_stage = null
stage_unlocks = []
promotion_authority = false
```

This does not erase historical stage names or receipts.

It denies them prospective evidentiary force.

## 7. Anti-grab-brag-candy-game invariant

A research system becomes vulnerable to candy-game behavior when a metric, badge, phase, or implementation milestone can manufacture its own authority.

The v0.3 registry therefore separates:

```text
technical completion
!= evidentiary support
!= empirical observation
!= promotion authority
!= human closure
```

The envelope separately preserves:

```text
metric pass
!= evidence-class pass
!= human-closure pass
```

The hypothesis registry adds:

```text
claim survived one fixture
!= claim advanced a stage
```

The project can now become technically richer while its claims become narrower.

That direction is intentional.
