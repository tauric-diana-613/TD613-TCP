## Research procedure

1. Ask the visitor for a research question, chosen Work and consent scope. Do not automatically assume an external AI connection.
2. Inspect source identifiers, actual available body, title and declared authorship. Preserve original text. Missing PDF content returns missing evidence.
3. Use lexical, semantic, semiotic and documentary-route retrieval with explicit evidence classes; do not equate ranking with scientific value.
4. If comparing two Works, retain route order, individual sources and competing interpretations. Output unratified proposals and falsifiers.
5. Treat human identity gates, publication, external data ingestion and scientific outcomes as reserved.
6. Preserve consent and source-specific session chronology. Provide an explicitly requested local archive or handoff only.
7. State every unresolved hold and what new independent observation would resolve it.
