## What the Podium is

The Podium is a **machine-legible research instrument** for a human-directed AI Conductor. It is deliberately unlike a human UI: no buttons to click, no layout crown, no consumer telemetry, and no independent daemon/agent. Its surface comprises an explicit route index, typed ESM dispatcher, offline Node task stream, source-bound receipts and separate native engine adapter slots.

- **Conductor:** the AI's procedural role for choosing a permitted research route, operating a connected read-only tool, testing a hypothesis, and returning a typed receipt.
- **Composer:** the AI's creative/reparative role for proposing new counterworlds, bridges, scholarly compositions, visual architecture or assay designs. Proposals are unratified until independently reviewed.
- **Podium:** the bounded machine instrument both roles may use, not a sovereign agent or authorization mechanism.
- **Operahouse:** shared hosting, tools, exact-reference transport, provenance and governance gates.
- **Opera:** Vixen Cybernetics' self-contained research program, questions and performances.
- **Performer:** original Work or manifestation within Em's source estate. A Universal record, Atlas source or Rulefield may be examined separately without silently becoming a Vixen Performer.

**OPERAHOUSE ≠ OPERA ≠ PERFORMER** is a typed non-transfer law, not a ban on context-indexed relations. Rulefield's role multiplicity applies only after separate role/estate/permission evidence; a source can be examined in multiple roles without one role granting authority to another.
