## Source-derived interface genre (not a hard preservation constraint)

- **Invitation instead of administration:** the newcomer meets Em's research before they meet internal controls, audit nomenclature or architectural filing.
- **Four operable doorways:** Meet the Research → original Works; Touch the Instrument → computational Observatory; Attempt a Relation → existing source-first Synthesis Salon; Follow a Citation → Vixen Folios.
- **A small working carry-forward cart:** reuses `VIXEN_STUDY_TRAY` and its existing count/event contract; does not automatically choose partners, infer causation, or ratify any candidate theory.
- **Typographic intelligence:** high-fashion Didone display, intentional negative space, nuanced Glinda pink, liquid chrome and Arc Flash color; no sterile administrative dashboard or mandatory five-color prison.
- **Under-the-hood rigor:** concealed advanced inspector, original 32 Work routes, 53 linked manifestations, eight unadjudicated human gates, 21 methods, extant Vixen folios and research engine remain independently testable.
- **Mathematical laboratory becomes a scholarly encounter:** each source-born Observatory folio offers *Follow the Source*, *Examine the Method* when exact instrument references exist, and *Open Vixen Folios*.
