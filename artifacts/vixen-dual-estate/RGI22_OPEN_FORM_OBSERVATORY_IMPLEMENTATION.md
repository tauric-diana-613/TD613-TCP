## Four optical obligations (Aperture v3.2-alpha inheritance)

1. **Layered D₃/φ moiré interference** — distinct periodic/quasiperiodic fields; measured folded seam/filament differences without treating the layers as one ontology.
2. **Separately measured beat geometry** — finite wave-vector differences, beat wavelengths, velocities and phases. These geometrical quantities remain invariant under frame time; the sampled constructive/destructive envelope evolves.
3. **Bounded bloom and electrical emission** — capped peaks, emission and radii with dark-trough pixel fidelity explicitly HELD pending visual inspection.
4. **Viewport-aware rendering and temporal behavior** — DPR/pixel budgets, Nyquist guard, reduced-motion support and the existing Foundry-owned frame clock. No child RAF is created for Composer previews.

The uploaded Aperture v3.2-alpha source remains a separate instrument. No Aperture firmware is copied, executed, renamed, or promoted by this commission.
