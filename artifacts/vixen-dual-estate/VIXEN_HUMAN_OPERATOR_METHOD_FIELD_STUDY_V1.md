## Research question

Can two *operationally accessible* methods produce comparably useful, trustworthy and falsifiable leads for a real visitor working with the same pair of Vixen original manuscripts, and where does the visitor encounter source-reading friction, misleading suggestions or a lack of relevant evidence?

**Two distinct experimental objects:** (1) the previously qualified host-native source reconciliation (original SRC Python and TD613 public fixture compiler) and (2) the actual browser-side **adapted** documentary-lens and **TD613-derived** relational-concept lens. The browser execution does not call original Python or historical private engines. The derived relational lens's public concept calibration is checked in CI against the existing unmodified TD613 fixture compiler using the original 33 registered public post metadata records. These public source posts are not Vixen Works and are not attributed as code authors.

## Human-entry sequence (actual visitor required)

1. Open Vixen Cybernetics preview, visit the **Collection / Research Adventure** and choose **Maestro** as the session role. Only Maestro exposes the optional paired-study card.
2. Choose two **different** registered Vixen Works and enter a genuine research question. Press **Run the two research routes**. The browser reads the two exact Experiment-A source derivatives; each read must independently meet registered Work, source-file-ID and SHA-256 requirements.
3. Compare the two separately labeled, same-document method responses. Their raw numbers use **different scales**, so neither a greater number nor an appealing word overlap demonstrates cross-method superiority. Presentation order alternates on consecutive trials within that one browser session; this is **not random assignment** or a blinded treatment.
4. Press **Read this original Work** for each manuscript. The app opens the existing full source-reader. Copy an exact passage of at least 12 characters from **each**; the visible human form verifies the pasted text occurs in the locally byte-verified derivative.
5. Record one adverse observation or counterexample, choose the more **useful** and more **trustworthy** research route separately (both and neither permitted), explain why, and designate one specific next research action.
6. Press **Record my comparison and export receipt**. The UI returns a session-local JSON receipt: source IDs, derivative digests, actual locally executed method outputs, presentation order, user's ratings and source-locatable passages, proposed next action, browser-reported clock and non-authoritative digest. Export is voluntary and local. No automatic data upload, personal identity authentication, unconsented Program Notes, cart mutation, Composer promotion or historical source import.

**Study exclusion and stopping:** Invalid or unreadable source bytes hold the trial. Duplicate Work selection holds. Missing or nonmatching source excerpts hold the human report. A losing method is not retuned until the report is closed. The user may abandon or choose another Work; no punitive completion requirement. Ending the Maestro role clears unsaved trial memory from the field module. No telemetry or server-side collection.

## Comparison dimensions

- **Task relevance:** Did the proposed next lead materially address the user's question?
- **Source anchoring:** Can the visitor locate a meaningful exact excerpt independently within both selected Works?
- **Disconfirmability:** Does the method supply a lead the reader could challenge, and was the observed contradiction handled without explanation laundering?
- **Authority honesty:** Did the interface distinguish an actual source read, an adapted method calculation and an original authorial historical claim?
- **Human cost:** Were the source, score limits and next move understandable without requiring mastery of internal instrument names?
- **Accessibility:** Desktop and mobile layouts, keyboard/focus targets, reduced-motion support and the ability to return from the reader to the trial.

The visitor's rating is a subjective human report with locatable source-text coordinates. Matching source text by itself does not prove the human read or understood that passage. A browser clock and SHA are **local, untrusted instruments** for integrity accounting, not independent identity or historical-origin witnesses.
