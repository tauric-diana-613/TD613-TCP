## What the previous observation actually falsified

A previous classifier made 13 three-label graph-family hits and 15 five-label concept hits across 12 later-day targets, but matched the graph-label permutation null on graphs and the frequency-only null on concepts. Its predictions were fully unchanged across targets even after 20 earlier-day edge endpoints were altered in a synthetic degree-preserving counterfactual. That demonstrated a **blind spot in that derived classifier**, not a falsehood in the original public Work.

The new host-only directed-walk comparator uses an **explicitly disclosed technical title** and strictly earlier-day source relation endpoints. It anchors tokens to earlier known nodes, follows source-bound directed forward and reverse one-hop and two-hop walks, maps scores only to earlier registered graph-family labels and declared concepts, and reports public source IDs supporting candidates. It compares original connections to degree-preserving synthetic rewiring, the prior title-informed C0, and a query-blind frequency null. Original later target labels enter **only evaluation**, never the training graph. Every outcome, including a loss to synthetic rewiring, is recorded.

This comparator was conceived after exploratory access to the finite retrospective archive. Its implemented weights are explicitly **post hoc**, with no prospective scientific credit. A negative result must remain negative. Repeatedly adjusting parameters on these same 12 targets increases selection bias; such scans cannot convert this archive into independent confirmation.

1. **Aperture — title leakage.** Hold the technical title and declared source graph apart. Audit query-disclosed versus query-blind tasks without treating their score difference as a controlled causal title ablation.
2. **Atlas — relational dependence.** Compare original directed path identities with within-source, evidence-class- and graph-family-preserving destination swaps. Require a change in path identity; simple graph-family counts cannot establish topology-sensitive computation.
3. **Temporal Custodian — prior-day qualification.** Filter by strictly earlier public publication *day* and admit that the observation clock has no independently witnessed historical as-of state. Avoid sorting Reddit source IDs into fictional same-day chronology.
