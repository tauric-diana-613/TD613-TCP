## 2 · The class-balance impossibility

**All 12 later-day targets have one or more labels outside earlier declared vocabulary**. We therefore have **12 positives and zero known-only negatives** for the binary task “does this post introduce at least one concept not in the earlier vocabulary?” On this finite dataset, a constant “novel” indicator scores all positives correctly and tells us nothing about discrimination. Binary novelty specificity, false-positive rate, calibrated posterior probability, and AUROC are not identifiable. An impressive accuracy statistic would conceal this.

A second, explicitly *title-disclosed lexical channel* produces only contiguous one-to-three-token lowercase phrases in the public technical header, excluding earlier registered concept names. Its five-candidate surface budget can recover labels textually disclosed by the title. A circular rotation of other later-day titles is a **synthetic lexical sensitivity control**, not an independent source observation. Whatever this route scores, it is title string matching—not previously undisclosed semantic discovery or original author capability.

## 3 · Falsification and independent acquisition requirements

The target cannot be upgraded by repeated tuning on these same 12 examined outcomes. A genuine prospective two-class study needs an independently timestamped, bounded new source sample acquired **after** frozen methods and sampling rules. Labeling has to be blinded, with explicit source URLs/rights/custody, and the predeclared endpoint vocabulary must be frozen before each target is opened.

The distinct, preregistered tasks should be:

1. **Known-neighborhood retrieval**: within the earlier source concept vocabulary and registered endpoints, compare degree-preserving topology rewires, title baselines, and frequency priors, with fixed top-k budgets, explicit abstentions, and documented sensitivity.
2. **Novelty discrimination**: on independently obtained *both known-only and genuinely novel* source outcomes, evaluate false positives, sensitivity, specificity, ROC or an appropriately declared rare-event metric, and uncertainty. Never manufacture “known-only” observations by swapping source labels and call them empirical negatives.
3. **Novel-label proposal**: keep literal title matching separate from genuinely nonliteral semantic candidate creation. Report exact string overlap with the title and annotation disagreements, not just a single “new concept” accuracy score.

**Sampling caveat:** Artificially enriching the test set with a chosen number of negative/positive cases can help test discrimination but alters the real-world class prevalence. Such a case-control sample cannot automatically calibrate production probability; predeclare its sampling weights and collect a representative independent prevalence sample if population-calibrated rates are claimed.

**Operator/custodian tasks to authorize separately:** Define source population and acceptable rights; designate an independent timestamp/custody witness; set sample size, training/target cutoff, stopping and exclusion rules; freeze algorithms and primary metrics by immutable hash *before* new target labels are acquired; arrange adjudicators who do not see model predictions until annotations close. An external observation must carry new evidence about origin, not just a signed or hashed transcription of the registered archive.
