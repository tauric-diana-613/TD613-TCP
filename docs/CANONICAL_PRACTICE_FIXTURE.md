# Canonical Practice Fixture / Calibration Phantom

A Canonical Practice Fixture is an opt-in Pedagogue/AIA calibration instrument for a consequential workspace. It carries manifestly fictional content through the workspace's real operator-facing route without fabricating evidence, widening authority, or creating a parallel demo architecture.

Human-facing products may call this a **Practice case**, **Practice inhabitant**, **Sample case**, or another plain-language label. The internal research term **calibration phantom** describes the known-ground-truth role of the fixture; it is not a claim that ordinary application code performs literal physical tomography.

## Why this exists

Complicated workspaces are difficult to diagnose when the content under examination is itself consequential or when the true route history is uncertain. A known fictional payload gives Pedagogue, CI, operators, and later recovery research a ground-truth control:

```text
known fictional input
→ real workspace route
→ real state transitions / receipts where safely admitted
→ observed route
→ expected-vs-observed comparison
```

The fixture is useful only when the real architecture remains in the loop. A separate fake demo world that bypasses production route/state logic is not a Canonical Practice Fixture.

## Governing laws

### 1. Container before content

A working container may exist before evidentiary records arrive. The fixture may populate fictional labels, names, questions, or other harmless operator inputs; it must not create evidence merely to make the screen look complete.

### 2. Route before jargon

The person using the practice fixture should encounter the actual consequence route in child-legible terms before internal custody, AIA, Cistern, tomography, or other implementation vocabulary is required.

### 3. Fiction may exercise reality; fiction may not become evidence

A practice fixture may traverse real controls, validators, route memory, receipts, reversible local state, rest, return, and exit when the product explicitly admits those effects. It may not silently become a source record, legal/compliance finding, donor record, user diagnosis, production fact, or other evidentiary claim.

### 4. Practice authority never silently upgrades

The shared fixture contract closes these authorities:

- evidence authority;
- consequence authority;
- external-write authority;
- production-mutation authority;
- automatic retrieval;
- automatic release;
- authority crossing.

Human closure remains required. A product may separately declare a bounded number of reversible local writes for practice-state persistence. That local permission does not confer external or evidentiary authority.

### 5. Same endpoint does not erase the practice route

A known endpoint reached through a different route remains a failed route calibration even when the screen looks identical. The shared assay consumes Pedagogue route-memory comparison and reports the route reconstruction error as a bounded discrete surrogate.

## Claim ceiling

The v0.1 assay may claim:

- manifestly fictional known ground truth;
- expected-vs-observed route comparison;
- exact-route or divergent-route posture;
- bounded discrete route reconstruction error;
- whether declared negative guarantees were preserved.

It may **not** claim:

- differential-geometric tomography;
- an affine connection or parallel-transport law;
- geometric curvature;
- geometric holonomy;
- a user-level score or diagnosis;
- deployment, release, or redesign authority.

The implementation therefore emits `KNOWN_GROUND_TRUTH_ROUTE_RECONSTRUCTION_SURROGATE` and keeps the geometric/tomographic claim flags false.

## Admission criteria

Do not add a practice fixture merely because a surface is complicated. A workspace is a good candidate when:

1. fictional content can enter the same real operator route as consequential content;
2. the fixture can remain unmistakably fictional;
3. evidence fabrication is unnecessary;
4. external or production consequences can remain closed unless an explicit human gesture separately authorizes them;
5. expected route steps can be declared before the witness runs;
6. the practice route does not reveal protected architecture to an unauthorized outside observer;
7. the product fixture stays outside shared core.

If those conditions fail, omit the fixture rather than building a misleading demo.

## Giving proving case

`tests/fixtures/pedagogue/giving-bikini-bottom-practice.json` is the first product proving fixture. Its public UI label remains `Load fictional sample`.

The fixture populates fictional contributor-research inputs and leaves the real Search, Save, Vault, and external-write controls untouched. Activation itself must preserve these negative guarantees:

```text
contribution records created = 0
retrievals started = 0
external mutations committed = 0
Vault writes committed = 0
authority upgrades = 0
```

The operator may subsequently choose to exercise a real route. That later gesture is governed by the product's ordinary authority and custody laws; the fixture does not grant it.

## Ash / Loom recovery relationship

The capability is available **about** Ash before it becomes a dependency **inside** Ash.

A future recovery assay may construct known fictional Ash practice inhabitants whose expected route histories are declared before observation. Pedagogue route memory can then compare expected and reconstructed histories. This can establish whether the recovery instrument retains route residue under controlled ground truth.

That is preparation for a future transport experiment, not permission to claim holonomy. Proto-Loom, transport law, connection, and holonomy remain separate research thresholds.
