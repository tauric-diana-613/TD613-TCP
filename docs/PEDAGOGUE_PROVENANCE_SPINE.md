# Flow-Core Pedagogue · Provenance Spine and Institutional-Time Extension

Status: **AUTHORED / PRE-IMPLEMENTATION / HUMAN-GATED**

This extension restores a distinction that is easy to lose once Pedagogue becomes mathematically and agentically sophisticated:

```text
intellectual ancestry != current mechanism evidence != implementation authority
```

Pedagogue descends from **Potato's evolving ASP → Flow-Core → Dome-World synthesis**. The source thinkers and knowledge traditions are inputs to that synthesis; they are not a flat council whose agreement creates truth.

## 1. Two stomachs, not one bibliography

Pedagogue keeps two non-equivalent provenance systems.

### 1.1 Lineage Spine

The Lineage Spine records how a concept entered Potato's work and how TD613 later operationalized it.

```text
source / thinker / routed knowledge
→ source concept
→ Potato synthesis stage
→ derived Dome-World / Flow-Core principle
→ mathematical abstraction
→ Pedagogue mechanism
→ current implementation surface
→ tensions / quarantine
→ authority ceiling
```

It may contain books, advocacy correspondence, strategic-planning documents, living knowledge routed with explicit provenance, metaphysical sources, architectural precedent, and later scholarly criticism.

### 1.2 Research Hydration

The existing research-transfer system remains the place for external literature structured as bounded assay-generating evidence.

```text
lineage provenance != research-transfer card
```

A classic source does not become a modern evidence card merely because Pedagogue still finds it useful. A modern paper may test or complicate a lineage-derived mechanism without rewriting ancestry.

## 4. Core provenance lenses

A lineage lens asks an operational question. It does not impersonate the thinker and it does not vote.

Initial lens family:

- `PREPARED_ENVIRONMENT` — can the environment make the appropriate action legible without additional command?
- `THIRD_TEACHER_DOCUMENTATION` — what is the environment teaching through its structure, and is process witnessed rather than reduced to a score?
- `EXPERIENCE_CONTINUITY` — does the route connect lived consequence to later transfer rather than fragment experience?
- `RHYTHM_AND_CADENCE` — is a valid process being temporally coerced, prematurely named, or interrupted before its consequence becomes available?
- `ANTI_PATHOLOGIZATION` — is failure being located inside a person when the observed response may be structurally intelligible?
- `INTERPRETIVE_LABOR` — who must explain, translate, wait, appeal, or comply for the system to function?
- `RELATIONAL_SAFETY` — is safety produced through legible relation and mutual accountability, or merely through opaque containment?
- `COMMUNITY_EMBEDDEDNESS` — is knowledge embedded in a navigable shared world or segregated into a managed specialist zone?
- `TEMPORAL_SOVEREIGNTY` — whose clock controls the transition, and can rest/exit remain structurally available?
- `LEGIBILITY_WITHOUT_EXPERT_MONOPOLY` — can a participant inspect enough of the mechanism to participate without surrendering interpretive sovereignty?

Lens outputs must preserve:

```text
CONVERGENCE
DIVERGENCE
NORMATIVE_ASSUMPTION
EMPIRICAL_CLAIM
DERIVATIONAL_CLAIM
UNVERIFIED_SOURCE_CLAIM
```

No lens count or convergence count creates truth.

## 7. Institutional Time Audit

Pedagogue gains a generic systems-facing Temporal Audit.

Declared clocks may include:

```text
t_sense   — condition experienced / sensed
t_model   — institutional model becomes legible
t_op      — operator action begins
t_inst    — institutional registration catches up
t_pub     — public visibility
t_context — causal context becomes institutionally admissible (optional)
```

The audit may compute typed lags such as:

```text
preemption_gap = t_inst - t_op
public_visibility_lag = t_pub - t_inst
context_preemption_gap = t_context - t_op
```

A positive `preemption_gap` means the system acted before its own institutional registration caught up.

The audit describes the **temporal architecture**, never a person's psychology, compliance, attention, or developmental state.

## 8. Dromological sequence audit

Pedagogue's phase order is treated as a partial-order contract:

```text
NOTICE < ACT < WORLD_ANSWERS < NAME
```

REST and TRANSFER retain their own structural requirements.

The audit detects, when explicitly declared:

- `PREMATURE_NAMING` — NAME precedes WORLD_ANSWERS;
- `ACTION_BEFORE_NOTICE` — ACT precedes NOTICE;
- `ASSESSMENT_BEFORE_CONSEQUENCE` — an assessment/closure event arrives before WORLD_ANSWERS;
- `REST_WITHHELD` — no structurally available rest/exit where the declared route requires it;
- `CADENCE_COMPRESSION` — a declared minimum dwell/process interval is violated;
- `CONTEXT_REGISTRATION_LAG` — an institutional token acts before its causal context becomes admissible.

Minimum dwell requirements, when used, are fixture-declared. They may not be inferred from age, diagnosis, identity, developmental rank, or hidden learner profile.
