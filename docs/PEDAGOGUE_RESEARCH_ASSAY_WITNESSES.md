# Pedagogue Research Assay Witnesses

Status: **GENERIC / RESEARCH-ONLY / NON-AUTHORITATIVE**

Research hydration gave Pedagogue a way to structure external scientific observations without importing source ontology or source authority. The next problem appears only after Pedagogue begins running its own bounded calibration assays:

```text
external transferable relation
→ internal assay
→ what may Pedagogue remember?
```

The answer is an **assay witness**, not a learned law.

## Witness outcomes

A `td613.flowcore.pedagogue-research-assay-witness/v0.1` may record exactly one bounded posture:

```text
SUPPORTED_BOUNDED
COUNTEREXAMPLED_BOUNDED
INCONCLUSIVE
```

The witness binds:

- the generic mechanism being tested;
- assay identity and schema;
- declared controls;
- bounded observations;
- what happened to the falsifier;
- alternative explanations that remain;
- the claim ceiling.

Every witness has zero promotion, redesign, product, production, and transmission authority.

## Mechanism review

`reviewPedagogueResearchMechanism(hydration, witnesses, mechanismId)` joins the external hydration state to internal bounded assays without collapsing disagreement.

Possible learning states include:

```text
CROSS_DOMAIN_PLUS_INTERNAL_BOUNDED_ASSAY_WITNESS
CROSS_DOMAIN_WITH_INTERNAL_BOUNDED_COUNTEREXAMPLE
CROSS_DOMAIN_WITH_INTERNAL_INCONCLUSIVE_ASSAY
CROSS_DOMAIN_WITH_MIXED_INTERNAL_ASSAYS
```

Single-domain variants are equally valid.

The critical rule is:

```text
support + counterexample
→ MIXED_INTERNAL_ASSAYS
→ DESIGN_DISCRIMINATING_ASSAY
```

Pedagogue does not average contradictory assays into confidence.
