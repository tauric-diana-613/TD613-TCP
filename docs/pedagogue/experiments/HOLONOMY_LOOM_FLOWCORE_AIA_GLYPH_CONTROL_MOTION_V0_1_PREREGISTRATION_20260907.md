## 4. Frozen gates

### G0 · Consequence before glyph

- No glyph rail is visible before a Loom result is visibly rendered.
- The result's ordinary-language status and consequence remain independently usable without the rail.
- Technical AIA/Flow-Core names are not required on the child route.

### G1 · Explicit AIA route selection

- A post-consequence control such as `SHOW THE PATH` is the explicit operator act selecting the product's `EXPERIENTIAL` AIA projection.
- The adapter may not infer a route from identity, age, behavior, viewport, content, status, or prior history.
- No other AIA route is silently selected.

### G2 · Canonical AIA binding

The adapter must consume `compileAiaSurfaceBinding` and `compileAiaSurfaceProjection` rather than declaring a look-alike AIA object.

The projection must:

- preserve the canonical required invariant family;
- contain no raw message text, raw match values, credentials, or private source bytes;
- keep `authority_may_cross:false`;
- keep automatic release/redesign false;
- keep human closure required;
- preserve Rest and Exit without penalty.

### G3 · Canonical glyph registry

The adapter must import the existing `FLOWCORE_GLYPH_REGISTRY` / `getFlowcoreGlyphSemantic` surface. Product code may choose a bounded subset and render it; it may not fork or rewrite glyph semantics, motion grammar, static equivalents, or authority ceiling.

### G4 · State-faithful motion

- `à` may visualize bounded local gathering only after CHECK has already produced the consequence.
- `米` may replay comparison against enabled deterministic Loom rules; divergence/finding state remains visible.
- `hõt` may animate only a bounded safer-copy emergence caused by the existing safer-copy action.
- `出` may never perform outward release motion while Loom release is blocked.
- `出` successful outward motion requires both deterministic release eligibility and successful browser clipboard completion. Clipboard denial/failure must produce a bounded non-release state.
- `cōl` represents continuity/return without erasing the inspected state.
- `𝄐` stops new pulses, allows existing motion to settle, preserves the final inspectable frame, and leaves Return/Exit available.

### G5 · Static and reduced-motion equivalence

With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`:

- no required semantic state may disappear;
- route order, status, blocked/available release, Rest, Return, and claim ceiling remain inspectable;
- no animation is required to discover a consequence;
- glyph state is represented by static geometry/text/status.

### G6 · Pedagogue / accessibility burden

- visible focus remains present;
- no color-only meaning;
- mobile viewport remains usable;
- forced-colors preserves control distinctions;
- the existing Pedagogue consequence-first / burden-non-worsening gate must remain GREEN.
