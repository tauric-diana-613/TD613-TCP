### FADT

FADT = **Finite Admissibility Descent Theorem**.

Its finite law concerns whether admissibility authority survives information loss. Exact descent holds only when lawful-support sets are constant on each erased-state fiber; equivalently, in the established finite formulation:

```text
U_y = I_y
Γ_y = ∅
```

If two erased states collapse to the same observed state while their admissible supports differ, exact authority cannot descend.

```text
q(x1)=q(x2) ∧ K_x1 ≠ K_x2
⇒ exact admissibility authority cannot descend
```

The theorem lineage must preserve the distinction:

```text

→ presentation consequence
```
