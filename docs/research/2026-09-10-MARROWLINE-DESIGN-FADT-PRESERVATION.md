## Concrete defects found before the rewrite

- Routine response anatomy, provider routing and held-stage text dominated the conversation. Small metadata text and persistent panels competed with the composer.
- The mobile High Zalgo decorator split on repeated newlines, trimmed lines, discarded blank lines, and appended spans without newline nodes. Combining characters survived, but the displayed source text did not.
- The ordinary relay renderer trimmed the provider stage before decoration. An exact mobile wrapper alone could not restore that lost whitespace.
- Mobile header compaction replaced the actual route with a hardcoded OPEN FIELD label; the full original label survived only in a hover title.

The requested full High Zalgo support means preserving combining-codepoint sequences, including noncanonical ordering, invisible join controls and supplementary characters. No declared five-glyph alphabet exists here. A font stack or layout treatment is a presentation aid, not a reversible text encoding and not a promise that every installed font covers every character.
