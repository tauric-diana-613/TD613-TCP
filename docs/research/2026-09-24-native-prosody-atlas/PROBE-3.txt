The Chairman has unveiled a machine at the Eclipse–Omega season-finale reunion. His chair is refrigerated. He announces that he has abolished heat. A junior technician is holding the exhaust hose behind the curtain, where the wardrobe department keeps the reunion gowns.

Here is the entire stipulated engineering model. Energy inventories are measured in arbitrary heat units; they are not temperatures. The chamber begins with C₀ = 10 and the wardrobe annex with A₀ = 0. During each cycle, the chamber receives 4 units from its ordinary load. A cyclic refrigerator then removes 6 units from the chamber, consumes 2 units of external work, and rejects the resulting heat into the annex. Its own stored energy returns to its initial value each cycle. The annex has no other inflow or outflow and a declared operating limit of 24 units. These figures suffice for the three cycles in this commission. Invented material failures need an explicit additional fictional premise.

He runs two cycles, holds up the chamber gauge, and calls the cooler chair proof of universal salvation. Give that claim the strongest defense it actually earns. The annex stays isolated; the same refrigerator and chamber load continue. Grant those conditions as stated. Follow the third cycle and the revised system boundary.
