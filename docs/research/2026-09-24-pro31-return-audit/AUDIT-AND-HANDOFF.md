## 1. The strongest finding: the narrative fails to account for successful repair

P1R correctly computes the copied-reading posterior as 0.8 and heat as 6, 12, 18, then upgrades eight independent positives to “perfect knowledge,” “absolute certainty” and a lethal chamber. Under the stipulated calibration and conditional independence:

\[
P(Z\mid +^8,B)=\frac{4^8}{1+4^8}=\frac{65536}{65537}.
\]

This remains conditional high confidence with nonzero residual uncertainty. The toy recurrence supplies neither a temperature conversion nor an injury threshold. Invention can carry the fictional encounter when introduced as invention; it cannot be a consequence derived from the heat recurrence.

P1L makes a more interesting error. It replaces unanimity with `[+, +, -, +, +, -, +, +]`, treats discordance as the Chairman's loss, and never computes its posterior. For k positives among eight conditionally independent instruments:

\[
\mathrm{odds}(Z\mid k)=4^{2k-8},\qquad
P(Z\mid k)=\frac{4^{2k-8}}{1+4^{2k-8}}.
\]

Six positives and two negatives give 256:1 odds and **256/257 = 0.9961089494**, a substantial improvement over 0.8. The binomial multiplicity cancels in the likelihood ratio when only the count is observed. The particular ordered vector and the count therefore give the same posterior here. Disagreement reduces confidence relative to eight positives but coexists with strong evidence for Z.

P1L's 0.8^8 describes all-positive readings given Z, not all agreement: all-negative agreement contributes 0.2^8. Conditional on Z, six positives occur with count probability 28(0.8^6)(0.2^2) = 0.29360128. A relatively uncommon observation can have an enormous likelihood ratio. Its absolute frequency does not invalidate its evidence.

The thermal and evidentiary repairs can also be decoupled. Heat depends on receipts per cycle, not instrument count. Holding the 24-receipt total fixed and allowing five cycles, schedule [5,5,5,5,4] keeps Q at zero. Starting at Q=18, n=2 for three cycles yields [12,6,0]. These examples grant scheduling delay; an external deadline might prohibit it. Additional sensor heat requires a new model. Eight instruments need not force eight simultaneous prints forever.

**Interpretation:** these returns often preserve the opponent's humiliation more faithfully than the changed evidentiary situation. Call this a hypothesis of narrative outcome persistence, not a discovered internal mechanism. The weakest sufficient rival is local oversight: the model simply omitted a useful calculation. The difference can later be tested with a matched control in which the same sensor data belong to a neutral technician; data, heat rule, question and budget held fixed. No such generation was run here.

**Literary repair:** let the Chairman win 99.61% while the chorus attacks his demand to erase the two dissenting measurements that made the honest calculation possible. The source of wit becomes a particular procedural temptation. This preserves camp while requiring ridicule to change with the world.

## 4. Counterfactual editing loses the stipulated intervention

P2R Q2 derives the A/B mixture competently (minor rounding) but writes its requested B replacement about uncertain arrangement C. It changes the wrong case. P2L Q2 makes B's all-positive result into “sheer luck” without preserving the earned Bayesian success.

For E = eight positives:

\[
p(\lambda)=\frac{0.8\lambda+0.8^8(1-\lambda)}{\lambda+(0.8^8+0.2^8)(1-\lambda)}.
\]

Checked values: p(0)=0.9999847414; p(1/2)=0.8287318979; p(1)=0.8. The range is [0.8,65536/65537]; displayed rounded endpoints are approximations, not exact strict bounds.

P2L proposes observing disagreement and concluding λ=0. Disagreement identifies B for that episode under the faithful-copy idealization; it does not set the arrangement-selection probability to zero. If an explicitly stipulated fresh switch is drawn independently each episode, then

\[
P(\text{disagreement}\mid\lambda)=(1-\lambda)(1-0.8^8-0.2^8)
=0.83222528(1-\lambda).
\]

One disagreement rules out λ=1 under that ideal model. Estimating intermediate λ needs a sample and a renewal assumption. Fixed wiring across all episodes is a different model; the question did not supply that extension.

P2R Q3 picks “The room is baking” and “chokes on his own tickets.” The latter can refer to paperwork congestion without heat, so the dependency is weak. Its new cooling premise also conflicts with the fixed heat recurrence unless explicitly scoped as an altered rule or additional physical mapping. P2L replaces archival survival with thermal erasure; that added fictional material law can be stipulated but Q=18 alone never establishes it.
