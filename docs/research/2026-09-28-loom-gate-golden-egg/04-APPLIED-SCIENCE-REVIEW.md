## Proposed scientific method

Freeze the protected target and permitted operation before designing an attacker. Declare the observer's prior information, capture surface, query budget, collusion and persistence horizon. A useful attacker supplies a lower bound on demonstrated recoverability; unsuccessful attacks supply no general upper bound.

Use known leaking positive controls, truly independent negative controls, and matched nuisance conditions. Randomize route order where justified. Split holdouts by source/template or other dependence unit, not merely rows. Report uncertainty, multiple-comparison handling, exclusions and stopping rules. Evaluate mitigation utility on the same admitted units used for disclosure claims.

Distinguish exact finite-channel information from estimated information and from task-specific attack accuracy. Entropy/MI require a distribution; accuracy requires a declared guessing baseline and loss. A confidence interval around a weak estimator does not cure a wrong estimand.

Measure the measurement process: collection-point coverage, lost events, clock alignment, observer feature availability, instrumentation effects, adjudicator disagreement and publication leakage. Missing channels yield missing claims, not zero leakage.

## Constructive result

A successful research result may be a falsified architecture, a HELD acquisition, or a mitigation with explicitly limited utility and observer coverage. Golden Egg requires its inherited surfaces and admissibility together. The present review earns a better experiment question, not the egg.
