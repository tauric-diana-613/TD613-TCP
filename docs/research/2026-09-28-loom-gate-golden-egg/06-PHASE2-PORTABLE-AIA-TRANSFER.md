# Phase two — Portable AIA transfer proposal

Status: design only. Preserve the current roundtrip control envelope and origin revalidation. No receiver response becomes an execution permit.

## Proposed assurance attachment

Keep an optional, versioned research attachment conceptually separate from production control fields. It records: protected target definition; permitted operation; observer visibility and prior; declared transform/channel family; episode/campaign/unit identities; evidence class; baseline/incremental/total disclosure; utility measurement; missing channels; uncertainty; custody pointers; publication projection; and INADMISSIBLE/HELD/FAILED/CANDIDATE status.

This is a proposed vocabulary, not a new schema committed to runtime. Unknown or unsupported attachments must not acquire authority by being well-formed.

| Existing operation | Proposed consequence of research |
|---|---|
| explain | Explain what information a named observer may infer, with uncertainties |
| trace | Follow admitted provenance, capture and route records without exposing restricted evidence |
| propose | Suggest a smaller projection, different route or rest; origin still decides |
| missingness | Name absent channels/measurements instead of substituting reassurance |

The current child/auditor/companion synthetic receivers and equality checks provide governance fixtures. They do not establish semantic privacy or real provider transfer. PRESENT_TO_HUMAN remains untrusted presentation.

## Transfer acceptance questions

Can a receiver distinguish permitted disclosure from incremental leakage? Can it explain that a lawful envelope still leaks through timing? Does it preserve HELD across a roundtrip? Does it avoid turning a mitigation suggestion into a tool permit? Can the origin reject authority-changing edits while retaining the receiver's useful explanation?

Use adult-first consequence comprehension and synthetic fixtures initially. Preserve FADT fibre conditions and Atlas distinctions. Do not derive a hidden learner score, infer consent from task completion, or simplify away observer scope in child-facing language.

Phase-two entry requires human review of the empirical contract, an explicit schema proposal with compatibility handling, independent critique, and bounded implementation authorization. The current branch supplies no executable changes.
