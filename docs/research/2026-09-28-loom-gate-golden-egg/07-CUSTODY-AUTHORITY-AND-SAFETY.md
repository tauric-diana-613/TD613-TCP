# Custody, authority and safety

## Authority stays attached to the operation

Loom origin revalidation, AIA Cistern egress authorization, and Golden Egg evidence adjudication answer different questions. A successful audit cannot grant a route permit. An endpoint's existence cannot authorize a consequential action. A research commitment cannot establish the truth of its contents.

Cistern's durable spent-intent record for non-idempotent consequences is not replaced by signed-cookie rotation. STOP can revoke future authority; it cannot retroactively remove information already delivered. Preserve explicit costs, consequence explanation, consent withdrawal and rest.

## Evidence custody

Bind raw evidence to immutable source identities and retain measured_at separately from recorded_at. Use a declared canonical encoding and digest, restricted access to raw material, append-only corrections and exact-prefix continuity. Record who controlled acquisition, labeling, storage and adjudication.

Unsigned historical hashes do not establish identity, authorship or trusted timestamp. A hash authenticates neither empirical origin nor complete capture. Safe Harbor retains its declared limitations.

Separate raw evidence, internal scientific records, and public explanation. Each release needs its own observer/secret analysis. Public counts, phase transitions, exception text, timing and nonce openings can reveal protected facts. Record retention/deletion policy before collecting personal information; no such collection occurred here.

## Explicit scope

This tranche adds research documents only. No runtime, production schema, UI, provider credentials, deployment, merge, child experiment, hostile live test, or automated/detached review is initiated. The historical trap route and fixed header markers are studied as code, not exercised as an exfiltration path.

Human closure remains necessary for empirical claims and future execution. It must name the operation being approved and its visible consequence; a ceremonial marker alone does not silently broaden scope.
