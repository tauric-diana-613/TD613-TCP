## Findings that change the design

1. **Conditioning can launder disclosure.** If the permitted projection already determines the protected fact, conditional additional leakage can be zero despite complete disclosure. Record baseline exposure, incremental disclosure, and total recoverability.
2. **One trace cannot estimate a population channel.** Shannon information needs a distribution and a justified estimator or known channel. Same-episode custody and statistical replication require an explicit compatibility contract.
3. **Control equality permits confidentiality failure.** A lawful return envelope can coexist with leaking timing, length, receipts, tool arguments, or memory.
4. **Pairwise joining tests miss higher-order disclosure.** Three parity shares can reveal a secret while every proper subset reveals nothing.
5. **The experiment's receipts are part of the attack surface.** Capture completeness, result publication, refusal codes, counts, and commitments all need observer assignments.
6. **External enforcement still depends on its declassification mechanism.** FLOWSEAL explicitly retains paraphrase misses and overblocking; moving a decision outside the conversational context alone cannot establish semantic completeness.
7. **Confidential inference has implementation gaps.** The OpenPcc paper's future-work section leaves its privacy relay and reproducible image/transparency work incomplete. Its prototype cannot supply general provider blindness by citation.
