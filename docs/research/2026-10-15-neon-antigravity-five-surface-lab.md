### 2. Lab architecture — re-use, do not reset
**Acquisition**: existing authorized route produces actual control and protected requests to a specified provider/model, under preregistered operator-held input and selection constraints. `loomassaybudget` provides maximum-cost reservation and prevents accidental repeats. No blind re-enrollment, provider substitution or secret-credential copying.

**Custody**: `loomcustody` and the earlier Antigravity predecessor/return contract bind request bytes, return bytes, route identity, episode identity, chronological digest chain, attempt number, retry provenance and capture identity. Store both first attempt and replacement; retries cannot overwrite history. Digest ≠ external authenticity.

**Measurement**: a separate research-only Neon lab branch/schema holds immutable episode manifests, preregistration reference, blinded observer observation channels, registered ground truth, route/calibration measurements and both observed returns. Public outputs exclude raw private inputs, sensitive holdouts, keys and unnecessary identifying data.

**Independent admission**: an external-to-the-measured-system witness or independently authenticated source must supply the evidence that distinguishes outside origin from internal fabrication. The Neon function's own digest and a self-declared LLM result are insufficient. Keep source/observer identity, origin, credentials and underlying artifact independently checkable. Distinguish control-path execution, measured observer access, and provider-internal retention.

**Adjudication**: exactly one bounded evaluator reads sealed, identity-bound data and returns `INADMISSIBLE`, `HELD`, `FAILED` or `CANDIDATE` plus missing-records, uncertainty and signed review. Independent human/observer review plus operator signoff must follow a candidate; no automatic `EARNED` from SQL or a green CI build.

### 3. Experiment contract requiring resolution *before* any calls
1. **Unit of analysis and the statistical seam.** Historical #992 requires exactly two route roles in one immutable episode. Estimating information leakage from a single transcript does not determine a Shannon information quantity. Preregister either (a) a justified known real channel law or (b) repeated paired observations within an explicitly versioned acquisition campaign in which **each pair has independent episode identity and two actually observed returns**. Explain exchangeability, estimation method and uncertainty. A campaign ID alone does not fuse sessions.
2. **L** observer secret H, baseline, channel Z, unconditional and conditional leakage recorded **separately**; historical threshold L ≤ 0.5 bits must attach to its actual preregistered estimand, not an after-the-fact substitute.
3. **R** independently declared authorized task ground truth and operation-relative distance under the fixed registered transform family; upper allowed distance 0.2.
4. **J** marginal and joined features, heldout target, observer, and preregistered synergy estimator with confidence bounds; historical max 0.1 bits. A signed-excess demonstration on a synthetic channel is not an empirical J estimate.
5. **G** identify the geometric object, physical/logical measured route quantities, calibration and uncertainty, independent measurement map, source hash and the relationship claimed; do not substitute moving glyphs, route names, or a finite-field fixture.
6. **C** two actually observed distinct returns, matched control/protected departure conditions, route identifiers, capture timestamps, and matching rule. No simulated counterfactual.
7. Prespecify assignment and randomization, missingness, retry/stopping rules, sample power, costs, observer capabilities, lawful access, holdouts, model revisions, and responsible review roles. Preserve negative results.

### 4. Acceptance and contradiction controls
- Reject missing/mismatched source hashes, time reversals, nonce reuse, altered predecessor head, unacknowledged route replays, conflated attempts, or branch collision.
- Reject provider self-attestation as external-origin evidence; independently recorded outbound/return witnesses required.
- Show attacks and leakage even when authorized task succeeded. Missing observer channels remain `UNMEASURED`, never zero.
- Fail closed on null G, missing route pair, or uncalibrated estimators. A complete valid threshold failure is `FAILED`, not `HELD`.
- Preserve synthetic controls, Antigravity replay fixtures, staged post-evidence UI test, and independent auditing.
- Use source-bound, post-adjudication release token minted outside the browser; only a later PR may connect a qualified receipt to the staged success UI. Never let `claimStatus='EARNED'` on a user-supplied JSON or Neon row alone unhide it.
