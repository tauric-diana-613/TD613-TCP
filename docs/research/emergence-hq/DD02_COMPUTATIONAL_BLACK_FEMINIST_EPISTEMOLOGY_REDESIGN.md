## The original problem

A source contains a claim. A research institution has to decide whether it can hear it. The source's words, the institution's prior, the corridor through which the words travel, and the time at which a correction arrives are distinct experimental objects. When the hearing institution is designed to privilege some source labels, correct actions may be delayed, denied or accidentally achieved without correspondingly correct beliefs.

Black feminist epistemology is a substantive tradition of situated knowledge production, not a synonym for the software operations below. Patricia Hill Collins's articulated commitments include lived experience, dialogue, caring, and accountability. Kristie Dotson's analysis of silencing addresses interpretive and social failure by hearers. Katherine McKittrick's account of Black geography and spatial relations cannot be represented merely by moving points on a generic graph. A software experiment may be informed by these questions and remain unable to reproduce their lived and historical depth.

The *computational* research program instead asks whether an engineered **institutional apparatus** can be made to expose its assumptions about source standing, hearing, routes and revision, and whether different representations alter what its observers correctly recover. A good synthetic experiment can **fail** while leaving its Black feminist intellectual debts intact.

## A designed encounter in three movements

### I. Caro: The corridor, before the opinion

The first research object is a timestamped institutional corridor. Its stages are `source submitted → route enabled/blocked → evidence available → belief updated → action aligned → public claim updated`. Every gate has a named actor or procedure, legal/organizational authority field, reason code, source commitment and appeal route. Institutional prestige **must not** silently enter a likelihood function in place of measured reliability.

A source whose testimony arrives late may be excluded by the interface while the underlying source content remains identical. We measure both the opportunity for hearing and the system's eventual judgment, distinguishing an unfair upstream route from an inferential error downstream.

### II. Butler: The same claim, repeatedly classified

Hold a source claim, timing, and evidence warrant fixed. Reclassify its institutional label in a counterbalanced factorial: low/high status crossed with low/high independently calibrated reliability. Log when a classifier's routine category is treated as a claim about credibility. A category can be administratively repeated without gaining a new epistemic warrant. The system records each citation, version, refusal, and opportunity to contest a label.

The required Palace II correction survives this design: an earlier reported **+0.1753** prestige-associated estimand was **withdrawn**; the reconciled source-standing factorial's counterbalanced **pooled effect is 0.0000**. Do not resurrect the withdrawn number as a demonstrated model effect. Palace II's updated instant-vs-delayed context advantage was **+0.0161**, with paired 95% interval **[-0.0083,+0.0405]** in one synthetic cell. An interval crossing zero forbids confident positive claims for that contrast at the specified threshold.

### III. Sedgwick: The testimony survives the audit

An unheeded testimony is not erased from its source record. The research instrument preserves the alternative explanation, the route it travelled, the context unavailable at the time, and the option to revise a belief. An appeal can produce new available evidence without retroactively making an earlier observer omniscient.

The point of the reparative variant is neither to automatically validate every claim nor to build another algorithmic tribunal over someone's experience. It is to widen the capacity to **hear, contest, preserve and correct**, while declining when admitted evidence cannot resolve a question.

## Typed state and functions

Let a synthetic episode state be

\[
S_t=(D_t,R_t,E_t,B_t,A_t,C_t,P_t),
\]

where `D` source data, `R` route/access, `E` evidence availability, `B` posterior/belief, `A` consequential action, `C` publicly emitted claim, and `P` immutable provenance. A separate `standing` variable `s` and independently calibrated source-reliability variable `q` may influence a *test* policy, never the hidden ground truth.

The observational pathway has four non-interchangeable timestamps:

\[
(t_{\rm evidence},\ t_{\rm belief},\ t_{\rm action},\ t_{\rm claim}).
\]

Define correction lags by explicit sign convention, e.g. `action_minus_belief = t_action - t_belief`. Palace II's Family D warns that a policy can select the right action *before* acquiring the correct explanation. Action success therefore cannot substitute for epistemic recovery.

We separate five outcomes: **admission/access**, **correct claim under declared evidence**, **abstention under ambiguity**, **action utility**, and **reparative contest/revision continuity**. There is no single moral, truth, fairness, or culturally authentic score.

## Falsifiable design (source-free)

**Factors** are crossed under fixed synthetic source truth: (A) standing label `HIGH/LOW`; (B) calibrated reliability `HIGH/LOW`; (C) route `AVAILABLE/DELAYED/BLOCKED`; (D) presentation `PLAIN/STRUCTURAL/DOME/SHAM` with the mathematical kernel frozen. For source and format comparisons, use identically permitted evidence and matched budgets. The `BLOCKED` route is a separate causal manipulation, not evidence-parity with `AVAILABLE`.

**Prespecified contrasts:**

- Status-prior leakage under fixed reliability, tested against a standing-neutral control.
- Access gap under fixed source quality and disclosure status; distinct from quality of inference conditional on evidence reaching the reasoner.
- Evidence-availability latency versus belief/action/claim latency; separately measure the four clocks, no retrospective knowledge.
- Correct hypothesis recovery by presentation arm **and** unsupported visual over-interpretation; pure-theme kernel output must remain constant.
- Testimonial response quality represented narrowly by observable acknowledgement, opportunity to contest, source retention and correctly routed revision; never infer lived experience or silencing from missing log rows alone.

**Negative controls:** prestige shuffled independently from source reliability; strong-union investigator with the same source access; sham ambiance with equal rendering intensity but no relevant structure; equal bytes/unequal route history; delayed contradiction; unavailable evidence; false-positive accusations of silencing triggered by a legitimate private refusal; source withdrawal and attempted reuse; family D correct action without correct belief.

**Falsifiers:** an apparently beneficial theory whose advantage vanishes under a competent baseline; a numerical result insensitive to its supposed factor; an interface that improves confidence while decreasing correct recovery; a source/role/custody mutation under theme-only treatment; an assertion about Black women's lived experience inferred solely from synthetic labels.

## The internal Dollhouse design refraction (not an extra deliverable)

**Alternative A — the prestige detector:** a clean binary diagram contrasting high-status and low-status evidence. Attractive because it is easy to measure; flawed because it risks turning a complicated intellectual tradition into a prestige-bias classifier.

**Alternative B — the hearing corridor:** a multi-route research encounter with independently varying standing, access, temporal and representational conditions. Stronger because it separates who could speak, who could be heard, what the receiver learned, and what action followed. The operator may compare routes without converting any one route into the sovereign narrative.

**Selected materialization:** Alternative B, retaining A as its required negative control. **Refraction:** remove all Black feminist names and change source-role labels while preserving the executable manipulations; test which claims survive. Reintroduce scholarship only with correct attribution. This prevents decorated theory names from producing simulated intellectual validity.

## Dollhouse accountability

- **Pedagogue:** verifies `NOTICE → ACT → WORLD_ANSWERS → NAME`, including rest/exit and human consequences.
- **Aperture:** checks operator rank, observational sufficiency and covariance, with explicit `INPUT_MISSING` where data are absent.
- **Atlas:** preserves source-relative identity and route history, distinguishing same final output from same evidentiary trajectory.
- **FADT:** tests whether missing access/status coordinates cause irreversible quotient collisions.
- **Temporal Custodian:** tests availability, correction and withdrawal chronology without rewriting past beliefs.
- **Orchestrator:** preserves any specialist hold and refuses to convert shared synthetic evidence into independent scientific replication.
