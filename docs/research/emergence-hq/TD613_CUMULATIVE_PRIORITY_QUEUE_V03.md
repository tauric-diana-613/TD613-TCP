## III. Distinct Black feminist commitments and executable hypotheses

### Black feminist epistemology — queue rank 47

The source tradition, including Patricia Hill Collins, foregrounds **lived experience, dialogue, ethics of caring and personal accountability**; Kristie Dotson studies testimonial silencing/epistemic oppression; Katherine McKittrick grounds questions of space, Black geographies and historical relations. These are substantive epistemic commitments and situated intellectual lineages, **not software proxy metrics**.

### Computational Black feminist epistemology — queue rank 4

The proposed computational research program translates *selected critiques of epistemic exclusion and power* into **falsifiable access, authority, standing and testimony-handling interventions**. Palace II gives unusually precise starting controls: standing prestige should vary independently from measured source reliability, and four clocks distinguish evidence access, belief revision, consequential action and public claim.

Palace II source criticism matters: the historical **+0.1753 prestige-effect claim was withdrawn**, the reconciled pooled counterbalanced effect was **0.0000**, and the instantaneous-versus-delayed sensor difference **+0.0161** had a confidence interval spanning zero in one synthetic grid cell. None is a universal proof about a social group. Separately, Family D showed correct action may precede belief revision.

**Dollhouse Design do-over:** one substantive theoretical instrument is authored separately, selecting Caro + Butler + Sedgwick (and optional dialectical counterpoint), never confusing a simulated accessibility score with Black feminist validation.
