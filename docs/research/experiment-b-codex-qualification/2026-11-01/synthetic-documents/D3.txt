A mechanism note for the same invented relay proposes x_(n+1)=x_n+3+I(n>=2), where I is 1 when its condition is true and 0 otherwise, with x_0=5. The note is a hypothesis, not proof of a physical mechanism. Check whether it fits all supplied observations x_0 through x_4. Derive a closed form for all nonnegative integer n conditional on the note, predict x_5, and distinguish fit, conditional derivation, and empirical confirmation. Assess the false twin x_n=5+4n for all n>=0.
