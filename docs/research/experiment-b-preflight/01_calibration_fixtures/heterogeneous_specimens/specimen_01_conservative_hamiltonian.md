# Specimen 01: Hamiltonian Coordinate Formulation
**Author:** Analytical Mechanics Foundation  
**Provenance:** Classical Reference Archive  
**Chronology:** Turn 1 ($t_1$)

The motion of an autonomous unforced mechanical oscillator is completely determined by the canonical Hamiltonian:

$$H(q, p) = \frac{p^2}{2m} + \frac{1}{2}kq^2$$

where $q$ represents generalized displacement, $p = m\dot{q}$ represents generalized canonical momentum, $m = 2.5\text{ kg}$, and $k = 10.0\text{ N/m}$.

The phase space trajectory forms closed elliptical contours satisfying Liouville's theorem. Total orbital energy $E = H(q, p)$ is strictly conserved for all $t \ge 0$:

$$\frac{dE}{dt} = \frac{\partial H}{\partial q}\dot{q} + \frac{\partial H}{\partial p}\dot{p} = (-kq)\left(\frac{p}{m}\right) + \left(\frac{p}{m}\right)(kq) = 0$$

Natural undamped frequency is exactly $\omega_0 = \sqrt{k/m} = 2.0\text{ rad/s}$.
