## Proposed replacement

Separate scientific opportunity, spending authority, provider consumption, physical limits and recovery status. After adoption, crossing 720,000 does not independently invalidate a future episode. It remains a historical decision, not an immutable scientific axiom. An accepted replacement still requires finite externally authorized episode attempts, financial authority, accounting basis, reservations, retries and window policy. Removing a scientific token ceiling never grants unlimited consumption.

Scientific parity freezes identical model/effort/effective configuration standards, source exposures, permitted input/output limits, meaningful call opportunities and deterministic-engine availability. The modular bottleneck and comparator's unhandicapped raw access are deliberate treatments. Equal opportunity does not require identical realized tokens, identical output lengths or deletion of a valid discovery. All attempts, refusals and uncertainties remain visible. Missingness, incomplete pairs and drift receive frozen adjudication rules. Repeated turns on one corpus do not establish statistical power or independent replication.

`resources.mjs` requires a finite episode-attempt ceiling, per-window attempt allowance, reservation tokens, accounted-token spending authority, explicit accounting basis, financial cap/reservation, account class and rejected-retry allowance. These are configurable operator spending controls, not hard-coded scientific-validity thresholds. A new quota window resets only its window-attempt count; cumulative attempts, unknown reservations and spending persist. Any extension to episode authority requires an externally signed replacement policy. The frozen scientific plan remains unchanged unless separately amended.

Default recommendation: subscription-only; API spending authority zero; conservative reservation accounting; no implicit account/API fallback; stop before the next permitted call if its reservation does not fit. Actual subscription limits are not inferred from token counts. Window confirmation is supplied by the provisioned runtime under the approved spanning-windows policy. Pause and resume under the same unchanged authorization do not require repeated approvals. A spending-policy change requires a new authorized policy and window identifier without regenerating accepted reasoning.

The accounted-token proxy is distinct from provider quota and scientific opportunity. Conservative mode retains at least each dispatch reservation even when reported input/output is smaller. A specifically approved `reported-input-output-proxy` may reconcile against observed input plus output, but hidden reasoning, overhead or unavailable semantics remain disclosed. This proxy is never relabeled an exact provider charge. Reservations must cover the accepted accounting uncertainty, or execution remains held.

## Accounting fields and limits

Reported input, output, cache and reasoning fields are retained separately; missing fields are `null`. Cached tokens are not added to input again. Reasoning tokens are not added when inclusion semantics are unavailable. Billable USD, subscription quota units and their absence are distinct. Unknown billable charges consume the approved conservative financial reservation; unknown token use retains its token reservation. Definitively unexecuted rejections release consumption reservations while still counting as attempts. Uncertain calls retain reservations and hold their unit.

Exact rendered-source/history input can be measured only by the accepted runtime's pinned tokenizer and serialization policy. Physical context and output limits remain prospective requirements. This implementation checks the measured input plus output allowance before dispatch and binds their configuration; the native platform's effective hard output/reasoning limits and invisible overhead are not established by local counting. Output and hidden future reasoning cannot be exactly predicted. If effective controls or accepted bounded alternatives are absent, hold protected execution; never truncate, omit documents or silently enlarge scope/authority.

Independent Stage 2 retains its own spending/accounting and exact frozen workload. The main runner retrieves externally produced, signed evaluator receipts and does not issue evaluator inference. Generation and evaluation authority must be approved together with finite allocations and overflow rules; no candidate is silently selected or dropped. This amendment does not substitute the synthetic test's three documents for the original protected study and proposes no additional capacity study.
