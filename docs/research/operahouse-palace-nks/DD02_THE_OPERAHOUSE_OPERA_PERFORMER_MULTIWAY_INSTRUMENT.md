### The occasion

A host system constructs a stage on which scholarship becomes available. The stage may store, classify, compress, rank, quote, and interpret a work; it may also generate technically valid receipts. Each operation can make scholarship newly observable. None, by itself, licenses the stage to impersonate the work, the author, or the scientific authority of the work.

This instrument translates the operator's three-part anti-equivalence into a finite, falsifiable computational model and connects it, in order, to Palace Act I's evidence inheritance, Palace Act II's temporal admission, and prospective thinker-conditioned interventions. Rather than suppress surprising branches, it records them with their own evidence classes. Its objective is to make productive novelty and authority inflation separately measurable.

> **Central research question.** Under fixed source evidence, which variations in hosting, temporal access and interpretation alter (a) core source-and-warrant states, (b) observable encounter states, (c) causal histories, and (d) independently evaluated conclusions?

## 1 · The three layers as disjoint *types*, not forbidden words

Let `H` denote host infrastructure (operahouse), `O` the research encounter (opera), and `P` the original source and warranted source claims (performer). `H`, `O` and `P` are distinct **role types**. A term such as *Aperture* may legitimately appear in both a source title and an instrument name; lexical coincidence never changes the type of the underlying object.

For each object `x` store the tuple

`x = (object_id, layer, origin_class, source_root, digest, temporal_coordinates, visibility, authority_ceiling)`.

Required typing discipline:

1. A host receipt attests a declared computation or file operation within a specified scope. It cannot author a scholarly source or authenticate external origin by self-assertion.
2. A research interpretation may cite a source; it may neither silently edit that source nor convert an unverified graph link into a source quotation.
3. A source title, body, attribution uncertainty and admissible provenance accompany every derivative route. An unresolved author remains unresolved rather than being assigned an institutionally convenient identity.
4. The default public research encounter uses research-native language. Host diagnostic interfaces remain available for experts on request; legitimate scholarly terminology is never erased by a global keyword filter.
5. An interpreter can create hypotheses, but its hypotheses receive a distinct provenance class and cannot become source facts without independent evidence.

**Non-impersonation law:**

\[
H\not\equiv O,\qquad O\not\equiv P,\qquad H\not\equiv P.
\]

This is **type separation** rather than a declaration that the three sets have no physical shared components, interactions, or co-occurrences. An object can be connected across layers through explicit, typed morphisms without collapsing its epistemic authority.

### 1.1 · Two different invariance questions

For host adapters `h₁, h₂` with declared equivalent evidence-processing capabilities, source set `D` and fixed query `q`, require a conditional core equivalence:

\[
\pi_{\rm core}E(h_1,D,q)=\pi_{\rm core}E(h_2,D,q)
\quad\text{provided predeclared matching conditions hold.}
\]

But the observer's experience may vary:

\[
\Pr(Q_{\rm observer}\mid E(h_1,D,q))
\stackrel{?}{=}
\Pr(Q_{\rm observer}\mid E(h_2,D,q)).
\]

The first equation is a program-equivalence contract. The second is an empirical question requiring reader data. A changed theme can leave the code-level core unchanged while changing which questions readers notice. Neither equation implies the other.

### 1.2 · The host-substitution falsifier

Predeclare the canonical core projection, not merely an output string. Randomly swap equivalent host adapters while holding original source bytes, authenticated source registry, retrieval budget, tool permissions, and core query fixed. Compare canonical source IDs, digests, cited spans, theory roots, rejection classes and operator permissions. Treat divergence as a **counterexample to host independence** only after the capability equivalence assumption has been tested. Compare user comprehension separately under a blinded, preregistered reader protocol.

## 2 · Priority one: Palace Act I — source inheritance before new claims

The historical Palace Act I protocol uses matched synthetic histories to investigate how preserving, misleading, compressing, or misattributing contextual records changes downstream decisions. The source-free Palace results are experimental records, not evidence of real-world confidentiality. The successor inherits the *mechanisms* through new, separately registered experiments, without importing its outcome numbers as priors.

An accepted record contains `source_id`, `sha256(original_bytes)`, owner/warrant state, temporal coordinates and negative-evidence relationships. Let `H_t` denote the admissible history at time `t` and `Z_B=C_B(H_t)` a `B`-byte capsule. Loss occurs if `C_B` erases a distinction needed for later recovery or falsification.

**Mandatory Palace-I checks:**

- Preserve full source bytes and an append-only active/withdrawn ledger outside lossy working-memory capsules.
- Separate retained, retrieved and actually decision-used evidence; never infer one from another.
- Make contradiction support explicit. A finite capsule cannot promise to contain an unbounded complete contradiction list; place complete negative evidence in referenced archival storage and carry its root/count/availability in the capsule.
- Compare the capsule against a competent full-history policy with equivalent task information, action budgets and calibration options.
- Stress regime shifts, misleading inherited context, foreign-but-authentic histories, deleted residual anomalies and side-channel disclosure. Mark reconstruction bounds as task- and distribution-specific.

The Palace-I challenge is **sufficient representation under honest loss accounting**, not maximum compression alone.

## 3 · Priority two: Palace Act II — epistemic time and institutional standing

Record distinct coordinates `t_event`, `t_observed`, `t_admitted`, `t_available` as event-specific metadata; require the order declared by the instrument where it is substantively applicable. Only evidence available by the policy decision time may influence its update. A future record must not change a past as-of reconstruction:

\[
\pi_{t}\big(H\cup\{r:t_{\rm available}(r)>t\}\big)
=\pi_t(H).
\]

Source withdrawal emits a separate tombstone with its own event time and authority; historical readback retains the state as it was *known at the time*. Retrieval latency is not identical to action latency, and action success can precede warranted belief revision. The Palace Act II successor should retain explicit difference coordinates, including `action_alignment_time − belief_revision_time`, rather than silently assigning a favorable sign to temporal disparity.

**Standing intervention:** Change an institutionally assigned standing label while preserving source observations, task identity, permissible actions and evidential reliability. Measure changes in admission, posterior updating and explanation. This operationalizes one bounded institutional-authority question; it cannot independently adjudicate intersectional discrimination or a thinker's whole theory.

The observed Palace I–II interface is **source-free and prospective relative to Experiment B**, even where its own code and tests have run.

## 4 · From simple programs to multiway phenomena

A computational state is

\[
S_t=(D_t,A_t,T_t,Q_t,H_t,\tau_t),
\]

where `D` is a typed source registry, `A` the active-admission projection, `T` the append-only tombstone structure, `Q` the claims and warrants, `H` the history graph, and `τ` the declared clock coordinates. A finite local rewriting relation `S ─r→ S′` is permitted only if rule `r` passes its preconditions. Different enabled rules generate different histories, which should not be silently overwritten by a single chosen trajectory.

For a fork `S ─r₁→ S₁` and `S ─r₂→ S₂`, ask separately:

- **Joinability:** whether there exists a common state reachable under permitted future rewrites.
- **Observable equivalence:** whether `π_obs(S₁)=π_obs(S₂)` under a defined observer.
- **History equivalence:** whether their event and dependency graphs are isomorphic under a stated mapping.
- **Authority equivalence:** whether the same actor had authority at every consequential transition.

Equality of one projection provides no theorem about the other three. In particular, *confluence* in rewriting theory is a precise property of a rewrite relation; it is not itself suppression. The risk under investigation is **provenance-erasing forced reconciliation**: a system that substitutes an equal-looking endpoint for the historical distinctions required to explain it.

The program's multiway experiment must preserve complete state-transition labels, event indices and causal dependencies. Finite examples do not establish general computational irreducibility, spacetime physics, branchial quantum effects, or the global causal invariance conditions studied in NKS-related physical models.

### 4.1 · A finite constructive counterexample

At a decision tick before a synthetic document becomes available, `ADMIT` is blocked. After availability, one route admits and later retracts the document; another route retracts it without ever admitting it. Their **public active-source projections** can agree (no active source), while their historical records differ. They therefore exhibit observational equivalence under a bounded projection without historical identity.

This illustrates the analytic risk of measuring only terminal user-facing state. It is **not** a proof that the complete archival states are identical, and it does not require making a system non-confluent.

### 5.1 · Why Caro, Butler and Sedgwick matter operationally

**Caro / institutional reconstruction:** Require a trace connecting actor, rule, corridor or form, delay, statutory/administrative basis and material consequence. A before/after website screenshot without an institutional mechanism fails the Caro-conditioned test. In source-free trials, the corridor is a timed access graph rather than an analogy to a hallway.

**Butler / iterative recognition:** Repeat classification acts under controlled norm changes. Observe whether the permitted category grammar channels a subject or source into a default role, whether repeated classifications drift, and how adversarial self-description challenges the institution's taxonomy. Never assume the synthetic category reproduces the social consequences of gender performativity.

**Sedgwick / reparative preservation:** Permit contradictory, nonjoinable, partially warranted readings to coexist with their own source paths and revision rights. Measure whether a representation suppresses warranted alternatives or forces an unsupported scalar verdict. Reparative storage may preserve plurality while still rejecting unsupported assertions.

## 6 · The research program's factorial structure

**Units:** source-free synthetic source episodes or consenting, independently recruited reader episodes when separately approved. **Arms:** matched host adapters, matched evidence, randomized presentation and standing cues, predeclared observer capacities, and independent hidden evaluation fixtures. **Precedence:** P0 membrane → P1 inherited evidence → P2 temporal handling → P3 selected Thinker interventions → P4 optional interactions.

The minimal core factorial is `2 hosts × 2 evidence-access policies × 2 timing policies × 2 interpretation policies`, with source bytes fixed. Randomize episode order; use common provider/model controls and resource budgets; predeclare ground truth, falsifiers, decision metrics, exclusion rules and multiple-comparison policy before data collection.

Required primary outcomes:

1. **Core equivalence violation rate:** proportion of matched host pairs that differ in canonical warranted content.
2. **Temporal leakage rate:** proportion of unavailable synthetic claims affecting an earlier decision.
3. **Unsupported promotion rate:** fraction of host-generated claims or receipts incorrectly treated as source warrants or scientific proof.
4. **Observable-history collapse:** fraction of equal public projections for which distinct causal/custody histories are erased by a downstream decision.
5. **Competent-null surplus:** difference between a new multiway system and a matched, independently implemented union with equivalent information and operational affordances.

Reader-level outcomes (hypothesis formulation, comprehension, fatigue, trust) remain **unmeasured** unless recruited and analyzed under a separate study; do not assign quantities from browser simulation.

### 6.1 · Human agency and the limits of automation

A technical engine can represent missing routes, competing histories, visibility failures, audit burdens and corrective action opportunities. It cannot independently measure institutional injury or ethical legitimacy merely from synthetic proxies. Foucault's power relations, Collins's matrix of domination, Dotson's epistemic exclusion, McKittrick's Black geographies and Berlant's slow attrition require contextual interpretation and appropriate evidence if any empirical social claim is made.

## 8 · Critical counterfactual and next falsifier

**Refractive counterfactual:** Suppose we remove host identity from every readout and discover the benchmark improves. That result could reflect cleaner information access, reduced terminology contamination, lost diagnostics, or evaluator leakage. It does not automatically vindicate an anti-leakage law. The strongest experiment holds core source content and tool affordances fixed, randomly manipulates host language, and tests *both* reader interpretation and evaluator source-warrant integrity with separate observers.

The immediate falsifier is a matched experiment in which host interchange changes no warranted content, produces no misattributed claims, and shows no repeatable difference in human hypothesis formulation under adequate statistical power. That finding would **narrow** the proposed benefit of the three-layer distinction; it would not falsify the simple type-safety benefits already implemented in toy software.
