## 4. Result disclosures remained easy to miss and structurally ambiguous

The operator nearly missed `Read the rest of the analysis` and later discovered additional disclosures for open questions, source references, and the full receipt/raw response.

The current disclosure affordances use native/triangle-style detail semantics in places where the visual language does not reliably read as open/close controls. The operator had previously requested `+` / `−` affordances and reported that the triangular indicators were particularly weak in the current font/visual system.

Human consequence observed:

- hidden answer content was almost missed;
- after opening multiple drawers, there was insufficient persistent indication of which sections were expanded and how to collapse them;
- `Suggested next step` sounded terminal even though substantial answer material remained below it;
- the source-reference explanation and raw/full response appeared as additional answer-like material rather than clearly subordinate verification/details.

Repair target:

- use explicit `+` when closed and `−` when open for every human-facing disclosure in the result lane;
- distinguish the primary answer from optional details;
- move `Suggested next step` after the complete primary analysis or label it as `Possible next action — optional`;
- label source references as `Sources named by the AI — not independently verified`;
- keep raw receipt/provider structure behind a clearly technical disclosure.
