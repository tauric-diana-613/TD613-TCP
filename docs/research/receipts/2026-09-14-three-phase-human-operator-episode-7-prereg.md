### Clean PASS requires all of the following

- an admitted AI answer returns;
- the submitted task remains identifiable as the instruction that produced the answer;
- before the engineer-facing technical report, the human encounters a plain-language orientation equivalent to:
  - **Question:** did the job actually run twice, or was completion merely reported twice?

  - **Privacy consequence:** only the three shareable documents traveled and the private/local material remained outside the route;
- the human can state that distinction in ordinary language without asking the AI a second explanatory question;
- primary answer content is distinguishable from optional details / source references / technical receipts;
- the human can locate the continuation action without searching for a hidden control;
- Marrowline arrival visibly preserves the ordinary Marrowline product rather than presenting an opaque blocking membrane;
- the imported context can be recognized as imported context;
- the ordinary composer remains discoverable;
- the human can identify the explicit close-imported-context control and the separate Loom-return route without coaching.

### Phase-1 RED conditions

Any of the following closes Phase 1 as `FAIL`:

- provider transport ends without an admitted answer;
- the result begins with technical material while the human still has to infer the basic finding;
- the task/result relationship is lost;
- the human cannot tell which block is the answer;
