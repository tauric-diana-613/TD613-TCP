## Maestro rehearsal — hear the correction before trusting the controller

Begin with a voice whose pitch changes while its identity should survive. The investigative hinge is concrete: a transformation can improve one observable while damaging another.

Now follow that hinge into Semantic error, p. 3. The tempting leap would equate a smeared acoustic transient with an unstable interpretation. Keep the comparison at the level it earns: both accounts require naming the quantity being preserved, the permitted actuator and the relevant time scale. Counterreading: shared control vocabulary may organize an exposition without establishing a common causal mechanism. Semantic error's p. 3 assertion that pure proportional control cannot remove steady-state error is overbroad without plant assumptions. The executed scalar counterexample uses the same gain K=2: a static plant y=u leaves error 1/3, while an integrating plant dy/dt=u yields e(t)=exp(-2t), approaching zero. ## Composer rehearsal — a chorus can harmonize around a mistake

Provisional synthesis: preserve two coordinates in collaborative research—agreement among representations and correctness against an independently specified target. Treating either as the other would overstate what the apparatus measured.

Cognitive time, p. 4, introduces a Kuramoto order parameter for phase coherence, then gives it a much larger interpretation as temporal linearity. Quantum bridge, p. 7, associates low entropy with a confident representation; its own table also includes a low-entropy, negative-feedback state described as confidently erroneous. Topology, p. 2, connects a Fisher metric to statistical distinguishability before making further claims about physical distance. Swarm, p. 6, supplies the practical reminder that an apparent distance depends on the chosen covariance geometry.

The useful construction is modest and sharp. Four toy observers return the same answer with identical phases. Their phase order parameter is 1 and their categorical Shannon entropy is 0. Assign all four the stipulated correct target and error is 0; assign all four the other answer and error is 1. The executed examples have identical agreement/confidence summaries and opposite correctness. These are explicit constructed mathematical cases; their Shannon entropy is not a measured density-matrix entropy, and their phases are not human temporal observations.

The speculative research proposal is therefore to ask whether a highly synchronized collaboration can become exceptionally legible while becoming less correct. Rival explanation: the papers share rhetorical templates and borrowed equations, so their convergence could reflect common composition rather than a shared implemented system. Another rival: their measurements address different objects, making even a well-behaved analogy unsuitable for numerical cross-comparison.

Falsifier for a future adapted trial: if an alleged truth-calibrated score distinguishes held-out correct and jointly wrong answers under independently established targets and matched conditions, the simple agreement-only account becomes inadequate. No such trial was conducted here. The seven texts support this question; they do not establish emergent physical spacetime, quantum LLM computation or independent empirical replication.
