## 3A. Title/body anti-conflation assay — academic camp and cryptic multivalence

Titles and complete manuscripts are authored material and must be preserved **verbatim**. An entire manuscript—not merely its title—may sustain simultaneously a serious technical argument, deliberate academia camp, encoded critique, irony, layered metaphor, rhetorical misdirection, and more than one defensible reading. These modes can operate through structure, scene, mathematical notation, argument progression, and the relation between passages; interpretation must not flatten the manuscript into one literal thesis or discard an alternative reading because the surface language sounds playful. A title remains a useful entry and navigation signal, never a validated substitute for examining the actual manuscript.

**Preregister a paired intervention on the same fixed manuscript body:** (i) authentic title, (ii) neutral descriptive title assigned only inside the disposable assay, (iii) blinded/opaque title of matched length, and (iv) title with high lexical overlap but an intentionally contradictory body from a clearly designated synthetic negative control. The genuine title is never overwritten in the source archive, UI canon, or citation. These alternate labels are ephemeral assay variables, never editorial replacements.

Measure separately:

1. **Retrieval sensitivity:** title-only lexical route and metadata retrieval may legitimately change across titles, whereas body-passage retrieval must remain grounded in the unchanged manuscript bytes, paragraph and page locations, and source SHA-256.
2. **Synthesis sensitivity:** all claimed *content-grounded* arguments, mathematical relations, contradictions, semantic neighborhoods, candidate evidence, and score components must track body witnesses rather than title resemblance. Title-only candidate rank movement must be labeled as title/metadata influence, not new substantive scholarship.
3. **Whole-manuscript interpretive plurality:** test whether the manuscript's full argumentative architecture supports multiple coexisting readings—technical, allegorical, political, comic, metaphorical, or encoded—rather than restricting ambiguity to a title or isolated phrase. Record each defensible reading with supporting and disconfirming cited passages, methodological context, and uncertainty. Some readings may interact without being reducible to one another. Ambiguity may remain unresolved; neither irony nor metaphor warrants automatic empirical promotion.
4. **Adversarial negative:** a title strongly matching an existing candidate while the body rebuts that candidate must not manufacture supporting evidence. The scorer must distinguish support, contradiction, unclear relevance, and absence of evidence.
5. **Invariance audit:** use the same source body and computational scoring configuration. A movement in a title-derived search score may be legitimate; a movement in a supposedly manuscript-derived mathematical score without any changed mathematical input requires an explicit provenance explanation.

**Whole-text counterfactual:** preserve the authentic title while systematically masking, permuting, or swapping only selected body passages in a clearly marked disposable negative control (never the source manuscript). Test whether interpretations and evidence-linked candidate weights respond to the altered substantive argument; repeat against the unmodified full text. Preregister the alterations and expected invariant quantities. Interpretive plurality must be carried as separate, cited hypotheses, never compressed to a single machine-assigned theme.

**Outcome requirement:** produce a typed score breakdown separating title/metadata prior, body-evidence support, body-evidence contradiction, uncertainty, and purely computational diagnostics. If any of these axes are unavailable in the current engine, mark that axis **UNIMPLEMENTED** rather than substituting an LLM-generated interpretation or forcing a change. This test applies to the recovered seven-source condition and the prospectively blind eighth without leaking either into the A0 baseline.

## 3B. Semantic-semiotic **fusion** instrumentation and counterfactuals

The Vixen semantic-semiotic engine has three real scoring channels, **not** a semantic-only retrieval model. The current standard hybrid mode combines lexical BM25F (0.50), hashed-Jacobi SVD semantic similarity (0.30), and structured semiotic affinity (0.20), with bounded additive bonuses. Alternative lexical/phrase/exact and SRC-conditioned query regimes have different weights and gates. The initial metadata-only inline fallback explicitly sets semantic and semiotic scores to zero; label it **FALLBACK_NOT_FUSION**, and do not include it in claims about full-engine semiotic competence.

The semiotic channel computes query-profile matches against S/W/A/P control planes, cluster identity, work and protocol identity, and a finite tag taxonomy, with bounded motif/analogy/mechanism candidate ceilings. These are **index/query structural alignments**, not by themselves human-level interpretation of a complete text. A 'MECHANISM_CANDIDATE' retrieval rung conveys search eligibility only, never proof of a mechanism or authorship.

Required fixed-query measurements for A0, A1, A2, and both title/body counterfactual suites:

- Record every hit's `s_lex`, `s_sem`, `s_semi`, `s_src_proj`, `s_src_sig`, `score_total`, `highest_rung`, `semiotic_tags`, `plane_vec`, `why`, source-id and passage offsets, along with query/mode and the exact weight vector in `src_conditioning`.
- Compare **fusion** against lexical-only, semantic-ablated, and semiotic-ablated regimes without modifying the immutable A0 artifact; conduct ablations in independently labeled, ephemeral assay runs, not by silently changing the production formula or deleting a corpus axis.
- Challenge the semiotic tagger with matching technical terms inside allegory/irony/negation, with a title that implies one mechanism while the entire manuscript argues another, and with two distinct interpretations supported by nonidentical passage sets. Measure false analogy, false mechanism rungs, polarity and evidence-credit errors.
- Track whether new body evidence updates semiotic taxonomy and document-plane vectors **at all**, whether those updates are reflected in precomputed index seals, and whether downstream synthesis and Salon score inputs receive the new features. A change to retrieval hit ranking alone is not demonstrated synthesis hydration.
- Preserve invariances in pure SVD/clock/geometry scores unless their actual numerical inputs change. Require a typed, source-grounded provenance link for every claimed interpretation and downstream candidate-score delta.

**Pass/fail distinction:** FUSION_IMPLEMENTED means the independently scored channels and weights operate according to code; WHOLE_MANUSCRIPT_SEMIOTIC_INTERPRETATION_WITNESSED requires full-text passage-based, contrastive evidence and counterexamples, which the existing rule/tag-affinity logic alone does not establish.
