## 1. Fault: a governance separation was mislabeled a universal metric theorem
Former P-044 asserted \(\tau_{\rm theory}\perp\tau_{\rm ambiance}\perp\tau_{\rm resynthesis}\) and later treatments claimed \(\partial g_{\mu\nu}/\partial u_{\rm ambiance}\equiv0\). Distinct clocks and separate release authorities do not supply an inner product, a differentiable ambient manifold, or proof of metric independence. Nor does *relational/local-section ambiance* mean the same thing as a change in UX palette or presentation.

The defensible requirement is **conditional invariance of protected computations and authority under a verified presentation-only intervention**. A change in observer legibility may still occur. A change in access, local condition, memory, route, custody, or governing input instead changes the relational system and is *outside the presentation-only domain*.

## 2. Types and intervention boundary
- \(D\): fixed evidence and provenance, including identity, source, missingness and recording/custody context.
- \(S\): fixed structural data, model parameters, algorithm revision and execution state; includes access, routes and permissions.
- \(p\in\mathcal P\): a **presentation-only** control such as a palette or visual theme, whose admissibility is explicitly verified.
- \(\ell_r\in\mathcal A(U_r)\): **relational ambiance**, a local section whose restrictions, historical route or accessibility may differ; it is NOT interchangeable with \(p\).
- \(K\): protected computed outputs of a declared numerical/causal kernel.
- \(A\): an authorization/admissibility decision based on independent custody, policy and human authority inputs.
- \(Y_i\): declared, measurable observation/discovery outcome for observer \(i\); an outcome never confers authorization by itself.

An intervention \(T_p\) is admissible to the invariance test only if it preserves \(D\), \(S\), route identity, operator grants, recording identity, inputs and the selected computational step. The proof obligation is conditional over those interventions, not universal over any arbitrary meaning of ambiance.

## 3. Replacing the false theorem
For every eligible \(p_1,p_2\) with declared fixed \(D,S\):

\[
  K(D,S,p_1)=K(D,S,p_2),\quad
  A(D,S,p_1)=A(D,S,p_2).
\]

These are **system contracts to verify**, not physical conservation laws. A component that reads \(p\) inside a protected numerical decision violates the contract unless the change is explicitly retyped as a structural intervention with fresh provenance.

The separate, empirical observer-response hypothesis is:

\[
H_0:\Delta_{\rm obs}=0,\qquad
\Delta_{\rm obs}=\mathbb E[Y\mid do(p_1),D,S]-\mathbb E[Y\mid do(p_2),D,S].
\]

The sign, magnitude, uncertainty, population and causal identification of \(\Delta_{\rm obs}\) are open to observation. A deterministic toy observer is not a human study. A local section \(\ell_r\) can affect which observations are possible, but such structural changes cannot be smuggled into \(p\).

**Three-clock governance:** \(\tau_{\rm theory}\), \(\tau_{\rm presentation}\), and \(\tau_{\rm empirical}\) have separate provenance, trigger and promotion authority. This is separation of responsibilities; no pairwise orthogonality or independence theorem follows. An unexecuted empirical protocol remains at zero empirical credit.

## 6. Falsification criteria
- Same admissible data/model/step with different **protected** output => **FAIL computational invariance**.
- Presentation-only change creates/mutates scientific credit, permission, grant, or release decision => **FAIL authority invariant**.
- Any substantive structural change mislabeled as presentation-only => **OUT OF SCOPE / INPUT TYPING FAIL**, not a false assurance of invariance.
- Observer responses differ while kernel/authority stay stable => **compatible with the revised hypothesis**; estimate, replicate and qualify.
- Observer responses do not differ => **compatible with null**; does not establish universal zero effect.
- Deliberately corrupted simulator passes the invariance test => **FAIL assay sensitivity**.
