For a state `x=(h,s)ᵀ` with two perfectly colocated signals observed through the **same** mixture `h+s`, any finite bank of linear filters acting identically on that mixture has an instantaneous impulse-response operator of the form `A=w[1,1]`. ### Refractive defect as a prospective measurement, not a metaphysical formula

For a chosen group action `g` on a well-defined mathematical skeleton `x`, a specified renderer `F`, and a corresponding image action `ρ(g)`, define a **test-specific equivariance residual**:

`E_g(F,x) = d(F(g·x), ρ(g)·F(x)) / max(ε, d_scale)`.

- Choose the group, image metric, observation operator, fidelity tolerance and fixed random seeds *before* the test.
