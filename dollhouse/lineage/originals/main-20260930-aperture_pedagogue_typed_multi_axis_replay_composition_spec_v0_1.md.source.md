## 3. Isolation perturbations

Construct a baseline packet and exactly three hostile variants.

### M · Measurement-only perturbation
Move from the lower to upper declared measurement-model coordinate while keeping decision coordinate and route fixed.

Required:

```text

```

### D · Decision-only perturbation
Move from the lower to upper declared decision-specification coordinate while keeping measurement coordinate and route fixed.

Required:

```text

```

### R · Route-only perturbation
Swap to the second Moss Lantern route while keeping measurement and decision coordinates fixed.

Required:

```text

decision axis unchanged
endpoint unchanged
operation order changed
```

If any perturbation leaks across axes, the gauntlet fails.
