# Pedagogue Mathematical Counterpoints

Status: **RESEARCH-SIDE / NON-ANCESTRAL / HUMAN-GATED**

Mathematical counterpoints are not part of Potato’s intellectual ancestry unless source provenance independently establishes that route.

They are introduced by TD613 when an external mathematical construction appears useful for attacking or sharpening an already-declared Pedagogue mechanism.

```text
mathematical counterpoint != lineage ancestor
mathematical rhyme != shared ontology
named object != complete authorship provenance
```

## Jakob Steiner

Jakob Steiner (1796–1863), the Swiss projective geometer, is introduced here for the first time as a TD613 mathematical counterpoint. He is not Rudolf Steiner and is not retroactively inserted into Potato’s ASP / Flow-Core / Dome-World lineage.

### Counterpoint 1 · projective dependence

Steiner’s 1832 *Systematische Entwickelung der Abhängigkeit geometrischer Gestalten voneinander* sought a small family of fundamental relations through which dependence among geometric figures and transfer of properties into more complex configurations could be understood.

Pedagogue does **not** inherit nineteenth-century projective geometry as ontology. The research question is narrower:

> Can a family of non-equivalent AIA projections preserve a declared relational invariant in a way that is usefully attacked with projective-geometric reasoning?

Admissible hostile comparisons include:

- preserve superficial visual similarity while breaking the governed relation;
- preserve the governed relation while changing the projection surface;
- compare a projective explanation against a weaker non-projective explanation under matched conditions;
- reject any result that requires an undeclared total-field observer.

The card may inform AIA projection assays. It grants no geometry crown.

### Counterpoint 2 · combinatorial coverage

Jakob Steiner’s 1853 `Combinatorische Aufgaben` is historically associated with problems later routed into the modern family of Steiner systems and Steiner triple systems.

Modern Steiner systems are block designs with controlled incidence. They are useful here only as a candidate language for **declared relation coverage under a fixed probe budget**.

Possible Pedagogue assay:

```text
same observation budget

repeated probe family
vs
arbitrary diverse probe family
vs
block-design / controlled-incidence probe family

measure:
  uncovered declared relation classes
  duplicate coverage
  rank / identifiability where applicable
  replay stability where applicable
```

The block design is not presumed optimal.

```text
balanced incidence != epistemic optimality
uniform coverage != equal decision value
coverage improvement != reconstruction improvement
```

### Historical naming caveat

The modern name `Steiner system` does not establish exclusive authorship of the modern theory. Related triple-system work predates Jakob Steiner, and reference literature explicitly notes that the modern name is historically imperfect.

That caveat is retained as a Pedagogue provenance lesson:

```text
name attached to object != complete authorship provenance
```

## Steiner surname identity control

Because TD613 also contains an independently important Rudolf Steiner coordinate, any search or recurrence analysis using the bare token `Steiner` must disambiguate at least:

```text
RUDOLF_STEINER_1861_1925
JAKOB_STEINER_1796_1863
```

No previous TD613 recurrence is reclassified as a Jakob/Rudolf collision merely because the collision is possible. The control exists prospectively and as a hostile test.

## Recurrence confounders

Before treating a repeated thinker or named coordinate as meaningful convergence, Pedagogue should distinguish:

1. `ENTITY_COLLISION` — the same token names different entities;
2. `SHARED_SOURCE_INHERITANCE` — routes share an ancestor or explicit exposure;
3. `HIGH_DEGREE_BREADTH_EFFECT` — a transdisciplinary corpus is statistically more likely to recur in a transdisciplinary search;
4. `INDEPENDENT_FUNCTIONAL_RECURRENCE` — separately routed cases share a mechanism after exposure controls;
5. `UNKNOWN` — provenance is insufficient to distinguish the above.

Unknown exposure never counts as independence.

Recurrence never establishes common cause, metaphysical truth, genealogy, or source authority by itself.
