## 0. Why this execution exists

The Giving 12-step bundle hydrated Pedagogue with six portable interface/evidence operators and deliberately held their transfer experiments behind a human gesture. The operator has now supplied that gesture.

This execution therefore performs **T1–T6 as bounded audits**, not as product patches. The audit asks whether the Giving-born operators travel cleanly to other TD613 surfaces, whether those surfaces exhibit the same failure modes, and whether any surface already provides a stronger positive control.

The distinction matters:


## 1. Method and claim ceiling

The pass inspected current repository source on the post-release relocked `main` descendant. It sampled the highest-value contract/UI files named by the original T1–T6 transfer plan and searched for adjacent status, missingness, context, advisory, responsive, and fixture semantics.

Evidence classes used here:

- **DIRECT_STATIC** — explicit code/contract language establishes the behavior or prohibition.
- **STRUCTURAL_STATIC** — source topology strongly supports the finding, but runtime consequence was not observed in this pass.
- **UNRESOLVED_RUNTIME** — a browser/runtime trace would be needed to close the question.
- **HIGH_SPECULATION** — a portable research hypothesis generated from the cross-surface pattern; never implementation authority.

This packet does not claim repository exhaustiveness, human usability evidence, causal user effects, or empirical superiority of one design. It does not rerun production browsers and creates no deployment authority.

`app/hush-pr111-review-candidate-bridge.js` does not merely emit a warning. When an eligible review candidate survives the bounded filters, it places that candidate into the protected output surface and states:


**Judgment:** `ADVISORY_WITHOUT_ROUTE` = **NOT ESTABLISHED** for this sampled bridge.

That proves the visible context is not dead metadata at packet construction time. However, this static pass did not trace every downstream generator selector to prove exactly how strongly each trigger constrains final candidate retrieval.

**Judgment:** `DORMANT_CONTEXT_WITHOUT_CONSEQUENCE` = **PARTIALLY FALSIFIED / RUNTIME CONSEQUENCE DEPTH UNRESOLVED**.

**Judgment:** `RESPONSIVE_ROLE_DRIFT` = **UNRESOLVED_RUNTIME** for Hush in this pass.

`app/engine/ash-keep-reader-disagreement.js` forbids unresolved reader states from carrying observed disagreement summary content. Pairwise comparisons become `UNRESOLVED` when either reader is unresolved, and their missingness travels into the comparison record.

```text
ownership_inference_authorized = false
prediction_authorized = false
```

**Judgment:** `NEGATIVE_RESULT_NEEDS_SCOPE` = **SATISFIED BY DESIGN**.

# 7. Cross-surface synthesis

The most important result is not a new list of UI defects. The six Giving-born operators behave more like **epistemic discriminators** than style rules.

Across the sampled surfaces they separate three states:

```text
A. representational overclaim
B. disciplined bounded uncertainty
C. consequence that remains unresolved and therefore must stay held
```

Giving supplied examples of A. Safe Harbor, Flow-Core, and Ash supplied unusually strong examples of B. Hush supplied a useful mixture of B and C.

That is stronger Pedagogue behavior than a lint rule that assumes every matched pattern is a defect.

## High-speculation transfer hypothesis H1 — Consequence Conservation

**Status:** HIGH_SPECULATION / HELD

A human-facing representation appears trustworthy when the semantic weight of its label is conserved through the action it actually authorizes.

Possible future generic question:

> **Does the interface representation carry more certainty, scope, role, or consequence than the underlying state transition can support?**

This could unify several currently separate operators without replacing them:

- `STATUS_ONTOLOGY_OVERCLAIM`
- `DORMANT_CONTEXT_WITHOUT_CONSEQUENCE`
- `ADVISORY_WITHOUT_ROUTE`
- `RESPONSIVE_ROLE_DRIFT`

The speculative common variable is not visual inconsistency. It is a mismatch between **represented consequence** and **executable consequence**.

No new Pedagogue operator is authorized by this hypothesis.

## High-speculation transfer hypothesis H2 — Aperture Before Absence

**Status:** HIGH_SPECULATION / HELD

Giving, Flow-Core, and Ash suggest a transferable invariant:

```text
absence claim authority
≤
visible retrieval / measurement aperture
```

A zero, abstention, unresolved reader, or missing source becomes pedagogically safe only when the operator can recover what was searched/measured, what remained unavailable, and which routes failed or stayed held.

No doctrine promotion is authorized by this packet.

## High-speculation transfer hypothesis H3 — Role Before Repetition

**Status:** HIGH_SPECULATION / HELD

Giving's contributor/payee overlap and Ash's reader/provenance architecture support a common diagnostic posture:

```text
repeated label or actor
→ preserve lane/role first
→ compare second
→ infer identity/equivalence only under separately earned authority
```

This may be more portable than a domain-specific same-name rule.

No generic engine mutation is authorized here.
