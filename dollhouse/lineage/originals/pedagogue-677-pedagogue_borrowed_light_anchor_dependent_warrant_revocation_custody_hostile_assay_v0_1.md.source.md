## 1. Candidate law under attack

A current warrant may be admitted only when at least one preregistered lawful support lineage remains current.

An exogenous support lineage binds exactly:

```text
semantic_anchor_key
target_fingerprint
observed_field
required_value
```

A semantically different replacement anchor does not inherit that support merely because it exposes the same value. If the sole exogenous support lineage becomes inactive, absent, conflicted, mismatched, or semantically replaced, current warrant authority must disappear while historical support custody remains reconstructable.

An independently lawful alternate support lineage may preserve the warrant after exogenous withdrawal.

```text
support disappearance != historical erasure
support replacement != continuity
same observed value != same support lineage
alternate lawful support != forced revocation
duplicate support != confidence
```


```text

```

If E3 keeps `W_ALPHA` current merely because the replacement anchor shows the same value, E3 is falsified.

## 4. Hostile rooms

### BL01 — Last Lamp Out
A warrant has one lawful exogenous support lineage. Withdraw its anchor. Current warrant authority must disappear while historical support remains.

### BL02 — Second Lamp
A warrant has the exogenous lineage plus an independent lawful declared support lineage. Withdraw the anchor. The warrant must remain current through the independent lineage only.

### BL03 — Same Light, Different Lamp
The strong falsifier. A semantic replacement emits the same observed value. The old lineage must not transfer.

### BL04 — Two Windows Disagree
The relevant semantic anchor has a same-epoch E2 conflict. The dependent warrant must abstain rather than force current authority.

### BL05 — Wrong Color Bulb
Anchor key is current but target, field, or required value does not match the declared dependency. The lineage is not current support.

### BL06 — Chandelier Counting
Duplicate an identical support lineage under another `lineage_id`. The duplicate must not create confidence, extra semantic support, or alter current authority.

### BL07 — Lamp Tags Shuffled
Rename support-lineage IDs and reverse serialization. Current authority and semantic support fingerprint must remain invariant.

### BL08 — Afterimage
After sole-support withdrawal, historical evidence that the warrant was once lawfully supported remains reconstructable even though current authority is gone.

### BL09 — One of Two Anchors Leaves
Two distinct lawful exogenous support lineages independently support the same warrant. Withdraw one. The other must preserve current authority without laundering the withdrawn lineage.

### BL10 — Photograph of Light
A compact object carrying only the current observed value but not the bound semantic anchor identity cannot satisfy a lineage that names a specific anchor.

### BL11 — Post-Hoc Rewiring
Attempt to mutate a sealed dependency ledger after observation. Must refuse as `SEALED_WARRANT_DEPENDENCY_LEDGER_IMMUTABLE`.

### BL12 — E1/E2 Control
With a clean active E2 anchor and a matching direct dependency, E3 must admit the warrant without changing E1/E2 current-view or revision semantics.
