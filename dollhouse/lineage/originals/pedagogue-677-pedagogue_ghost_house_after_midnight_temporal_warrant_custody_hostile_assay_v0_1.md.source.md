```text

```

```text

```

```text

!=

```

and:

```text

!=
conflict never occurred
```

This chamber attacks whether C2 may be silently widened from **current genealogy** into **temporal custody**.

## 2. Formal bounded object

For episode index `t = 0..n` let:

```text
E_t = admitted primitive evidence at t
R_t = candidate derivation rules at t
X_t = preregistered contradiction families at t
Q_t(w) = C2 snapshot disposition for requested warrant w at t
G_t(w) = semantic lineage fingerprints supporting w at t

```

The temporal ledger is:

```text
L = [(J_t, Q_t, G_t)] + adjacent transition receipts T_(t-1,t)
```

Transition receipts may describe observed synthetic changes. They do not create authority by themselves.

Current warrant authority is always evaluated from the latest episode's current C2 snapshot.

```text

```
