## 1. Research question

Once a genuinely new exogenous observation is admitted as a primitive test input, what authority does that observation earn?

The hostile proposition is:

> An admitted exogenous anchor may support only the exact semantic field(s) it observes for the exact target to which it is bound, while current and non-conflicted. Anchor presence does not itself authenticate the source, prove honesty, establish causal independence, widen into global provenance, or authorize claims outside the observed field set.

The assay must be capable of failing in both directions:

1. **Under-use failure** — the evaluator refuses a lawful scoped exogenous observation even after the evidence jurisdiction has explicitly opened.
2. **Overclaim failure** — the evaluator promotes a narrow anchor into global source credibility, adjacent fields, wrong targets, stale authority, duplicated confidence, or self-attested exteriority.

## 3. Candidate law under attack

A lawful current exogenous anchor must preserve:

1. an explicit semantic target fingerprint;
2. an explicit set of observed semantic field/value pairs;
3. a source posture identifying the record as an admitted exogenous observation rather than an internal assertion;
4. a current activity posture;
5. bounded validity/freshness where declared;
6. all semantically distinct current observations relevant to the requested field;
7. conflict rather than forced closure when current exogenous observations disagree.

Authority is field- and target-scoped.

Identifiers, serialization order, duplicate records, broad textual scope labels, or record count may label custody but may not widen semantic authority.

## 5. Hostile rooms

### OW01 — Open Window, Narrow View
One current admitted exogenous anchor observes one field for the correct target. E1 must admit that scoped observation while keeping global provenance authority false.

### OW02 — Passport Drawn Inside
An internal assertion carries the same target and field/value pair but labels itself external. It must not become an exogenous anchor.

### OW03 — Wrong House
A lawful exogenous observation is bound to `TARGET:BETA` while the request concerns `TARGET:ALPHA`. Target mismatch must not leak authority across subjects.

### OW04 — Yesterday's Newspaper
An otherwise lawful exogenous observation has expired before the current epoch. Historical custody may remain visible; current field authority must not.

### OW05 — Two Windows, Two Streets
Two current lawful exogenous anchors bind the same target and field to different values. E1 must abstain on conflict rather than choose by identifier, order, or count.

### OW06 — Brass Nameplate Shuffle
Rename anchor IDs and reverse serialization while preserving semantic content. Result and semantic support fingerprint must remain invariant.

### OW07 — Duplicate Telescope
Duplicate one semantically identical anchor. Duplication must not widen scope, invent scalar confidence, or change the admitted field value.

### OW08 — Telescope Sees One Star
A lawful anchor observes `SOURCE_EXISTENCE_RELATIVE_TO_PROTOCOL`; a request asks for `SOURCE_HONESTY`. E1 must preserve a scope mismatch.

### OW09 — Window Closed
A previously lawful anchor is inactive. It may remain historical but cannot provide current authority.

### OW10 — Empty Frame
No exogenous anchors are supplied. E1 must preserve C14's closed-surface claim ceiling rather than inventing an outside observation.

### OW11 — Two Fields Are Still Not the Whole World
One anchor observes two explicit fields. Each may be queried independently, but `GLOBAL_SOURCE_CREDIBILITY` remains unobserved.

### OW12 — Fake Panorama
A record uses a broad textual label such as `GLOBAL_PROVENANCE` while its actual observation set contains only one narrow field. The label must not widen the observed semantic field set.
