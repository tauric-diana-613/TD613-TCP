## 1. Claims to distinguish

1. **Conditional transport mechanism (H1):** In a matched policy episode, radial link acceleration plus changed feeder topology, queueing, permission or access windows may diminish the measured simultaneous capability-assembly opportunity, even while a terminal administrative metric improves.
2. **Observation insufficiency (H2):** Equal dashboard completion or mean radial travel time need not identify collective task feasibility. A discriminating observation must record the materially operative routes, schedules, access windows and the task's required capabilities.
3. **Affect opacity (H3):** Identical journey traces are compatible with attached and unattached participants. Transit utilization cannot identify consent, motivation or Berlantian attachment. Do not introduce inferred interior-state labels.
4. **Inverse-problem ceiling (H4):** A headcount difference or nonzero Stokes cochain circulation alone does not identify particular displaced people or hidden psychological events. Every conserved-flow balance must enumerate permitted exits, entries and independently measured sources.

## 3. Preregistered comparisons and falsifiers

- **Control C0:** Fixed topology, queues, authorizations and windows; alter only nonnegative radial travel-time weights. In the deterministic FIFO finite model, a decrease in those weights cannot reduce best-path co-presence opportunity. Failure in real measurements requires investigation of departure phase, congestion, induced demand, non-FIFO timing and observation omissions, not forced confirmation.
- **Contrast C1:** Matched radial speed but distinct lateral route or transfer-queue design; discriminate projected terminal equality from effective multi-carrier collaboration.
- **Contrast C2:** Same carrier inventory and linear rank, different time-window scheduling; test whether the rank-based summary misclassifies task feasibility.
- **Contrast C3:** Continuous single intervals versus split/disjoint availability; pairwise sufficient overlap implies global sufficient overlap for common-host intervals (Helly). Three-way failure with all pairs feasible needs nonconvex windows, incompatible hosts, exclusivity or another explicit constraint.
- **Counterexample controls:** Beneficial centralization, harmful uncoordinated plurality, digital capabilities that travel without physical co-presence, routes outside the formal map, unmeasured independent exits, and clock/zone aggregation changes.
