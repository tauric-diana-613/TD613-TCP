### The Four Foundational Theses

1. **The Witness and the Sentence are Distinct Historical Objects:**
   An utterance transforms through transmission stages without altering the originating historical event. An institution alters the evidentiary significance of a statement through editorial abstraction.
2. **Intervention-Relative Distinguishability (Yoon Y-01):**
   Two histories that are completely indistinguishable under passive reading ($R(h_1) = R(h_2)$) become rigorously separated under an authorized ancestry query intervention ($R(T_a h_1) \ne R(T_a h_2)$).

4. **Component Reversal versus Total-System Reversal (Yoon Y-03):**
   Restoring an altered text to its original wording inverts only the representational component ($V \to -V$). It cannot reverse the intervening physical and institutional consequences ($H_0 \ne 0$).
