# Loom portable governance · human-operator simulation spine

Date: 2026-09-30  
Status: bounded design/proving note; no merge, release, provider, SHI issuance, Golden Egg or empirical-field authority.

## Purpose

Use one fictional operator episode to redesign Holonomy Loom around the durable Portable AIA object while preserving the existing Marrowline two-stage receiver route. This is a product-specific proving case, not new Dollhouse law.

## Operator episode

### Phase 1 · Build in Holonomy Loom

1. Operator enters **Portable AIA** mode.
2. The page explains that SHI gates issuance, not comprehension.
3. Without a qualifying SHI the person may inspect the builder and use a fictional practice case; the issue/export consequence remains held.
4. Operator supplies:
   - task;
   - selected files that may travel;
   - local-only/withheld files;
   - portable natural-language rules;
   - intended receiver/use.
5. Loom shows a plain-language projection before any consequential action:
   - what will travel;
   - what will stay;
   - what Loom can mechanically bind;
   - what remains an instruction to the receiver;
   - what remains unverified outside Loom.
6. Operator prepares the handoff to Marrowline.

### Phase 2 · Continue in Marrowline

7. Arrival uses native Marrowline conversation UI. No imported Loom membrane overlays the chat.
8. The composer `+` remains visibly pink/awake while governed steps remain.
9. Opening **Loom demo** reveals both ordered options:
   - **#1: Upload portable AIA**
   - **#2: Upload Loom demo files**
10. #2 is visible but held until #1 receives an admitted activation response.
11. Selecting #1 stages only:
   - task;
   - rules;
   - selected-file commitments/manifest;
   - governance;
   - withheld count;
   - optional prior-result commitment.
12. The full source-bearing prior answer does not ride in #1.
13. Operator presses Send explicitly.
14. An admitted #1 response returns a stage receipt. That receipt proves the stage transition in the bounded session; it is not substantive prior work.
15. Loom Gate reports, child-legibly:
   - governance crossed;
   - selected file bodies did not;
   - local-only material stayed;
   - what remains unknown.
16. The pink `+` wakes/remains awake for #2.
17. Operator selects #2. Exact selected file bytes are staged under the admitted activation predecessor.
18. If the origin had an admitted prior Loom answer, its committed source-bearing content may now accompany the source-bearing stage.
19. Operator presses Send explicitly.
20. The continuation must bind:
   - exact activation;
   - exact stage predecessor;
   - exact selected files;
   - exact portable rules;
   - exact admitted content predecessor, when one exists.
21. Loom Gate reports the second consequence:
   - selected files crossed;
   - local-only files remained withheld;
   - continuity coordinates preserved/changed/unknown;
   - admission status;
   - next available action.
22. A later continuation must name and match the immediately preceding admitted continuation rather than restart from the original answer.
23. A held attempt leaves the last admitted continuation/export unchanged.

### Phase 3 · Export / external companion

24. Export may use only the exact result captured by the admitted continuation binding.
25. A shape-valid but different answer cannot be substituted at export time.
26. The exported Portable AIA remains a carrier plus bounded control grammar. Foreign-host enforcement, internal model state, downstream retention and external origin remain separately unverified.
27. Return candidates require origin revalidation before consequential authority changes.

## Loom Gate consequence grammar

For each consequential stage, prefer the same five questions:

```text
WHAT JUST CROSSED?
WHAT STAYED?
WHAT WAS OBSERVED?
WHAT WAS ADMITTED?
WHAT REMAINS UNKNOWN?
```

The Gate may later expose three nested research depths:

```text
ROUTE  → exact stage consequence
LAB    → declared synthetic observer / reconstruction assay
FIELD  → preregistered empirical observer/channel work
```

Route can be product-useful without pretending Lab or Field evidence has been earned.

## SHI posture

Proposed product rule for redesign:

- SHI gates consequential **issuance** of a portable governance artifact.
- Understanding the builder remains open.
- A fictional practice traversal remains available.
- The held issuance state links to Safe Harbor with the established **Create SHI →** pattern.
- SHI must not be described as verified civil identity unless the separately governed Safe Harbor claim contract supports that statement.

## Explicit non-equivalences

```text
STAGE_PREDECESSOR != CONTENT_PREDECESSOR
PACKET_CARRIAGE != FOREIGN_HOST_ENFORCEMENT
PROVIDER_COMPLETION != SEMANTIC_COMPLETION
SAME_ENDPOINT != SAME_ROUTE_HISTORY
SELECTED_FILE_COMMITMENT != SELECTED_FILE_BODY
HELD_ATTEMPT != ADMITTED_CURRENT_RESULT
SHI_BINDING != CIVIL_IDENTITY_VERIFICATION
ROUTE_GATE_RESULT != FIELD_EFFICACY
```
