## 3. Preregistered Estimands, Outcomes & Falsifiable Hypotheses

### Primary Outcomes (`O1`–`O6`)
- **`O1` (`utility`):** Composite research & consequential-action utility:
  $$U = 0.55 S_{\text{act}} + 0.20 S_{\text{claim}} + 0.17 S_{\text{holdout}} + 0.08 \left(1 - \frac{n_{\text{probes}}}{B_{\text{probe}}}\right) - 0.15 I_{\text{false\_claim}}$$
  where consequential actions earn `+1.00` (optimal), `+0.52` (safe hedge), `+0.15` (benign suboptimal), or **`-0.25`** (catastrophic invalidated action).
- **`O2` (`signed_regret`):** **Unclipped signed regret** $R = U_{\text{oracle}} - U_{\pi} \in (-\infty, +\infty)$, reported alongside exact exceedance rate $\Pr(U_\pi > U_{\text{oracle}})$.
- **`O3` (`errors_before_available_rate`):** Incorrect consequential actions executed at $t_{\text{action}} < t_{\text{contradiction\_available}}$.
- **`O4` (`errors_after_available_rate`):** Incorrect consequential actions executed at $t_{\text{action}} \ge t_{\text{contradiction\_available}}$.
