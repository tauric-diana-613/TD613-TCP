## Abstract

Act I of *The Palace of Unborn Futures* varied how much historical memory an investigator inherited. ## 1. Intellectual Lineage and Explicit Epistemic Boundaries

Computational models of belief revision routinely treat error as a deficit of data: if two agents reach different conclusions, one must have observed fewer samples or suffered higher sensor noise. Yet scientific and institutional history repeatedly exhibits a sharper phenomenon — **the simultaneous presence of corrective evidence inside the archive and consequential error inside action**. To investigate that gap without flattening social theory into toy arithmetic, this study places two distinct intellectual lineages in sustained contrapuntal tension (`DIALECTICAL_TENSION`) across an explicit architecture of procedural clearance (`CARO_INSTITUTIONAL_RECONSTRUCTION`).

### 1.1 Paul Virilio: Dromology, Operational Cadence, and the Constitutive Accident

In *Speed and Politics: An Essay on Dromology* (1977/1986), Paul Virilio argues that historical power is organized not merely through territory or accumulation, but through velocity — the differential speed of transport, signal transmission, and decision execution relative to an adversary or changing environment. In *The Original Accident* (2005/2007), Virilio formulates his sharpest diagnostic proposition: **every technical system invents its own specific, substance-bound accident**. To invent the ship is to invent the shipwreck; to invent high-speed rail is to invent derailment; to invent instantaneous networked execution is to invent the systemic flash crash. The accident is not an external perturbation visiting an otherwise neutral apparatus; it is the negative imprint of the capability itself when operational speed ($\nu$) outstrips the horizon of perception and correction ($\tau$).

Applied to scientific inheritance, Virilio's thesis predicts **plural, policy-specific accidents** rather than a single failure of conservatism:
- An institution built to preserve hard-won founding truth against noise (**Monumentalism**) invents the accident of *canonical petrification* when environmental hazard $h$ accelerates past its evidentiary threshold.
- An institution built on cumulative empirical tallying without regime memory (**Evidentialism**) invents the accident of *actuarial hysteresis* — trailing gradual drift (`FAM_C`) and whipsawing out of phase under cyclic return (`FAM_E`).
- An institution built on rapid recency discounting and relentless diagnostic testing (**Provisionalism**) invents the accident of *skeptical exhaustion* — burning finite investigative capacity on early noise (`ADV_02`, `ADV_08`) and chasing corrupted artifact bursts (`ADV_04`).
- An institution built on context-indexed reliability (**Temporal Custodianship**) invents the accident of *spurious context decoupling* when observable administrative or environmental proxies shift without an underlying causal change (`ADV_11`).

### 1.2 Patricia Hill Collins, Kristie Dotson, and Katherine McKittrick: Credibility, Resilience, and Epistemic Geography

Dromology alone cannot explain *why* an admitted observation sits inert inside an archive while older claims continue governing action. Here scholarship in Black feminist epistemology and social epistemology supplies indispensable analytical distinctions — which must be engaged through each scholar's specific argument rather than collapsed into a generic scalar parameter of "bias":

1. **Patricia Hill Collins (*Black Feminist Thought*, 1990/2000, Ch. 11, "Black Feminist Epistemology"):** Collins demonstrates that knowledge validation processes are never reducible to formal logic applied to raw facts; every epistemological framework specifies institutional criteria for who qualifies as a credible knower and what counts as adequate justification. Critiquing Eurocentric positivist validation rules that demand disembodied distance and defer to credentialed hierarchy, Collins articulates four alternative dimensions of Black feminist epistemology: *lived experience as a criterion of meaning*, *the use of dialogue in assessing knowledge claims*, *the ethics of caring*, and *the ethic of personal accountability*. Crucially for our problem, Collins shows how dominant validation apparatuses dismiss anomalous testimony from marginalized positions not by refuting its empirical content, but by classifying its bearers as lacking institutional standing before substantive evaluation begins.
2. **Kristie Dotson (*Hypatia* 2011; *Social Epistemology* 2014):** Dotson distinguishes multiple mechanisms that must never be conflated. In "Tracking Epistemic Violence, Tracking Practices of Silencing" (2011), she separates *testimonial quieting* — where an audience fails to identify a speaker as a knower due to controlling images or institutional hierarchy — from *testimonial smothering*, where a speaker truncates or withholds their own risky testimony because the audience exhibits demonstrable testimonial incompetence regarding its intelligible uptake. Subsequently, in "Conceptualizing Epistemic Oppression" (2014), Dotson differentiates first-order and second-order epistemic exclusions (remediable by fairer application of existing rules or by expanding shared hermeneutical resources) from **third-order epistemic oppression**: a *structural resilience* of the epistemological system itself, wherein established validation parameters absorb enormous contradictory pressure without revising the governing framework.
3. **Katherine McKittrick (*Demonic Grounds*, 2006; *Dear Science and Other Stories*, 2021):** Drawing on Sylvia Wynter's analysis of the "demonic" in physics and cognition (a nondeterministic vantage point outside a closed governing code), McKittrick critiques the illusion of "transparent space" — the assumption that archives, maps, and classification grids simply mirror where facts naturally reside. In *Dear Science and Other Stories* (2021), McKittrick examines how citation practices, footnotes, and quantitative descriptions can either function as *pedigree* — endlessly re-inscribing founding authority and treating oppressive arrangements as mathematical inevitabilities — or as *relational methodology* that holds open how we know.

## 5. Synthesis: Answering the Governing Commission Question

> **When does inherited knowledge cease to guide inquiry and instead become the mechanism of its error — and what forms of epistemic governance allow a civilization to revise its deepest commitments in time?**

Within the bounded mathematical universe of Act II, inherited knowledge ceases to guide inquiry and becomes the active engine of consequential error when **three structural conditions** coincide or compound:

1. **Authority Is Attached to Institutional Pedigree Rather Than Scoped Calibration (`w_standing` $\gg$ `r_calibration`):**
