System  1  (S1)  Muscle  /  Organs  The  operational  units  that  perform  the  primary

risk  appetite.  It  acts  as  the  final  arbiter,  resolving  inherent  tensions  between  the  efficiency  demands  of  S3  and  the  adaptation  demands  of  S4

Deterministic  Control:  PID  Architecture  and  Signal  
Processing
 

Translating  the  principles  of  homeostatic  negative  feedback  and  requisite  variety  into  the  realm

First

.
 
The  PID  controller  calculates  an  ongoing  error  value   as  the  difference  between  a  desired  
setpoint   and  a  measured  process  variable  
26
.  It  achieves  regulation  by  applying  a  correction  based  on  three  distinct  mathematical  terms:  
1.  Proportional  ( ):  Produces  a  control  action  directly  proportional  to  the  current  magnitude  of  the  error

.  While  it  provides  the  bulk  of  the  restorative  force,  relying  solely

error
 
(offset)
 
because

to
 
generate
 
any
 
restorative
 
output
26
.
 
2.  Integral  ( ):  Integrates  the  error  over  time,  calculating  the  accumulated  historical  offset
 26
.  By  responding  to  the  duration  of  the  error,  this  term  drives  the  steady-state  
error
 
to
 
zero,
 
ensuring
 
the
 
system
 
eventually
 
reaches
 
the
 
exact
 
setpoint
26
.
 
However,

term
 
can
 
induce
 
overshoot,
 
and

prevent
 
the
 
setpoint
 
from
 
being
 
reached,
 
it
 
suffers

integral
 
windup—accumulating

massive

signal
 
that
 
leads
 
to
 
violent
 
oscillations
 
upon
 
release
26
.
 
3.  Derivative  ( ):  Calculates  the  rate  of  change  (velocity)  of  the  error
 26
.  This  term  introduces  predictive  control,  applying  a  dampening  effect  that  opposes  rapid  changes.  
By
 
anticipating
 
future
 
error,
 
the
 
derivative
 
term
 
minimizes
 
overshoot,
 
increases
 
system
 
stability,
 
and
 
reduces
 
overall
 
settling
 
time
