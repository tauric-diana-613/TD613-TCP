## Status-machine separation

The namespace reuse also hides distinct status predicates.

Open Seed:

```text
Derivative Spec
= structural modification / refinement / automated optimization of Seed
```

OSSL v1.1:

```text
valid lineage certification
= candidate fork falls within stylometric/homeostatic drift bounds
```

These are not interchangeable.

```text
STRUCTURAL / LEGAL DERIVATIVE STATUS
!=
STYLOMETRIC LINEAGE CERTIFICATION STATUS
```

A genuine structural derivative may become stylometrically distant. A close stylistic artifact may be lineage-compatible without proving the causal derivation event.

The v1.1 Work contains no exact `license`, `legal`, or `ShareAlike` token, so a v1.1 `severed` lineage-certification result cannot currently be interpreted as cancellation of Open Seed's contractual derivative obligations.
