## 1. External academic confrontation frame

The sieve used conservative source-domain ceilings:

- Fisher information is ordinarily defined through derivatives of the log likelihood with respect to model parameters; data-coordinate score gradients are a different object family.
- Lindblad dynamics concern open quantum systems and system-environment coupling under Markovian/open-system assumptions.
- Belavkin quantum filtering concerns a posteriori states under specified continuous/nondemolition quantum measurements.
- Orthogonal Procrustes is an established method for aligning representation/embedding spaces.
- The Kuramoto model is an established phase-oscillator model of synchronization across physical, biological, chemical, and social systems.
- EEG/fMRI hyperscanning can measure inter-individual neural coordination; that measurement alone supplies no spacetime-generation observable.
- Yule's K is a computational text-constancy/repetition statistic and can be related to a second-order Rényi-entropy approximation; it is not by itself a hallucination or truth detector.
- VSM has a substantial organizational-cybernetics literature and empirical applications, but organizational evidence does not automatically validate a particular LLM runtime implementation.
- Nonequilibrium thermodynamics and dynamical-landscape approaches are legitimate biological frameworks; their use does not by itself establish a semantic or ontological claim.
- Analogue-gravity literature explicitly distinguishes an effective acoustic metric from the underlying physical spacetime metric.
- Contemporary scholarship on organizational closure continues to distinguish formal self-reference from causal physical realization; a 2026 genealogy explicitly notes that Varela's self-indication formalism lacked the causal physical grounding required for natural-system realization.

Representative confrontation sources include Stanford Stats 311 Fisher-information notes; Manzano (2019) and Nathan & Rudner (2020) on Lindblad/open systems; Belavkin's quantum filtering papers; ACL literature using orthogonal Procrustes for representation alignment; Acebrón et al. (2005) on Kuramoto; Liu et al.
