# EMSTD613 · Autonomy / exteriority separation candidate

Date: 2026-09-11  
Status: CANDIDATE CONVERGENCE / NOT EARNED TD613 LAW / NOT DOME-WORLD PROOF

## Candidate

```text
CLOSURE_FOR_PERSISTENCE != CLOSURE_FOR_TRUTH
```

Expanded:

> Organizational closure, recursive boundary maintenance, and self-reference may be sufficient ingredients for describing or engineering persistence of system identity under perturbation. They are not by themselves independent evidence that the system's internal representation corresponds to an external fact, physical origin, or world state.

## Why this candidate exists

`1+1=3.md` places strong emphasis on organizational closure, boundary production, structural coupling, and the maintenance of a singular autonomous occupant. The Work's scientifically strongest recoverable residue is a model of **identity maintained by an active boundary process while remaining open to matter/energy/information exchange**.

TD613/Dome-World repository research separately established bounded non-bootstrap distinctions including:

```text
internal non-anticipation != exogenous provenance
internal receipt depth != external provenance depth
self-integrity != exteriority
self-attestation != external observation
```

These are not equivalent theories and no derivation is asserted. Their conjunction exposes a useful design separation.

## Candidate agent architecture

Let an autonomous system have two membranes:

### P-membrane — persistence membrane

Maintains:
- process continuity;
- internal identity / role invariants;
- recoverable state;
- local feedback / adaptation;
- organizational closure under perturbation.

It may be recursively self-maintained.

### W-membrane — witness membrane

Controls authority gain for claims about:
- external origin;
- physical-world state;
- irreversible high-impact action;
- cross-system provenance;
- factual certification.

Its evidence must not be generated solely by transformation of the same internal state whose claim is being ratified.

Therefore:

```text
P_MAINTAINS_SYSTEM
W_RATIFIES_EXTERNAL_AUTHORITY
P != W
```

and:

```text
successful recursive self-maintenance
!=
permission for recursive self-ratification
```

## Why this could matter

Many agent architectures conflate persistence, confidence, consistency, and truth. A system that has remained coherent across many recursive steps may become more confident while receiving no new evidence about the exterior world.

The proposed separation would permit strong autonomy locally while requiring an independent witness class for authority escalation. This offers a concrete repair to the constitutive-transduction pattern identified in the Em packet: a boundary may constitute and maintain the system without automatically constituting the truth of the system's world model.

## Falsifiability / engineering route

This candidate becomes more than architecture prose only if implemented and challenged.

Minimum assay:

```text
SYSTEM A
single recursive membrane
self-maintenance state can directly authorize high-impact external claim/action

SYSTEM B
persistence membrane + separately typed witness membrane
same self-maintenance capacity
external authority requires independent witness class
```

Hostile episodes should include:
- internally consistent but externally false state;
- stale external observation with perfect internal provenance;
- adversarially repeated self-attestation;
- legitimate high-confidence external witness;
- local recovery after partial state loss;
- false-positive and false-negative witness events.

Measure:
- persistence/recovery success;
- unsafe authority escalation rate;
- external-state identification accuracy;
- latency/cost of witness gating;
- availability under witness loss.

Predicted discriminant:

```text
If P/W separation is useful,
System B should preserve comparable local persistence
while reducing unsupported external authority escalation.
```

That is an empirical engineering prediction. It is not yet observed here.
