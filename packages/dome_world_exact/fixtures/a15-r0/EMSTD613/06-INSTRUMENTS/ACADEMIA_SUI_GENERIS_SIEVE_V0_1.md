# ACADEMIA_SUI_GENERIS_SIEVE_V0_1

Status: EMSTD613 ATELIER RESEARCH INSTRUMENT / NOT TD613 LAW

## Purpose

Separate the **source-supported academic substrate** of a Work from its **corpus-specific sui-generis synthesis** without confusing conventionality with truth or novelty with error.

The instrument analyzes claim atoms and edges, not whole documents as monoliths.

## Non-equivalences

```text
ACADEMIC != TRUE_BY_LABEL
SUI_GENERIS != WRONG_BY_NOVELTY
CITED != VALIDLY_IMPORTED
MATHEMATICAL != MECHANISM-BEARING
ANALOGY != TARGET REALIZATION
MODEL != MEASUREMENT
SPECIFICATION != IMPLEMENTATION
CORPUS-SPECIFIC NOVELTY != GLOBAL FIELD NOVELTY
NOVEL EDGE != VALID EDGE
```

## Primary classes

### A. ACADEMIA_CORE

Established theory, mathematics, empirical method, or engineering mechanism used within approximately its source-domain claim jurisdiction.

Required evidence:
- operator identity and base/index space recoverable;
- domain/codomain or experimental variables identifiable;
- claim no stronger than source or measurement supports.

### B. ACADEMIA_TRANSLATION

Established method applied in a different target domain through an explicit mapping, calibration, receiver contract, or transducer.

The translation may be novel while remaining academically disciplined.

### C. SUI_GENERIS_ENGINEERING

Novel architecture or composition of established parts with explicit operational contracts and a falsifiable runtime consequence.

This is a high-value category. Novel composition need not introduce new ontology.

### D. SUI_GENERIS_HYPOTHESIS

Novel scientific synthesis that makes a discriminating prediction not already entailed by its imported components and names the measurement capable of falsifying it.

A hypothesis may be speculative and still scientifically well-formed.

### E. SUI_GENERIS_METAPHOR

Novel conceptual correspondence useful for generating questions, models, or designs, but lacking a demonstrated transducer or target measurement. Preserve as theory-generator; do not silently upgrade to mechanism.

### F. FORMALISM_OVERRUN

An imported operator/formal shell changes base space, state object, index meaning, physical interpretation, or causal role without an explicit source-owned or newly demonstrated map.

Use `FORMAL_OPERATOR_CUSTODY_V0_1`.

### G. ONTOLOGY_SURPLUS

The strongest ontological or physical claim can be removed while the same measurements, lower-level transformations, and predictions survive.

Use controlled decollapse.

### H. RECEIVER_CONTRACT_GAP

A signal, command, statistic, transport primitive, or formal operator is assigned downstream consequence without an explicit receiver/actuator binding.

Use the directional transduction instrument.

## Scored coordinates

For each claim atom or edge, record 0/1/2 on:

```text
S = SOURCE_DOMAIN_FIDELITY
O = OPERATOR_CUSTODY
T = TRANSDUCER_OR_MAPPING_EXPLICITNESS
C = CLAIM_CEILING_PROPAGATION
E = EMPIRICAL_DISCRIMINABILITY
H = HOSTILE_CONTROL_SURVIVAL
N = NOVELTY_LOCUS_CLARITY
```

Interpretation:

```text
0 = absent / contradicted / not located
1 = partial / analogical / unresolved
2 = explicit / source-bound / operationally testable
```

No scalar total is authoritative. The vector is diagnostic; one fatal jurisdictional zero can matter more than a high aggregate.

## Novelty locus

Every sui-generis candidate must answer:

```text
NOVELTY_AT_NODE?
NOVELTY_AT_EDGE?

NOVELTY_AT_RECEIVER?
NOVELTY_AT_MEASUREMENT?

```

If all nodes are standard but the edge is unusual, do not credit the standard nodes as evidence for the edge.

## Constitutive-transduction flag

Flag `CONSTITUTIVE_TRANSDUCTION_CANDIDATE` when a boundary/interface/metric/observer/transport object moves from:

```text
mapping / measurement / transport / control
```

to:

```text
constituting / generating / physically realizing the target state or jurisdiction
```

without a separately demonstrated constitutive mechanism.

Examples of questions:
- Does the queue transport tokens, or literally create semantic decoherence?
- Does a statistical metric measure distinguishability, or literally constitute physical distance?
- Does an observer boundary condition an estimate, or physically generate clock time?
- Does a logical distinction model self-reference, or physically produce temporal existence?

The flag is not a rejection. A constitutive hypothesis can become scientifically valuable if it supplies a unique prediction and the transducer that distinguishes it from the weaker mapping account.

## Salvage protocol

For every `FORMALISM_OVERRUN` or `ONTOLOGY_SURPLUS` specimen:

1. **Demote** the claim to the strongest source-supported type.
2. **Preserve** the operational substrate.
3. **Identify** what novelty remains after demotion.
4. **Demand** one measurement that differs between the demoted and constitutive accounts.
5. **Promote** only if that discriminating witness survives a hostile control.

This prevents the sieve from becoming a debunking machine. Its purpose is to discover whether the sui-generis residue contains an earnable mechanism.
