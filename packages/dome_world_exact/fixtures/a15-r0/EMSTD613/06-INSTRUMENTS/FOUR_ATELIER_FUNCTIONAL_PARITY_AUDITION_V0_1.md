## 0. Meaning of “functional parity”

- **Same available operations and output contracts**, subject to lawful corpus/evidence limits.
- **Not** identical raw-corpus ontology, formulas, security clearance, automatic authorization, interpretation, independent witness, or claim ceiling.
- Each operation has a machine-auditable state: IMPLEMENTED_AND_TESTED, BOUNDED_IMPLEMENTATION, DOCUMENTED_ONLY, NOT_GENERATED, NOT_APPLICABLE_WITH_JUSTIFICATION, or HELD.
- A shared envelope may record results and differences only after preserving the native source references, negative states, graph projection, and evidence class.
- “Function exists” differs from “positive result earned.” A blurb, pun, metaphor, or deliberately absurd academic flourish can support a **research hypothesis**, even when it cannot establish a unique mathematical mechanism or scientific novelty.
- Scientific claims remain bounded by actual observations; invention is permitted and recorded separately from evidence.

## 4. Assaying theories reconstructed from fragmentary, playful or nonstandard research

A blurb, absurdist premise, playful mathematical metaphor, or visually suggestive artifact may be **candidate evidence of a research question or an intellectual motif**. The protocol should not dismiss it because of register. Nor should it infer a theorem from a poetic surface.

The proposed sequence:

- Preserve exact available source unit, author attribution as supplied, locator and negative states; if title/blurb-only, mark FULL_TEXT_NOT_OBSERVED.
- Identify declared objects, variables, transformations, relations, claimed phenomenon, boundary assumptions, and whether symbols have semantic definitions. Unknowns remain typed unknowns.
- Perform a **generative hypothesis pass** with competing reconstructions, including the null that the text only names a metaphor. This may be artistic or theoretical design.
- Preregister discriminants: what additional source, code, observation, or formal definition would distinguish candidates?
- Compare the candidate to ordinary prior work and similar conventional mechanisms, allowing convergence without presumed transmission.
- Stress-test by deleting nonessential metaphor, withdrawing weak source edges, splitting supported Work identities, permuting labels, and holding out known relationships.
- Require a unique reconstructed operator or discriminating prediction **only for strong structural / novelty claims**. Lack of such evidence yields a valuable bounded hypothesis and a next test, not a failure verdict.
- For proposed unified theories, distinguish an imaginative synthesis, coherent formal definition, an established mathematical result, and independently supported empirical theory. Their admission gates differ.
- Respect existing known theorem families and original authorship; a creative analogy may be useful even where novelty is defeated.

**Candidate emergence test (research protocol, not an asserted result):** at least two independently source-grounded parent structures, an explicit composition rule, a resultant property or failure mode not supplied by either alone, alternatives, and discriminating disconfirmation. Semantic resemblance or recurrent motifs alone cannot establish a unified theory.
