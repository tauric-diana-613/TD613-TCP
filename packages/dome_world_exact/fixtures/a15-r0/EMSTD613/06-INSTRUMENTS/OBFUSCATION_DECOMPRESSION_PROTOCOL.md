# Obfuscation Decompression Protocol v1

This instrument handles works described by the human provenance source as academically oblique or intentionally obfuscated.

`AUTHOR_REPORTED_OBFUSCATION` is a provenance statement. It does not by itself prove concealment, adversarial intent, or a hidden mechanism.

## Decompression stack

For each dense passage:

```text
S0 raw passage
S1 syntactic skeleton
S2 defined terms and symbols
S3 explicit propositions
S4 cited upstream dependencies
S5 implicit assumptions required for coherence
S6 operational/testable kernel
S7 alternative plain-language kernels
S8 predictions and falsifiers
S9 relation candidates to other works
```

Every S6–S9 statement must point backward to the exact S0 span and identify which transformations were interpretive.

## Kernel rule

A “true kernel” earns analytical standing when it explains more source structure with fewer unsupported assumptions than rival decompressions and yields at least one discriminating consequence.

A memorable paraphrase alone is not a kernel.

## Ambiguity preservation

When two decompressions remain compatible with the source, preserve both. Do not select the more TD613-like reading merely because it creates a prettier lineage graph.

## Deliberate-obfuscation test

Where the corpus itself supports intentional obliqueness, seek source-witnessed indicators such as explicit author statements, controlled terminology shifts, systematic indirection, or version comparisons. Even then:

```text
intentional indirection != malicious concealment
```

## Forensic AI layer

When a passage concerns AI-mediated workspaces or platform behavior, separate:

- user-observed behavior;
- reproducible interaction trace;
- inferred middleware/routing behavior;
- inferred model behavior;
- documented product behavior;
- causal attribution to a named platform or actor.

The assay may map anomalies without prematurely naming their cause.
