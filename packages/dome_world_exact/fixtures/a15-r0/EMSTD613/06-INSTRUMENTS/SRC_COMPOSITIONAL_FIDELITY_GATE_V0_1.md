## 1. Correction to the v0.1 flat capability table

The initial parity document enumerated ten independent verbs. That captures useful interface similarities but **does not test the actual composition of SRC's research operators**. In SRC, a work can be cross-platform, multiply captured, expressed at different resolutions, recategorized over time, governed by non-chronological authority, and reachable by non-equivalent navigation routes.

A shallow Em adapter can produce four labels, a typed adjacency list, and a source withdrawal result without being SRC-equivalent in any substantive sense.

At least three independent parity questions must be answered:

- **Contract parity:** can each Atelier expose the same operations, typed finding packets, claim ceilings, terminal missingness states, and human closure protocol?
- **Compositional parity:** can each Atelier preserve meaning through compositions of intake → identity resolution → version authority → query-conditioned projection → typed relation traversal → adversarial ablation → bounded reconstruction, including non-commuting orders and representation loss?
- **Domain applicability / evidence parity:** which of those operations is meaningfully instantiated by its own corpus? A source-domain lacking cross-platform manifestations cannot manufacture them. It must retain the capability and emit NOT_APPLICABLE_OR_UNOBSERVED with source-grounded justification. Execution on SRC specimens through a lawful isolated, read-only assay is necessary for high-fidelity cross-Atelier comparison.

No three-way parity is established until independent tests distinguish these. **“Same list of operations” ≠ “same compositional behavior” ≠ “same corpus affordances.”**

## 2. Source-derived SRC complexity inventory

SRC's Phase-2 projection declares 1,408 platform artifacts, 1,650 captures, 1,183 content-addressed blobs, and separate acquisition/rights outcomes. ### SRC-B. Query-conditioned canonical representation sufficiency

SRC's \`QUERY_CONDITIONED_CANONICAL_REPRESENTATION_SUFFICIENCY_REST_2026-09-14.md\` records five questions:
\`Q_place\`, \`Q_type\`, \`Q_history\`, \`Q_bridge\`, \`Q_reader\`.

Different canonical surfaces are sufficient/partial/insufficient for different questions. Canon Map, Canon + Field Architecture, Provenance Registry, CFIF, and Signature/Explore cannot substitute for each other, even when they overlap.

**Gate:** query × representation matrix; source-bound sufficient/partial/insufficient states; tests that a valid answer under one question does not automatically answer another. Parity cannot be inferred from having a keyword search engine.

### SRC-C. Several irreducible navigation functions

The public source provides at least: Canonical Index as governance root, Canon Map as formal topology, START HERE as experiential/diagnostic entry routing, Explore:X as local semantic address, and Versioning Map / Provenance Registry as diachronic lineage references.

**Gate:** ask the same work graph five distinct navigation questions; record different traversals and explicit authority scopes. A simple conceptual graph with interchangeable edges fails.

### SRC-D. Polycompilation rather than mere deduplication

The August 25 \`src-polycompilation-conservation\` receipt reconstructs conceptual invariants across Lexicon, Handbook, Canon Map, and Deployment Manual, with role differences (definition, public translation, placement, implementation). The Field Manual is a negative comparator rather than another synonym.

**Gate:** identify stable invariants, representation-role shifts, lost/introduced qualifiers and operational outputs; negative control: similar language but different architectural role. Distinguish source-declared relations from archive-formulated sequence. \`same words\` is not a conservation metric.

### SRC-E. Canonical identity is type-conditioned and diachronic

The September 14 identity rests show that operator identity may be preserved by functional/temporal skeleton and falsifier continuity even as domain variables change. Translation can preserve symbolic relations without empirical authority. Lineage can survive merger/reclassification without preserving distinct present field status. A shared signature can persist without giving its host works scientific identity.

**Gate:** test positive continuity and false twins for operator, translated artifact, historical lineage, and signature; preserve both split and merge identity alternatives.

### SRC-F. Relational composition refuses illicit endpoint promotion

SRC's September 14 typed composition rest permits genuine multi-step paths but forbids a guessed direct causal, authority, or identity edge from mere path existence. Provenance history and current canonical inclusion are independent.

**Gate:** positive multi-hop path with source-ref evidence; negative endpoint identity / empirical promotion. Require edge-specific witnesses for every stronger assertion.

### SRC-G. Publication chronology differs from epistemic dependency and authority

The public order of a manifest, companion paper, promised later work, and subsequent correction may differ from composition or dependence order. SRC separately records publication, revision, capture, asserted dependency, canonical current role, and retroactivity boundaries.

**Gate:** deliberately supply forward references and versioned corrections; verify preservation of conflicting clock coordinates without inventing drafting history or treating newest as controlling.

### SRC-H. Three-stratum reconstruction and tomography-shaped controls

The prospective assay separates formal/analytical, narrative/simulation, and governance/publishing strata. It specifies blinded \`Q(S)\` on seven subsets and matched-budget, topic-template, label-leakage and chronology controls. A triple gain is meaningful only if the full combination recovers a frozen relation better than every pairwise combination; even a positive result cannot establish physical tomography or unique latent-object inversion.

**Gate:** preregister relation targets and held-out criteria; compare every nonempty subset with hostile controls. Mark source-specific support accurately. Do not call this experiment already executed.

### SRC-I. Deferred destination resolution and semantic address

\`Explore:X\` routes to named-work, internal-concept, alias, sometimes later publication neighborhoods. Exact or lexical later matches are candidate observations, neither future-prediction nor evidence of intentional puzzle design.

**Gate:** compare later exact, pre-existing, lexical-only, unresolved cases under frozen time; prohibit promotion from address to DOI or private-message code.

### SRC-J. Signed authority and evidence-independence ancestry

Evidence multiplicity can differ from work and representation multiplicity. Cross-surface repetition and independent confirmation require separate ancestry accounting. Query results must preserve sealed epoch, evidence basis, licence/rights, authority status, and source classes.

**Gate:** duplicate manifestation vs independent witness; stale authority vs current authoritative record; withdrawal and restoration without unauthorized source mutation.
