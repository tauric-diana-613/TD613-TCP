- source-faithful;
- provenance-preserving;
- machine-navigable;
- topologically manipulable;
- anatomically assayable;
- motif-aware without motif/mechanism collapse;
- capable of convergence, divergence, and emergence analysis;
- cross-comparable against TD613, the SRC Atelier, retrieved conversation memory, external research corpora, and current scientific literature;
- resistant to turning speculation into evidence merely because the speculation is interesting.
