This

By

as

of

and

a

framework

rooted

in

structural

and

It

situates

these

dynamics

within

broader

political

economies

of

food,

water,

and

labor,

arguing

that

cancer

reflects

not

only

cellular

drift

but

the

systematic

design

and

neglect

of

modern

infrastructures.

In

a

where

in

five

in

Canada

in

(Canadian

2025),

the

are

are

statistical

destiny

for

a

large

share

of

the

population. Introduction Cancer has long been framed as a genetic malfunction. Yet emerging research reveals a deeper rupture:

mitochondrial

This

from

(Pavlova

Nutrition,

These

Metabolic

not

only

a

biological

process,

but

a

social

and

infrastructural

one. Ultra ‑ Processed Food and Metabolic Drift Modern diets are dominated by ultra ‑ processed foods engineered for shelf life rather than survival.

These

products

are

high

in

refined

sugars,

additives,

and

synthetic

fats,

and

low

in

and

A

10%

in

ultra ‑ processed

food

is

in

overall

cancer

risk

(Fiolet

et

al.,

2018).

These

foods

disrupt

insulin

regulation,

increase

inflammation,

and

starve

mitochondria

of

essential

cofactors,

accelerating

cellular

drift.

But ultra ‑ processed dominance is not simply the outcome of individual preference. It is produced by: • corporate consolidation • price architectures that make nutrient ‑ dense foods unaffordable • zoning that concentrates fast food in low ‑ income areas • subsidies that favor commodity crops over whole foods What appears as “choice” is often a constrained decision landscape engineered by economic and

logistical

infrastructures. Water Contaminants and Cellular Sabotage Water, often assumed to be neutral, can carry carcinogenic agents. PFAS, arsenic, nitrates, and disinfection

byproducts

have

to

digestive,

endocrine,

and

cancers

(Post

et

2017;

Smith

et

2002).

These

interfere

with

repair,

oxidative

and

immune

(Kim

al.,

2019),

destabilizing

mitochondrial

integrity. Fluoridation adds another layer of complexity. In Canada and Ontario, fluoridation uses industrial

byproducts

regulated

national

and

protocols

(Health

Canada,

2024;

Ontario

Ministry

of

Health,

2023).

Large

(Public

Bhasha

2025),

exposure

systems. Impaired barrier function increases vulnerability to environmental exposures, including chemical penetration

through

(Vestergaard

al.,

2017).

This

reinforces

that

water

quality

is

not

a

consumer

choice

but

a

structural

obligation. Atopic March, Barrier Function, and Environmental Exposure While the atopic march itself is not considered a direct cause of cancer (Bantz et al., 2014), it reflects

a

chronic

inflammation,

These

Impaired

The connection is not causal but infrastructural: the same environments that produce early ‑ life allergic

In

same

underlying

conditions

rather

sequential

reinforcing

that

environmental

conditions

shape

biological

vulnerability

across

multiple

pathways. Chronic Stress and Tumor Promotion Stress is not just psychological—it is biochemical. Chronic stress suppresses immune surveillance

and

Elevated

of

structural

Micronutrients as Agents of Restoration Micronutrients play a critical role in maintaining mitochondrial health: • Vitamin D regulates cell growth and immune response (Feldman et al., 2014). • Selenium supports DNA repair and antioxidant defense (Rayman, 2012). • Vitamin C induces apoptosis in cancer cells at pharmacologic doses (Chen et al., 2005). • Vitamin E protects cellular membranes and modulates immunity (Traber & Atkinson, 2007).

Yet access to micronutrient ‑ rich foods is tightly coupled to income and geography. Low wages, high

is

not

a

but

an

infrastructural

outcome. Pathologized Infrastructure The patterns described in this essay reflect a broader phenomenon of pathologized infrastructure—systems

that

When

but

structural

drift. The “buried signal” is not simply metabolic; it is infrastructural. Public Health Failure Public health systems often misclassify environmental and nutritional exposures as matters of individual

choice

This

“personal

it

helped

normalize. Containment Protocol: From Individual Burden to Structural Accountability A containment protocol cannot rely on individual purchasing power. Reverse osmosis systems, organic

A structurally coherent containment framework requires: • Food reform: regulate ultra ‑ processed products, restructure subsidies, and support nutrient ‑ dense

systemic

conditions,

not

substitutes

for

structural
