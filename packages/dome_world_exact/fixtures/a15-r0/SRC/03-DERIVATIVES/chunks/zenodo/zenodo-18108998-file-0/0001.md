2. Pharmaceutical intervention: Prescribing antidepressants, antipsychotics, or stimulants to manage

symptoms. 3. Psychotherapy: Offering cognitive or behavioral strategies to cope with distress. While these tools can be life-saving, they often treat symptoms without addressing root causes. Many

The

It

What

missing?

What

systems

are

dysregulated? Micronutrients: The Brain’s Forgotten Fuel Micronutrients are essential for brain function, neurotransmitter synthesis, and emotional regulation.

Deficiencies

B6

(pyridoxal-5-phosphate),

(Faugere

al.,

2025). • PTSD: Trauma depletes nutrients through chronic stress and inflammation, worsening emotional

instability

(Baik,

2024). • Substance-induced psychosis: Drug use damages nutrient absorption and metabolism, leading to

anxiety,

(Lindsay

al.,

2024). In a cross-sectional study of over 1,000 psychiatric patients, vitamin D, B9, and B12 deficiencies were

2025). Case Example: B12 Injection Reverses Psychosis A 52-year-old man with no psychiatric history developed persecutory delusions and auditory hallucinations.
