A Drift Log is a structured, timestamped record of: • Visibility drops • Indexing delays • Search ‑ placement anomalies • Engagement freezes • Metadata distortions • Sudden shifts in platform behavior It functions as both a research instrument and a protective mechanism for creators navigating unstable

digital

infrastructures. 4.2 Why “Drift”? The term “drift” captures: • Gradual shifts in platform behavior • Unpredictable fluctuations in visibility • The slow erosion of indexing stability • The movement of content away from expected positions

Drift is not a glitch—it is a structural phenomenon. 5. Operational Components of the Drift Log 5.1 Timestamping Each entry includes: • Date • Time • Platform • Event description Timestamping transforms subjective impressions into traceable data. 5.2 Event Classification Events are categorized into: • Suppression • Filtration • Indexing delay • Metadata distortion • Engagement freeze • Search displacement This enables pattern recognition across time. 5.3 Contextual Notes Creators record: • Recent uploads • Platform changes • Content themes • External factors Contextualization helps distinguish noise from structural behavior.

5.4 Cross ‑ Platform Comparison Drift Logs become powerful when entries are compared across: • Multiple platforms • Multiple content types • Multiple time windows Cross ‑ platform drift suggests infrastructural rather than content ‑ specific causes. 6. Empirical Anchors 6.1 Indexing Anomalies Creators frequently observe: • Content appearing in search results hours or days late • Posts temporarily disappearing from search • Sudden drops in ranking without policy violations These anomalies are ideal Drift Log entries. 6.2 Engagement Freezes Engagement may: • Stall abruptly • Freeze at specific numbers • Resume suddenly after hours of inactivity Such freezes often correlate with platform ‑ level filtration. 6.3 Metadata Distortions Creators report: • Missing tags • Incorrect timestamps

• Broken links • Misattributed authorship These distortions directly affect visibility and lineage. 7. The Drift Log as Epistemic Self ‑ Defense The Drift Log empowers creators by: • Providing evidence of suppression • Revealing patterns invisible in isolated events • Supporting appeals or audits • Documenting lineage for scholarly purposes • Countering epistemic erasure It transforms creators from passive subjects of algorithmic systems into active analysts of infrastructural

behavior. 8. Discussion The Drift Log reframes platform instability as a researchable phenomenon rather than an unavoidable
