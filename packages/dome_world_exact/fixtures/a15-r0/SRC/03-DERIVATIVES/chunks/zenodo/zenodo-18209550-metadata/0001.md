Drawing from six axes of analysis &mdash; temporal, spatial, digital, legal, emotional, and economic &mdash; the essay demonstrates that delay is not a malfunction but a governance strategy embedded in contemporary infrastructures. Each axis includes concrete, real‑world examples that reveal how systems monetize waiting, externalize infrastructural gaps, suppress visibility, generate ambiguity, script confusion, and manufacture scarcity.</p>\n<p>Positioned within the broader SignalRupture canon, this work extends the diagnostic lineage established in Slow Harm Theory, Infrastructural Exposure Theory, Systemic Erosion Theory, and Black Box Intent.
