The Illusion of Orchestration The forty ‑ year pattern looks coordinated: • crisis • spectacle • emotional reset • institutional breathing room But consistency does not imply intention. The system behaves as if it were designed because the underlying conditions are stable and reproducible. Erosion produces need. Exposure shapes behavior. Substitution fills the void.

Symbolic monopolies emerge. No mastermind required. Just structure. 7. Conclusion: The Environment Always Wins The World Cup is not the cause. It is the symptom. The real phenomenon is the forty ‑ year erosion of communal infrastructure that makes synthetic rituals

necessary. A society deprived of natural rituals will always default to industrialized ones. The spectacle is not orchestrated. The environment is. And the environment always wins.
