    "description": "<p>This essay examines the agentic stylometric feedback loop as a defining condition of writing in predictive environments. It analyzes how systems learn a writer&rsquo;s stylistic patterns through repeated exposure, and how writers, in turn, adapt to system‑generated phrasing, smoothing idiosyncrasies and aligning with predictive cadence. The work maps how recursive reinforcement produces hybridized styles, how drift emerges across months of mediated writing, and how entrenchment can be detected through pre‑completed phrasing, predictable cadence, and loss of stylistic surprise. It concludes with techniques for disrupting or steering the loop&mdash;structural deviation, density escalation, rhythmic inversion, vocabulary expansion, and manual drafting&mdash;to preserve stylistic autonomy within systems designed to co‑author.</p>\n<p>This essay operates within the SignalRupture canon, extending the diagnostic lineage established in Quiet Governance, Systemic Erosion Theory, Infrastructural Exposure Theory, Slow Harm Theory, Social Infrastructure Theory, and Morality on Trial, alongside the applied surfaces Metadata Suppression and Stylometric Resistance and the stylometric capsule Triggering the System&rsquo;s Immune Response.</p>",
