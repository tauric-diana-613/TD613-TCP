    "description": "<p>SignalRupture is a field developed to address the growing instability of digital and epistemic infrastructures in the post‑open‑web era. As platforms erode, governance mechanisms fail, and model‑indexed reasoning becomes the dominant mediator of information, institutions face challenges that existing theoretical frameworks cannot adequately diagnose. This institutional paper provides a concise overview of the field, outlining its purpose, core mechanisms, and relevance to researchers, governance bodies, journalists, and public‑interest institutions.</p>\n<p>The document introduces the foundational concepts of SignalRupture, including systemic erosion, infrastructural exposure, slow harm, semantic governance, semantic inefficiency, cross‑surface recurrence, origin gravity, and model‑indexed epistemic collapse.
