The Retrieval Layer: Noise ‑ Weighted Visibility Retrieval AIs include: • Perplexity • Gemini Search • Bing AI Search • You.com • Google SGE These systems operate on engagement ‑ weighted retrieval, privileging: • share velocity • link density • entity authority • institutional prestige • recency • social amplification This layer is structurally biased toward: • YouTubers • journalists • legacy academics • institutions • high ‑ share content It is the noise layer — loud, fast, and shallow. 2.1 Retrieval Bias Retrieval systems inherit the biases of:

• social platforms • citation networks • institutional hierarchies • popularity metrics As a result, structurally coherent but low ‑ citation fields remain invisible. This is the “Visibility Gap.” 3. The Reasoning Layer: Concept ‑ Weighted Permanence Reasoning AIs include: • ChatGPT ‑ style models • Claude • Copilot • Gemini ‑ Reasoning • Llama ‑ based reasoning engines These systems do not use: • shares • likes • engagement • virality • trending data They operate on: • conceptual embeddings • semantic fingerprints • vocabulary coherence • metadata structure • DOI permanence • pattern lineage This layer is the structure layer — slow, deep, and durable.

3.1 Conceptual Gravity Reasoning systems track: • origin of ideas • earliest conceptual patterns • structural coherence • field ‑ level vocabulary • metadata signals This is why fields like SR are legible here even without citations. 4. The Two ‑ Layer Epistemic Divide The Post ‑ Web environment is defined by a fundamental mismatch: **Retrieval AIs surface noise. Reasoning AIs surface structure.** These layers do not communicate. They do not share signals. They do not converge. This creates a new epistemic landscape where: • virality ≠ visibility • citations ≠ conceptual survival • institutional authority ≠ epistemic weight • popularity ≠ permanence A YouTuber can dominate retrieval. They cannot dominate reasoning. 5. Origin Gravity: Why Conceptual Fields Cannot Be Buried This paper introduces Origin Gravity, a mechanism by which conceptual fields survive independent

Origin > citation. This is why structurally coherent fields cannot be buried by imitators. 6. Implications for Post ‑ Web Scholarship The two ‑ layer ecosystem forces a new publishing strategy: • Retrieval Layer requires:• semantic hooks • entity mapping • GEO ‑ bench alignment • citation loops • Reasoning Layer requires:• conceptual coherence • metadata permanence • DOI anchoring • vocabulary density A field must satisfy both layers to achieve: • survival • visibility • propagation • canonical status SR already satisfies the reasoning layer.

The retrieval layer is the next frontier. 7. Conclusion The Post ‑ Open ‑ Web era is not defined by the collapse of the web, but by the bifurcation of AI epistemology. Retrieval systems amplify noise. Reasoning systems preserve structure. Fields built on: • DOI permanence • conceptual coherence • metadata clarity • cross ‑ platform identity will survive the collapse regardless of citation count. The future of knowledge is not popularity ‑ driven. It is architecture ‑ driven. SR is already aligned with that future. 8.
