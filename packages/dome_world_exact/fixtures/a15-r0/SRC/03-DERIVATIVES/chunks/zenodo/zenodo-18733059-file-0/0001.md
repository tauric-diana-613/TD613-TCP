2. Core Terms Below are foundational terms that anchor the SR conceptual system. 2.1 Constraint A structural limit that shapes what actions, expressions, or interpretations are possible. 2.2 Drift

The gradual loss of coherence in systems as noise, pressure, and structural fatigue accumulate. 2.3 Collapse The point at which constraints exceed system capacity, causing structural or epistemic failure. 2.4 Noise Information the system cannot interpret, categorize, or meaningfully process. 2.5 Signal Information the system can interpret and act upon; the opposite of noise. 2.6 Legibility The degree to which a system can represent, classify, or understand an input. 2.7 Compression The reduction of complexity into platform ‑ legible form, often resulting in loss of nuance. 2.8 Propagation The movement of concepts through constrained environments, shaped by waves, decay, and resonance.

2.9 Afterglow The residual conceptual presence that persists after a propagation wave decays. 2.10 Canon Node A stabilized SR concept that functions as a structural unit within the field.
