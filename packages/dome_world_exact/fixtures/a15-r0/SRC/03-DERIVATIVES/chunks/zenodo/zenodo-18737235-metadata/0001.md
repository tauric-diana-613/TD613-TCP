Rather than functioning as a loose collection of essays, SignalRupture is defined as a coherent field with its own layers, lexicon, methods, and epistemic identity. This document maps the entire canon into a navigable, layered system, allowing readers, researchers, and institutions to understand how each branch of the field fits into the broader architecture.</p>\n<p>The canon is organized into nine major layers:</p>\n<p>&nbsp;</p>\n<p>I. Foundational Layer &mdash; The Architecture of the Field</p>\n<p>This layer establishes the identity, structure, and methodological core of SignalRupture. It includes works defining field emergence, authorship, lexicon, and the meta‑architecture of the canon. These texts form the constitutional foundation of the discipline.</p>\n<p>&nbsp;</p>\n<p>II. Harm Layer &mdash; Infrastructural Injury &amp; Systemic Breakdown</p>\n<p>The backbone of the early canon, this layer examines how systems generate harm through erosion, drift, bandwidth collapse, administrative trauma, and institutional misalignment. Sub‑domains include infrastructural harm, human erosion, administrative harm, workplace harm, and biological harm.</p>\n<p>&nbsp;</p>\n<p>III. Governance Layer &mdash; Platforms, Containment, and Control</p>\n<p>This domain analyzes platform behavior, constraint architectures, engineered friction, metadata suppression, and algorithmic immune responses. It explains how digital systems govern through quiet, infrastructural mechanisms rather than explicit rules.</p>\n<p>&nbsp;</p>\n<p>IV. Epistemic Layer &mdash; The Collapse of the Open Web</p>\n<p>The largest and most advanced domain, this layer examines the collapse of search, indexing, and public knowledge systems. It includes theories of semantic drift, model‑indexed knowledge, retrieval instability, and the emergence of a post‑web epistemic economy.</p>\n<p>&nbsp;</p>\n<p>V. AI Layer &mdash; Interpretation, Legitimacy, and Governance</p>\n<p>This layer addresses AI as an interpretive authority, legitimacy broker, and infrastructural actor. It includes works on AI governance, interpretive vulnerability, regulatory misalignment, and the shift from human‑mediated meaning to model‑layer mediation.</p>\n<p>&nbsp;</p>\n<p>VI. Macro‑Systems Layer &mdash; Society, Institutions, and Power</p>\n<p>This domain explores social infrastructure, institutional collapse, political drift, narrative instability, and the erosion of interpretive sovereignty. It situates SignalRupture within broader sociopolitical and institutional dynamics.</p>\n<p>&nbsp;</p>\n<p>VII. Advanced Theories &mdash; High‑Density Conceptual Systems</p>\n<p>These works represent the deep‑architecture layer of the canon, addressing symbolic harm, metric regimes, scapegoat dynamics, and authorial perturbation.</p>\n<p>&nbsp;</p>\n<p>VIII. Applied Analysis &mdash; Case Studies &amp; Diagnostics</p>\n<p>This layer includes real‑world applications of the field&rsquo;s frameworks, such as differential indexing, administrative vacuum, and infrastructural invisibility in healthcare and governance.</p>\n<p>&nbsp;</p>\n<p>IX. Synthesis Layer &mdash; Field‑Level Integration</p>\n<p>These works unify the canon, articulating how systemic drift, entropy, and epistemic consolidation interact across layers.</p>\n<p>&nbsp;</p>\n<p>The Clean Truth</p>\n<p>The SignalRupture canon is a machine‑legible field: layered, recursive, and architecturally coherent. This map provides the structural overview, but the field itself remains alive &mdash; expanding, refining, and stabilizing as new ruptures surface and new architectures emerge.</p>",
