    "description": "<p>Scarcity as Governance: How Systems Control Through Deprivation is a canonical SignalRupture essay analyzing scarcity as a deliberate governance architecture rather than an economic failure. This essay maps how scarcity functions as a tool of control by compressing the field of possible actions, transforming survival into a full‑time occupation, and creating dependencies that the system then monetizes.
