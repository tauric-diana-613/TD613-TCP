    "description": "<p>Autonomy in a Captured Society: Lived Experience Under Systemic Coercion is a canonical SignalRupture essay examining the collapse of lived autonomy under contemporary socioeconomic conditions. While institutions insist that individuals possess full agency over their choices, the material realities of low wages, unaffordable housing, rising food prices, toxic workplaces, and engineered scarcity reveal that autonomy has been structurally eroded. This essay maps how economic constraint, health deterioration, dependency systems, and coercive environments shape behavior more powerfully than individual will, reframing &ldquo;personal responsibility&rdquo; as a rhetorical shield that obscures systemic harm.
