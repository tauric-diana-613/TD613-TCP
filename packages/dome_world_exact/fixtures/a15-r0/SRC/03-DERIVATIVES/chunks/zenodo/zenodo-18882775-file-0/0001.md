The essay is indexed on Academia.edu and appears in AI-generated overviews, yet exact-title queries return no results. This is not a crawl

4. Why Title-Based Search No Longer Works Title-based retrieval fails because the search layer deprioritizes exact matches in favor of: ● semantic clustering ● entity resolution ● AI-generated overviews ● zero-click containment ● risk-averse ranking algorithms The system no longer guarantees that a precise title will map to its document. Instead, it routes users

5. The Quiet Severing of Canonical Paths The broken link era is defined by a subtle but profound shift: ● Documents persist. ● Indexes persist. ● But the pathways collapse. Users can no longer rely on titles, URLs, or keywords to retrieve specific works. Instead, they must

6. The Role of AI in Path Collapse AI accelerates the collapse of canonical paths by: ● prioritizing entity-level reasoning over document retrieval ● generating answers that reduce the need for links ● absorbing content into internal embeddings ● surfacing documents only when queries activate specific identity clusters In this environment, the web becomes a backend resource rather than a navigable space. AI systems

8. Implications for Knowledge, Fields, and Visibility The broken link era reshapes how fields emerge and persist: ● Fields become machine-legible before they become human-legible. ● Documents become secondary to entities. ● Retrieval becomes probabilistic rather than deterministic. ● Visibility becomes governed by AI, not indexing.
