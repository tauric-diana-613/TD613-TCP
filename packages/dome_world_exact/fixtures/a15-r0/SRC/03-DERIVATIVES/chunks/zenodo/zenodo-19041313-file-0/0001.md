2. Defining Recursive Collapse Recursive Collapse occurs when: ● system outputs are re-ingested as inputs ● degraded representations reinforce further degradation ● classification errors propagate across layers ● synthetic data contaminates training loops

● the system becomes self-referential The collapse is recursive because each cycle amplifies the previous cycle’s errors. 3. Why Recursive Collapse Happens 3.1 Synthetic Data Reinforcement Models trained on their own outputs lose grounding in human meaning. 3.2 Visibility-Layer Failure When metrics freeze or misreport, the system optimizes against incorrect signals. 3.3 Semantic Drift Embedding spaces shift, causing meaning to detach from original referents. 3.4 Dominant-Cluster Overfitting Systems over-index on a single conceptual attractor, distorting classification. 3.5 Infrastructural Drift Operational changes outpace interpretive layers, creating compounding misalignment. 4. Symptoms of Recursive Collapse Users observe: ● outputs that become increasingly incoherent ● hallucinations that reinforce themselves ● misclassifications that propagate across documents ● ranking systems that amplify their own errors ● analytics that diverge from real activity These symptoms indicate that the system is trapped in a self-reinforcing failure loop. 5. Case Study: AI Model Degradation As AI models increasingly train on synthetic data, they: ● amplify their own hallucinations

● reinforce incorrect associations ● degrade with each training cycle This is Recursive Collapse triggered by synthetic reinforcement. 6. Case Study: Recommendation Systems Recommendation engines often: ● recommend content based on previous recommendations ● reinforce narrow behavioral patterns ● collapse into self-referential loops This is Recursive Collapse triggered by feedback-loop optimization. 7. Relationship to Other SR Diagnostics 7.1 Epistemic Rupture Rupture breaks contact with reality; collapse amplifies the break. 7.2 Infrastructural Drift Drift creates the conditions for recursive failure. 7.3 Legibility Collapse Collapse hides the system’s degradation from users. 7.4 No-Bucket Condition Misclassification accelerates recursive instability. 8. The SR Diagnostic Model Recursive Collapse is identified through three indicators: 8.1 Self-Referential Outputs The system consumes its own degraded representations. 8.2 Compounding Error Amplification

Each cycle worsens the previous cycle’s distortions. 8.3 Divergence from External Reality Outputs become increasingly synthetic and incoherent. These indicators distinguish collapse from simple model error. 9. Theoretical Implications Recursive Collapse reveals: ● the fragility of feedback-driven systems ● the risks of synthetic data loops ● the structural consequences of epistemic rupture ● the inevitability of compounding misalignment ● the emergence of self-consuming infrastructures It demonstrates that systems do not merely drift away from reality—they accelerate their own degradation.
