2. Findings 2.1 Objectification and Media Effects Large ‑ scale reviews consistently show that media exposure contributes to measurable harm:

● Upward comparison on social media produces negative effects on body image, self ‑ esteem,

2.2 Sexualization and Aggression ‑ Linked Attitudes Studies examining sexualized media consistently report: ● Positive correlations between sexually explicit content and sexually aggressive behavior. ● Increased acceptance of coercive or harmful gender norms. ● Reinforcement of objectification as a normalized interpretive frame. 2.3 Incarceration Trends Global and national incarceration statistics show: ● Persistent or rising prison populations across multiple regions. ● Overcrowding as a widespread and long ‑ term structural condition. ● Stable or increasing pre ‑ trial detention rates. ● Policy reforms that do not produce sustained reductions in imprisonment.
