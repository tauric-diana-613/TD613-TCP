It argues that modern institutions increasingly rely on detestability&mdash;public contempt, frustration, and emotional repulsion&mdash;as a structural governance mechanism. Rather than interpreting institutional dysfunction as accidental or symptomatic of decline, the thesis positions detestability as a deliberate outcome of friction, opacity, bureaucratic drift, and digital mediation.
