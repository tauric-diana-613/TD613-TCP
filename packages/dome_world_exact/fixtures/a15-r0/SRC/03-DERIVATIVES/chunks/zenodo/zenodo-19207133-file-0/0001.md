II. The Meta ‑ Research Problem: How Poverty Studies Produce

False

Success

2.1 Short ‑ Window Measurement Many poverty studies measure outcomes immediately after interventions such as cash transfers or

These

Administrative

2.2 The Rebound Effect When benefits expire, poverty returns — often worse — due to rising costs of living. In Canada, 63%

2.3 Baseline Manipulation Studies often compare outcomes to crisis years (e.g., recessions, pandemics), making any improvement

This

2.4 Incentive ‑ Driven Blind Spots Institutions have incentives to demonstrate program success. Research on administrative burden

4.4 Administrative Burden Infrastructure Administrative burdens — learning costs, compliance costs, and psychological costs — systematically
