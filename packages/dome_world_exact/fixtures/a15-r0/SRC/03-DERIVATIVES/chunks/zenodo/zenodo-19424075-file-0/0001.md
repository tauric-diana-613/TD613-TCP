1. Introduction: Peer Review as an Institutional Experiment

Peer review is often presented as a timeless mechanism of scientific rigor, but historically it is a mid ‑ 20th ‑ century

2. The Assumptions Embedded in Peer Review Peer review rests on several untested assumptions: expert neutrality, disciplinary coherence, methodological
