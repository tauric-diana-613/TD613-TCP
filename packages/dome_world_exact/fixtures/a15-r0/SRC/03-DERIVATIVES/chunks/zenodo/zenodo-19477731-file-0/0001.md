3. The Intentionality Question: Feature or Emergent Property?

Public discourse often oscillates between two explanations: ● Institutions are incompetent, or ● Institutions are conspiring. Both interpretations miss the structural truth. Institutional blindness is not intentional. It is emergent. Governance systems were built for: ● slow information flows ● stable categories ● linear cause ‑ and ‑ effect ● predictable environments They now operate in conditions defined by: ● exponential dynamics ● category volatility ● real ‑ time exposure ● nonlinear propagation The mismatch between design assumptions and environmental conditions produces a patterned blindness

that

4. Why Blindness Appears Intentional Patterned failure invites narratives of conspiracy or incompetence, but both misread the structure.

Persistent directional failure is interpreted as intent. In structural systems, it is evidence of constraint.
