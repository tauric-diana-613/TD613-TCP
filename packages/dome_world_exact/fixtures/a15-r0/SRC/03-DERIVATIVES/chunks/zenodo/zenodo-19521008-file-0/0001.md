3. Visibility Constraint Visibility Constraint Institutions act on what they can see, not on what produces the problem.

Platform infrastructures are: ● opaque ● transnational ● technically complex Children are: ● visible ● countable ● legally addressable The policy target is therefore not the source of harm, but the only accessible surface of intervention.
