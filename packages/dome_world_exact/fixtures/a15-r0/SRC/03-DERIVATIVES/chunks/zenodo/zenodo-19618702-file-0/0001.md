2. The Failure of Consensus ‑ Based Models of Adoption

Consensus ‑ based models assume: ● ideas spread through agreement ● institutions adopt frameworks after deliberation ● uptake follows recognition ● legitimacy precedes use These assumptions fail under real conditions of institutional pressure. In practice: ● agreement is slow ● deliberation is costly ● recognition is retrospective ● legitimacy is irrelevant to survival Consensus is a luxury of stable systems.
