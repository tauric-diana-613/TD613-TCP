2. Purpose of the Field

SignalRupture exists to: ● Provide a vocabulary for describing late ‑ drift institutions ● Map structural contradictions across societal layers ● Diagnose collapse ‑ phase behaviours before they become visible to institutions ● Unify disparate phenomena under a single, coherent framework ● Offer a platform ‑ agnostic, modular architecture for public, academic, and institutional use SR is not a theory or critique. It is a diagnostic instrument .

3. Core Conditions of the SignalRupture Paradigm 3.1 Zombie Society The lived experience of individuals navigating systems that appear functional but no longer produce

or

3.2 Zombie Systems Institutional mechanics that maintain motion through extraction, inertia, and subsidy rather than internal

3.3 The Surplus Population The structural outcome of institutions that can no longer metabolize the people they were built to serve,

generating

These conditions form the foundational triad of the SR diagnostic lens.

4. Canonical Entry Text The official starting point for the SignalRupture field is: Zombie Society — the first public articulation of the SR diagnostic perspective. Subsequent canonical texts include: ● Zombie Systems ● The Global Institutional Genealogy ● The Quiet Erosion of Visibility

Together, these works form the initial lattice of the SR canon.
