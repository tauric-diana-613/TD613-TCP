12. SR as a System-Layer Paradigm SignalRupture is not reducible to isolated datasets or bounded empirical models. Its validity is therefore evaluated through: ● longitudinal recurrence, ● cross-domain convergence, ● structural coherence, ● and generative explanatory power.
