    "description": "<p>Empirical Application of SignalRupture: A System‑Level Analysis of Technology Companies &mdash; Field Paper IV applies the SignalRupture framework to large‑scale technology firms, including platform ecosystems and AI‑driven organizations. Unlike public institutions, technology companies operate with high adaptability, substantial resources, and rapid iteration cycles, making them a critical boundary test for SR&rsquo;s generality. The analysis evaluates six SR signatures&mdash;latency persistence, policy churn, buffer compression, automation under strain, downward regulation, and fragmentation&mdash;across a decade of extreme scaling complexity.
