A9. Reliability Protocol A9.1 Cross-Coder Procedure ● minimum 2 independent coders per CEU

● blind coding of F ′ and I ′ separately ● reconciliation only after scoring A9.2 Agreement Metrics ● Cohen’s κ (categorical dimensions) ● ICC (continuous indices: F ′ , I ′ , GTDI) A9.3 Acceptability Thresholds ● κ ≥ 0.70 = acceptable ● ICC ≥ 0.75 = acceptable ● below threshold → recoding required

A10. Dataset Construction Workﬂow 1. Extract CEUs from institutional systems 2. Split into F-source and I-source corpora 3. Apply coding instruments (A3, A4, A7) 4. Compute GTDI, SCR, AAI 5. Assign GA level 6. Aggregate into panel dataset Final dataset structure: | CEU | F ′ | I ′ | GTDI | SCR | AAI | GA | Outcome |

A11. Analytical Readiness Rules Dataset is valid for analysis only if:

● ≥ 80% of CEUs have complete F ′ and I ′ pairs ● inter-coder reliability thresholds met ● tokenization consistency veriﬁed across domains ● GA distribution spans at least 3 levels

A12. Primary Statistical Models (Operational Use) ● Multilevel regression (CEU nested in institution) ● Structural equation modeling (GA → SCR → AAI → GTDI) ● Cross-lagged panel models (time-series CEUs) ● Mediation analysis (SCR and AAI pathways)
