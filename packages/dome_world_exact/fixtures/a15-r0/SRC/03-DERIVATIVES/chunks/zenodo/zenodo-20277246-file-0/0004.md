Future work should explore: ● longitudinal institutional drift, ● predictive strain modelling, ● international comparative governance, ● automated compression analysis, ● large-scale narrative mapping.

legible

abstraction.

Institutions do not merely misunderstand people. They systematically transform behavioural complexity

into

theory-compatible

representations

optimized

● throughput, ● categorization, ● auditability, ● procedural stabilization, ● governance continuity. The empirical stability of SCR, AAI, and GTDI across unrelated systems suggests that institutional
