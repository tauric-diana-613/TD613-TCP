4. Methods 4.1 Study Design Cross-domain longitudinal analysis was conducted across: ● child welfare ● corrections ● disability adjudication ● emergency medicine ● addictions governance ● psychiatric systems ● school discipline Cases were selected based on procedural duration, oversight involvement, and documented reform

4.2 Lock-In Detection Criteria Governance systems were considered operationally locked-in when: ● GTDI failed to substantially decrease following contextual reintegration efforts, ● AAI remained stable despite reform interventions, ● procedural summaries displaced behavioural narratives, ● and oversight systems re-encoded contextual information into abstraction categories. 4.3 Data Sources The analysis incorporated: ● ombudsman reports, ● tribunal decisions, ● coroner inquests, ● audit reviews, ● oversight findings, ● and multi-stage institutional case files.

● risk classifications, ● compliance frameworks, ● diagnostic categories, ● procedural narratives, ● and audit-oriented summaries. 6.2 GTDI Rarely Decreased Following Escalation No observed cases demonstrated sustained large-scale GTDI reduction following major procedural

Temporary contextual reinsertion occasionally occurred but was typically reabsorbed into higher-altitude

6.3 AAI Remained Structurally Stable After Reform Most reforms altered procedural language rather than reducing abstraction density. Institutional abstraction structures therefore demonstrated strong stabilization tendencies. 6.4 Oversight Frequently Deepened Lock-In Oversight layers frequently generated the highest abstraction density and GTDI values due to secondary

procedural

encoding

processes.

9. Most large-scale governance systems resist these operational costs. 9.2 Lock-In Explains Reform Failure Institutional reforms frequently fail because they attempt contextual reintegration without altering the

Systems therefore become progressively unable to perceive excluded contextual complexity. 9.4 Lock-In Does Not Imply Absolute Impossibility The theorem does not claim that contextual reintegration is universally impossible. Instead, it proposes

10. Boundary Conditions Governance Altitude Lock-In weakens under conditions characterized by: ● extremely small governance scales, ● sustained relational continuity, ● low audit dependency, ● decentralized decision systems, ● community-embedded governance, ● and prolonged frontline behavioural engagement. However, lock-in tendencies remain structurally recurrent across observed domains.
