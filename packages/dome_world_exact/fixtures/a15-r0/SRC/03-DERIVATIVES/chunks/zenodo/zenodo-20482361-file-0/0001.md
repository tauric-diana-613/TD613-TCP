Academic publishing is often framed as a neutral mechanism for disseminating scientific knowledge.

4. Prestige Hierarchies and the Illusion of Quality Publishing operates on a prestige economy: high ‑ impact journals, citation metrics, institutional rankings, and editorial gatekeeping. Phys.org notes that even top journals are vulnerable to compromised review processes and inconsistent standards (Phys.org, 2025). Prestige does not correlate with rigor.
