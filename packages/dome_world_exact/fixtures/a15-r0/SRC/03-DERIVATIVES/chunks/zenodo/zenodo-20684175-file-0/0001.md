Introduction Institutions rarely collapse because of a single failure. They collapse because they cannot perceive

the

substrate

1. Institutions Mistake Efficiency for Stability When a new technology promises faster workflows or reduced friction, institutions interpret it as progress.

They

see

smoother

user

and

lower

What

Institutions

They
