6.8 — Limitations The SR framework carries several limitations: ● Evidence is indirect rather than causal , relying on structural inference. ● The transition period is still unfolding , limiting longitudinal validation. ● Regional variation remains significant, particularly across Africa, Latin America, and Southeast

Asia. ● Institutional adaptation may alter trajectories in ways SR cannot yet predict. ● Future testing is required to refine operational indicators and evaluate competing explanations.

SR should therefore be interpreted as a structural explanatory framework rather than a completed

empirical

law.
