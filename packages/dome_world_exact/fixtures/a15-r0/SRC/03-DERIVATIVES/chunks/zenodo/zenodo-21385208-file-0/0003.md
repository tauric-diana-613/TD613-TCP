4. Artificial Intelligence and the Changing Economics

of

Knowledge

Throughout much of intellectual history, the diffusion of new ideas has been constrained not only by

empirical

evidence

but

also

by

the

cognitive

demands

required

to

understand

Major

often

into

and

The

created

a

new

frequently

required

years

of

formal

education,

extensive

reading,

and

disciplinary

apprenticeship.

This relationship between complexity and knowledge transmission profoundly shaped the history

of

science

and

higher

education.

Universities

developed

specialized

disciplines

partly

because

no

individual

could

master

the

growing

volume

of

knowledge

across

all

fields.

As

specialization

increased

throughout

the

twentieth

century,

intellectual

progress

generated

a

paradox.

Researchers

produced

increasingly

sophisticated

explanations,

yet

communication

across

disciplines

became

progressively

more

difficult.

The

expansion

of

knowledge

was

accompanied

by

the

fragmentation

of

understanding.

Artificial intelligence introduces a structural change into this historical relationship. Contemporary

AI

systems

are

increasingly

summarizing

complex

comparing

competing

theories,

generating

conceptual

maps,

translating

technical

language

into

accessible

identifying

and

in

navigating

large

Although

AI

does

replace

reduces

cognitive

acquiring

and

comparing

From an educational perspective, this transformation may prove as significant as previous technological

revolutions

such

the

printing

or

widespread

Earlier

AI

enables

interact

ask

iterative

receive

personalized

and

compare

competing

real

Consequently,

theoretical

is

becoming

progressively

less

upon

memorization

increasingly

dependent

upon

conceptual

organization.

This development has important implications for paradigm formation. Historically, complexity itself

often

functioned

practical

theoretical

Dense

Universities

professional

The

upon

explanatory

to

AI alters this relationship by reducing the educational costs associated with complexity. Students are

now

to

examine

competing

theories

side

request

simplified

without

losing

As

This observation does not imply that all theories become equally valid or that AI eliminates the need

AI

SignalRupture

From this perspective, artificial intelligence represents more than a technological innovation; it constitutes

By
