Classical and contemporary sociology provide indispensable foundations for studying

●

Collapse Sociology begins with the first principle: Institutional and social capacity can deteriorate before failure becomes visible. Traditional sociology still treats collapse as crisis. Collapse

Collapse Sociology concentrates on conjunctions that are often distributed across

●

why identities fragment faster than they can be rebuilt ●

why meaning dissolves into metrics ●

why trust collapses even in stable economies ●

why governance becomes friction instead of force ●

why communities exist without cohesion ●

why narratives collapse into simulation ●

Collapse Sociology begins where these established literatures can be reorganized

It treats erosion as a measurable architecture rather than assuming it to be either normality

It treats drift as a candidate process of changing misalignment that must be measured

It treats fragmentation as a potentially structural condition while preserving the possibility

Collapse Sociology is the sociology of continuity under structural erosion: the study
