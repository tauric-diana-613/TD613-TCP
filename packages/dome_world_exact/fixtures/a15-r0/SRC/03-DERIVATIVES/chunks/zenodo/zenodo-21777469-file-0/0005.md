1. Why Energy Is a Foundational Dependency Sector Energy infrastructures determine: ● continuity of government operations ● stability of digital services ● functionality of telecommunications

● operation of logistics networks ● reliability of payment systems ● resilience of public ‑ sector institutions They are also: ● partially privatized ● regionally monopolized ● chokepoint ‑ dense ● substitution ‑ limited ● vulnerable to cascading failures Energy is therefore a meta ‑ dependency sector : every other infrastructural system depends on it.

Infrastructure Dependency (ID) Energy dependency is extremely high because: ● government cannot operate without power ● digital services collapse during outages ● emergency response relies on electrical continuity ● backup systems are partial, not comprehensive ID score: 0.92 Substitution Capacity (SC) Substitution capacity is low because: ● backup generators are limited ● fuel supply chains are vulnerable ● grid switching is slow and complex ● alternative energy sources are not scale ‑ ready SC score: 0.30 Regulatory Leverage (RL)
