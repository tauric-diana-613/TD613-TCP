2. The GAUID National Dashboard A national GAUID dashboard tracks six core metrics: Metric 1 — Infrastructure Dependency (ID*) Shows how reliant the government is on external operators. Metric 2 — Substitution Capacity (SC*) Shows how quickly the government can switch providers or infrastructures. Metric 3 — Regulatory Leverage (RL*) Shows how reliably operators comply with government direction. Metric 4 — Structural Power (SP) Shows how much veto power operators possess. Metric 5 — Government Agency Index (GAI) Shows baseline agency before structural power correction. Metric 6 — Effective Agency (GAI_eff) Shows real operational agency after structural power and thresholds. These metrics form the core GAUID reporting architecture .

3. Dashboard Visual Architecture The dashboard contains five visual modules: Module A — Dependency Map Shows dependency across telecom, cloud, logistics, payments, energy, transport.

Module B — Substitution Matrix Shows redundancy, switching time, fallback systems, multi ‑ provider routing. Module C — Leverage Gauge Shows compliance levels, enforcement speed, exemption frequency. Module D — Structural ‑ Power Heatmap Shows market concentration, lobbying intensity, standard ‑ setting influence. Module E — Agency Score Panel Shows GAI, GAI_eff, threshold proximity, crisis ‑ specific agency. These modules allow governments to see agency at a glance .

4. Annual GAUID Reporting Cycle GAUID reporting follows a structured annual cycle: Step 1 — Data Collection Regulators, procurement authorities, crisis agencies, and infrastructure operators submit data. Step 2 — Indicator Normalization All indicators are normalized to 0–1. Step 3 — Score Calculation ID*, SC*, RL*, SP, GAI, GAI_eff are computed. Step 4 — Threshold Assessment Check whether: ● ID > τ_ ID ● SC < τ_ SC ● SP > τ_ SP ● GAI_eff < τ_ GAI

Step 5 — Drift Detection Identify: ● substitution decay ● dependency spikes ● leverage weakening ● structural ‑ power growth Step 6 — Public Reporting Publish: ● dashboard ● annual report ● threshold alerts ● crisis cooperation summary This cycle ensures transparency and accountability.

5. Threshold Alerts GAUID includes automated alerts when thresholds are crossed: Alert 1 — Dependency Threshold Triggered when ID* > 0.60. Alert 2 — Substitution Threshold Triggered when SC* < 0.40. Alert 3 — Structural ‑ Power Threshold Triggered when SP > 0.45. Alert 4 — Agency Collapse Threshold Triggered when GAI_eff < 0.40. Threshold alerts allow governments to intervene before collapse.

Crisis Cooperation Code Documents whether operators: ● fully complied ● negotiated ● imposed conditions ● refused This module becomes the official record of crisis behaviour.

7. Public Transparency Instruments GAUID requires public transparency to prevent structural ‑ power growth. Instrument 1 — Annual GAUID Report Published by central agencies. Instrument 2 — Sectoral GAUID Reports Published by regulators.

Instrument 3 — Crisis Cooperation Reports Published after emergencies. Instrument 4 — Structural ‑ Power Audits Published annually by competition authorities.
