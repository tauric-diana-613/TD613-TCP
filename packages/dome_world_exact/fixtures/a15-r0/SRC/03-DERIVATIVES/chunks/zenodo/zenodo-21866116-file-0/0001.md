the

substrate

It

generated,

stabilized,

reproduced,

The field does not exist within the broader domain of policy science, public administration,

political

The field does not begin with the policy instrument. It begins before the instrument. It asks

The central SR question is: What structural substrate generates, constrains, delays, transfers, conceals, reproduces,

SR does not require permission from existing disciplinary boundaries to identify an object
