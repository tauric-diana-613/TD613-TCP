The argument for SR Anthropology does not require depicting classical anthropology

It focuses on: •

infrastructural subjectivity and dependency •

institutional hollowness measured as form-capacity divergence •

cross-national and cross-class variation rather than planetary sameness The resulting field is not the anthropology of culture’s disappearance. It is an anthropology
