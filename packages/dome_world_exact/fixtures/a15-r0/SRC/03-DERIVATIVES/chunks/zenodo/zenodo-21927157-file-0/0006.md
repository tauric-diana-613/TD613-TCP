Section XVI — Fieldwork Protocols in Collapse Environments

XVI.1 Entry and Presence Use flexible, multi-site entry while preserving clear consent and institutional boundaries.

Do

not

presume

volatility;

document

it.

XVI.2 Observing Erosion Track narratives, rituals, trust, meaning, and identity over time. Record strengthening

as

well

as

weakening.

XVI.3 Observing Drift

Map role changes, expectation changes, institutional rule changes, and planning

horizons.

Distinguish

imposed

instability

from

chosen

experimentation.

XVI.4 Observing Hollowness Trace processes from formal initiation to outcome. Compare policy claims, resources,

actual

capacity,

and

user

experience.

XVI.5 Observing Dependency Map essential functions, failure points, substitutes, workarounds, and recovery

times.

XVI.6 Observing Algorithmic Mediation Document interfaces, outputs, classifications, appeals, overrides, and known decision

rules.

Label

inferred

mechanisms

explicitly.

XVI.7 Data Resilience Use secure, encrypted, redundant storage appropriate to ethics approval and local

law.

Data

minimization

takes

priority

over

collecting

every

possible

digital

trace.
