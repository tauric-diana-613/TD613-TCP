29H.4 Negative Controls Across the Field

A valid SR-RIF test should actively seek cases where its predicted distortion is absent or reversed:

29I.3 The Linked Longitudinal Crown Test

The strongest SR-RIF design is a longitudinal panel indexed by research topic d and time t, linking

A minimal state vector is:

X_{d,t} = [R_{d,t}, Q_{d,t}, F_{d,t}, K_{d,t}, V_{d,t}, T_{d,t}]ᵀ,

where R is external reality, Q proposal/inquiry composition, F funding, K produced knowledge, V

A cross-lagged dynamic model can be written:

X_{d,t+1} = A X_{d,t} + Γ Z_{d,t} + ε_{ d,t},

where Z contains scientific opportunity, tractability, cost, workforce, technological change, and policy

The core recursive hypotheses are:

H1: F_{d,t} predicts Q_{d,t+1} after R_{d,t} and Z_{d,t} controls.

H2: K_{d,t} and V_{d,t} predict Q_{d,t+1} after R_{d,t} and Z_{d,t} controls.

H3: Q_{d,t} and F_{d,t} predict K_{d,t+1}.

H4: K_{d,t} and V_{d,t} predict T_{d,t+1} and/or F_{d,t+1} after external need controls.

H5: The reciprocal internal paths improve prediction beyond a model containing only external need,

opportunity,

and

established

The strongest evidence for a Closed Knowledge Loop would require reciprocal lagged paths across

multiple

29I.4 Falsification of Recursive Closure

The closed-loop claim weakens if:

• proposal composition tracks external reality but not lagged funding or knowledge after controls;

• targeted funding produces no reproducible topic movement; • prior knowledge visibility does not predict future priority formation; • apparent recursion disappears under fixed effects, exogenous shocks, or matched comparisons; • lagged internal variables add no out-of-sample predictive value; • observed feedback is better explained by scientific opportunity, disease burden, technological change,

workforce

constraints,

or

other

established

variables.

A fully empirical field must permit the possibility that the strongest recursive claim fails while stage-specific

selection

mechanisms

survive.
