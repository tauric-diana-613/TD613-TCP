2026 The economy can expand while the nation contracts. Abstract Collapse Economics is the study of economies that remain statistically productive while the

human,

institutional,

deteriorate.

Its

is

It

between

measured

economic

motion

and

the

condition

of

the

society

Gross

zombie

when

is

as

proof

that

a

country

is

healthy,

resilient,

or

recovered.

The

metric

remains

alive;

the

referent

has

died.

Preface: The Economy That Survives Its Society A country can report growth while its forests burn, its citizens are displaced, its hospitals absorb

preventable

illness,

its

infrastructure

accumulates

rehabilitation

needs,

and

its

lose

None

They

a

to

track

production

is

asked

to

certify

the

health

of

the

nation.

Collapse

Economics

begins

The decisive economic problem of the collapse era is not that activity stops. Activity often

intensifies.

Fire

insurance

administration,

reconstruction,

medical

treatment,

pharmaceutical

remediation,

all

generate

The

may

to

with

more

motion.

The

social

can

remain

injured.

Output

can

recover

before

people

do.

A

flow

can

rise

while

the

stock

beneath

it

erodes.

Collapse Economics names this condition without romanticizing catastrophe and without treating

every

adverse

trend

as

collapse.

It

studies

measurable

divergence:

when

the

headline

indicator

improves

while

independently

measured

loss,

insecurity,

depletion,

or

delayed

recovery

remains

elevated.

The

field

asks

what

was

lost,

who

absorbed

it,

which

response

entered

measured

production,

how

long

the

loss

persisted,

and

whether

national

aggregation

concealed

concentrated

damage.

I. Field Declaration 1. The object of study Collapse Economics studies the production of positive economic signals under conditions of

negative

societal

transformation.

Conventional

macroeconomics

already

distinguishes

flows

from

stocks,

nominal

from

real

quantities,

and

aggregate

output

from

welfare.

Environmental

economics,

ecological

economics,

wealth

accounting,

distributional

national

accounts,

public

health,

disaster

economics,

and

well-being

research

each

document parts of the problem. The field opening lies in integrating those fragments around

a

specific

phenomenon:

the

persistence

of

authoritative

economic

optimism

after

the

indicator

has

lost

contact

with

the

condition

it

is

publicly

used

to

represent.

Collapse in this field does not mean the immediate termination of markets or government. It

means

sustained

erosion

in

one

or

more

foundational

systems—human

health,

ecological

integrity,

material

infrastructure,

household

security,

institutional

capacity,

or

distributive

access—combined

with

weak

recognition,

delayed

repair,

or

compensatory

economic

activity

that

masks

the

deterioration

at

headline

scale.

2. Canonical definition Collapse Economics is the scientific study of divergence between measured economic activity

and

the

evolving

human,

material,

ecological,

and

institutional

capacity

of

a

society.

Its canonical proposition is: Measured expansion does not establish societal recovery when loss stocks remain

elevated,

recovery

is

incomplete,

or

compensatory

activity

is

counted

without

symmetric

recognition

of

the

damage

that

required

it.

3. What the field is not •

It is not the claim that GDP is useless. •

It is not the claim that every medical, rebuilding, or emergency expenditure is socially

undesirable.

•

It is not the claim that disasters reliably increase GDP. •

It is not a new label for recession analysis. •

It is not degrowth by another name, although it overlaps with debates about ecological

limits.

•

It is not a moral index disguised as national accounting. •

It is not an assertion that one composite number can replace all existing statistics.

The field instead separates three questions that public debate routinely fuses: How much market

production

occurred?

What

losses

accumulated?

How

much

of

the

affected

population,

environment,

and

asset

base

recovered?

II. Intellectual Foundations 4. GDP and the boundary of production GDP measures the value of final goods and services produced within an economy over a period.

Its

was

never

measure

the

condition

of

society.

Kuznets

warned

continues

to

production

from

balance-sheet

changes

and

other

changes

in

asset

volume

(Kuznets,

1934;

United

Nations

et

al.,

2009).

Catastrophic

destruction

can

outside

the

production

account

while

rebuilding

enters

production

and

investment.

This

asymmetry

is

an

accounting

consequence,

not

a

conspiracy.

The problem arises when institutional communication silently transforms a production statistic

into

a

civilizational

verdict.

A

quarter

of

positive

growth

becomes

evidence

that

“the

economy

is

strong,”

and

the

strength

of

the

economy

becomes

a

proxy

for

the

strength

of

the

society.

Collapse

Economics

that

semantic

the

indicator

6. Ecological economics and throughput Ecological economics rejects the treatment of the economy as a closed circular flow detached

energy

Production

The

Collapse

Collapse
