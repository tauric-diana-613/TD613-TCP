V. Hypotheses and Falsifiability 19. Core hypotheses Code Hypothesis Minimum test

H1 Positive GDP growth frequently coexists with rising loss in at least one independently measured domain. Panel of output and loss indicators with preregistered directionality. H2 National aggregation understates the social salience of spatially concentrated shocks. Regional/national exposure ratios and affected-population outcomes. H3 Compensatory sectors can expand during persistent harm without implying recovery. Sector output, harm incidence, and recovery-duration series. H4 Loss-adjusted measures improve prediction of later capacity or well-being beyond GDP alone. Out-of-sample model comparison. H5 Support withdrawal before human recovery increases insecurity or delays recovery. Policy discontinuity or quasi-experimental design. H6 Reliance on GDP alone produces diagnostic stagnation as loss complexity increases. Forecast-error trend and comparison with multidimensional models. H7 The share of burden-mediated production weakens GDP as a signal of net productive expansion. Composition estimates and out-of-sample signal comparison. H8 Human-buffer indicators rise before visible institutional failure in systems that permit compensation. Longitudinal timing of overtime, debt, unpaid labour, turnover and service failure. 20.
