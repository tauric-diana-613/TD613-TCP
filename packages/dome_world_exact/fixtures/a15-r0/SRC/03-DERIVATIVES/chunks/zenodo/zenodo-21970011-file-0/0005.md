XII. Domain Architecture of Collapse Economics 52. Collapse labour economics Collapse labour economics studies situations in which employment and participation remain

visible

while

the

security,

bargaining

power,

time

autonomy,

and

net

return

attached

to

work

deteriorate.

The

unit

of

analysis

is

not

simply

the

job

count.

It

is

the

employment

relation

as

a

carrier

of

capability.

A

labour

market

can

add

positions

while

increasing

involuntary

part-time

work,

schedule

volatility,

unpaid

search,

commuting

cost,

platform

dependence,

or

the

transfer

of

equipment

and

insurance

risk

to

workers.

These changes matter because headline employment can remain strong while the effective

livelihood

produced

by

each

hour

of

participation

weakens.

The core empirical distinction is between extensive and intensive participation. Extensive participation

asks

how

many

people

worked.

Intensive

participation

asks

how

much

secure

income,

control,

predictability,

and

benefit

coverage

their

work

produced

after

expenses

and

unpaid

time.

The

relevant

denominator

may

therefore

be

worker-hour,

active

participant,

or

household

rather

than

task

or

transaction.

This

is

particularly

important

in

platform

markets,

where

a

larger

pool

of

available

workers

can

increase

completed

transactions

without

increasing

earnings

per

worker.

Collapse labour economics predicts that participation becomes a zombie measure when entry

into

work

is

interpreted

as

restored

agency

despite

declining

net

earnings,

rising

volatility,

or

survival-driven

multiple

job

holding.

That

prediction

requires

transaction-level

or

worker-panel

data.

Personal

observation

can

reveal

the

mechanism,

but

only

hours,

expenses,

task

density,

algorithmic

allocation,

and

net

income

can

test

it.

53. Collapse housing economics Housing is simultaneously shelter, asset, debt relation, geographic access system, and precondition

for

psychological

continuity.

Conventional

indicators

often

separate

construction,

prices,

rents,

mortgage

and

homelessness.

Collapse

Economics

joins

them

through

effective

housing

the

capacity

to

occupy

adequate

without

sacrificing

food,

health,

mobility,

or

future

security.

A

rising

assets

may

measured

wealth

for

owners

while

intensifying

entry

barriers

and

rent

burdens

for

others.

The housing-loss stock includes displacement, overcrowding, forced moves, time spent searching,

administrative

barriers,

shelter-cost

burden,

deferred

maintenance,

and

the

loss

of

access

to

schools,

employment,

care,

and

community.

Construction

activity

can

support

GDP

while

affordability

and

security

deteriorate.

This

is

not

once

production,

distribution,

and

access

A Canadian test should link quarterly residential investment and construction output to renter

and

owner

housing

need,

shelter-cost

ratios,

eviction

filings,

homelessness-system

use,

vacancy

rates,

and

migration.

The

but

housing

expanded

for

exposed

54. Collapse health economics Health economics already studies costs, outcomes, resource allocation, and value. Collapse

health

focuses

on

divergence

between

health-sector

activity

and

population

health

under

More

may

necessary

The

The field therefore distinguishes protective health production, restorative health production,

chronic

and

Protective

production

prevents

Restorative

production

returns

capability.

Chronic

management

sustains

enduring

conditions.

Failure

absorption

occurs

when

the

health

system

treats

consequences

generated

elsewhere—unsafe

housing,

toxic

exposure,

poverty,

occupational

strain,

violence,

or

administrative

exclusion.

These

categories

can

overlap

and

should

be

measured

without

patients

or

providers.

The empirical objective is not to subtract healthcare from GDP. It is to pair health-sector output

with

avoidable

mortality,

disability,

unmet

need,

wait

time,

workforce

strain,

and

recovery.

A

health

system

is

not

succeeding

merely

because

it

is

busy,

and

it

is

not

failing

merely

because

spending

rises.

Success

requires

an

outcome

relation

between

resources,

access,

burden

reduction,

and

restored

capability.

response,

reconstruction,

and

energy

all

while

forest condition, biodiversity, air quality, soil, water, and climate stability can deteriorate. Because

many

are

the

can

through

that

without

equivalent

Collapse Economics adopts the environmental-economic accounting insight that ecosystems

are

assets

while

resisting

the

assumption

that

all

value

can

be

reduced

to

one

monetary

estimate.

Monetary

valuation

supports

comparability,

but

physical

indicators

remain

necessary

where

thresholds,

irreversibility,

uncertainty,

and

non-substitutability

dominate.

Hectares

burned,

species

abundance,

particulate

exposure,

carbon

emissions,

watershed

condition,

and

recovery

time

should

remain

visible

beside

any

dollar

The collapse-economic question is whether the economy is preserving the ecological conditions

required

for

future

capability.

If

GDP

growth

depends

on

rising

material

throughput

while

natural

wealth

per

capita

apparent

income

The

field

predicts

will

progress.

rebuilding,

debt

The

It

A collapse budget separates capability-building expenditure from loss prevention, emergency

The

The policy risk is austerity based on recovered GDP while unrecovered loss remains off-budget

When

The
