Proposition Support criterion Failure or weakening criterion

GDP diagnostic divergence Positive GDP classification coexists with preregistered deterioration beyond the expected false-alert rate. Divergence is rare, transient, or disappears using per-capita and distributional alternatives. Incremental validity Integrated models improve out-of-sample prediction of loss or recovery over conventional baselines. No reproducible predictive gain after complexity penalties and vintage-correct evaluation. Human Buffer sequence Buffer indicators lead later service failure or institutional strain at preregistered horizons. Effects are contemporaneous only, reverse in time, or vanish after demand and composition controls. Compensatory dependence A rising response share coincides with persistent loss and weak net-capacity growth. Response reliably reduces loss and raises durable capability proportionately. Model stagnation Validated supplementary indicators remain weakly integrated into consequential decisions. Broader measures are demonstrably integrated, timely, and decision-relevant.
