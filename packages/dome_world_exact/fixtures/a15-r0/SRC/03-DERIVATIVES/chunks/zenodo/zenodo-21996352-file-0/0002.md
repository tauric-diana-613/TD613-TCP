4. Historical Test I: Production Did Not Decline OECD data show global plastics production increasing from 234 Mt in 2000 to

460

Mt

2019.

Production Growth = (460 - 234) / 234 = 96.6% Production CAGR ≈ 3.62% per year Thus, total plastics production nearly doubled over nineteen years. If the relevant

environmental

material

5. Historical Test II: Waste Grew Faster Than Production Plastic waste increased from 156 Mt in 2000 to 353 Mt in 2019. Waste Growth = (353 - 156) / 156 = 126.3% Waste CAGR ≈ 4.39% per year Waste growth exceeded production growth over the interval: 126.3% > 96.6% This finding matters because a system can become more active in downstream
