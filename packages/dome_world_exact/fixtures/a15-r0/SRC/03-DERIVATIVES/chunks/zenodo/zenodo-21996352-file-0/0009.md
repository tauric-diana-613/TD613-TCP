65. What Is Now Established and What Remains Open Status Finding Empirically established Canada's national GHG emissions fell 10% from 2005 to 2024 while oil-and-gas emissions were 5.1% higher. Empirically established Electricity emissions fell 57%; coal generation contracted sharply under phase-out architecture. Empirically established Canadian crude-oil production more than doubled from 1990 to 2024 and reached a record 5.13 MMb/d in 2024. Empirically established TMX increased nameplate oil-pipeline capacity from about 300 to 890 kb/d and is federally owned. Empirically established Canada's 2025 ERP contains 164 measures and identifies 18 priority measures. Pilot classification 7 of the 18 priority measures are directly architectural under the conservative SR coding; 7 are output-oriented, 2 behavioural/practice, 2 mixed/enabling. Mechanism-consistent Electricity versus oil/gas is consistent with GAUID differences in substitution capacity, dependency, leverage, and incumbent structural power. Operationalized STI and ARI now have reproducible formulas and explicit coding rules. Physically grounded The substrate model is linked to cumulative CO2 and finite carbon budgets. Still unproven University curriculum architecture causes government policy architecture. Still requires calibration Numerical GAUID coefficients and full STI dependency/lock-in components across sectors. Still requires full audit Measure-level R_B/R_O/R_A coding of all 164 ERP measures.
