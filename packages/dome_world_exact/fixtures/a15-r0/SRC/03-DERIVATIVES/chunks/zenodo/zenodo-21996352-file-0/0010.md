70. The Distinctive SR Endpoint Climate Science: What physical change is occurring?

Mitigation Science: What interventions reduce emissions? Transition Studies: How do socio-technical systems change? Material-Flow Analysis: Where do physical materials flow? 73. Rebound and Realized Structural Gain A missing failure mode in the earlier manuscript was rebound: efficiency improvements

The

RE = (Expected Reduction - Realized Reduction) / Expected Reduction Realized Structural Gain = Efficiency Gain - Rebound - Throughput Growth RE is bounded and domain-specific. A value near zero indicates little rebound;

This operator prevents SR from assuming that efficiency is meaningless. Efficiency
