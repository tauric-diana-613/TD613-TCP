90. The Transition Cannot Be Evaluated at the Carbon Boundary Alone The preceding field architecture asks whether a harmful substrate contracted,

or

The

must

Electrification

can

while

dependence

grids,

batteries,

copper,

lithium,

nickel,

cobalt,

and

water.

The

correct

is

whether

electrification

is

combustion—it

generally

not—but

whether

the

transition

transfers

structural

dependency

and

environmental

pressure

into

new infrastructures that are insufficiently represented by carbon-only evaluation.

Substrate A → Substrate B is not automatically Problem → Solution Transition Test = Old-Substrate Contraction + Replacement-Substrate Burden 91. Substrate Dependency Transfer (SDT) SR defines Substrate Dependency Transfer as the movement of structural dependence

from

into

SDT_A→B = max(0, -Δ D_A) × max(0, Δ D_B) The multiplicative form is preferred for comparative work because dependency

D

[0,1]

SDT is not inherently a failure. A transition can rationally exchange a high-harm

The
