Core Definitions Construct Operational definition Cognitive infrastructure The institutional, material, relational, temporal, and technological conditions through which cognitive capacity is formed, exercised, expressed, delegated, or suppressed. Exposure-Silo Bias Bias or incompleteness produced when structurally co-occurring exposures are modeled separately and their joint developmental load is excluded. Compound Exposure Measurement Window A declared period in which relevant exposures, supports, transitions, and outcomes are aligned for systematic inference. Output–Capacity Gap The difference between performance achieved with assistance and capacity available without that assistance under comparable conditions. Developmental non-formation Failure to construct a skill because essential practice was delegated before the underlying representation became stable; distinct from loss of a previously acquired skill. Developmental Automation Contradiction The institutional demand that children use automation for learning or productivity while being judged as though cognition developed independently of that automation. Time–Money Care Constraint The limit on adult developmental support created by paid work, irregular schedules, commuting, chores, health, care obligations, and insufficient income. Digital care substitution Use of digital media to supply supervision, relief, entertainment, instruction, or emotional regulation when adult time or affordable alternatives are constrained. Temporal Fragmentation Architecture An environment that repeatedly redirects cognition before immersion, closure, or consolidation can occur. Cognitive Re-entry Tax The time, effort, error risk, and stress required to reconstruct an interrupted task.
