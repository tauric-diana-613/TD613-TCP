58. Hard Negative Controls The network edition requires deliberate counterexamples. •

High SC + low disruption because redundancy/substitution is strong. •

High GF + fast transformation because CCAP is strong. •

Low GF + poor transformation because resources, incentives, or politics dominate. •

Low SC + severe failure due to single-system internal fragility. These cases prevent SC/GF from absorbing all explanations of institutional or infrastructural

failure.
