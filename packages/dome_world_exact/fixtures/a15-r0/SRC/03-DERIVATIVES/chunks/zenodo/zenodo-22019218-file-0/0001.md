The canonical architecture, research programs, foundational papers, and field boundaries of SignalRupture

SignalRupture (SR)

2026

Preface

SignalRupture is a metatheoretical structural grammar organized through five causal pillars and a bounded set of research programs. Its papers do not stand as isolated declarations. They form a recursive architecture in which foundational grammar, causal separation, drift, propagation, infrastructure, governance, and human consequences remain connected without becoming conceptually interchangeable.

The Canon Map establishes the internal order of the field. It identifies which works define SR, which works provide methods and validation, which works establish durable research programs, and which works operate as domain applications or supporting mechanisms. The purpose of the map is not to reduce SR, but to preserve its explanatory range without allowing terminological expansion to fragment the architecture.

Canonical sequence: Pressure → Reflex → Drift → Collapse → Reorganization

The Function of the Canon

The SR canon distinguishes foundational theory from measurement, mechanism from application, and scientific research from public translation. A title containing the word “field” does not independently establish a scientific domain. Canonical standing arises from a distinct object of study, conceptual necessity, methodological contribution, measurable constructs, and the ability to generate or organize falsifiable inquiry.

Canon Admission Rule

No new top-level field enters the SR canon without a distinct object of study, at least one measurable construct, a method not reducible to an existing SR program, a reusable dataset or evidence base, falsifiable predictions, and a demonstrated reason that unification improves explanation, prediction, or intervention.

5. Canon Governance and Validation Sequence

1. Govern field formation: New ideas enter the architecture as core theory, mechanism, research program, application, operational tool, or hypothesis before receiving field status.

2. Maintain canonical lineage: Each canonical work retains a stable title, conceptual role, and relationship to the works it extends or supersedes.

3. Test canon nodes: The thirty active nodes remain open to conceptual correction, empirical challenge, and revision of variables, thresholds, and causal ordering.

4. Build shared datasets: Prioritize incident, policy-substrate, dependency, visibility-lag, human-compensation, and outcome registers.

5. Pre-register tests: State variables, competing explanations, exclusion rules, falsifiers, and success thresholds before final estimation.

6. Publish replication packages: Independent replication, including null findings, governs advancement from research program to validated field.
