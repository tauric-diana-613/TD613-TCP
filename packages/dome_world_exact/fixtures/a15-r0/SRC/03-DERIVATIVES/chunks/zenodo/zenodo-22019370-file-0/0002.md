within

the

assessment

population.

•

Confirmed: single-exposure narratives omit measured co-exposures and their

potential

11. Conclusion PISA 2022 demonstrates how an epistemic distortion can be built from accurate statistics.

Digital

distraction

is

real.

Bullying

is

real.

Food

insecurity

is

real.

Socioeconomic

inequality

is

real.

Each

can

be

estimated

separately,

yet

no

student

experiences

them

as

separate

journal

sections.

Exposure-Silo

Bias

when

analytic

separation

is

converted

into

ontological

separation—when

what

the

model

did

not

include

is

treated

as

absent

from

the

person.

The bounded result is already meaningful: between 30% and 58% of students experienced

at

least

one

of

three

widely

reported

exposures,

while

the

extent

of

co-exposure

remains

unidentified

from

published

marginals.

The

correct

to

incompatible

effects

It

to

preserve

the

boundary

the

person

explanation.
