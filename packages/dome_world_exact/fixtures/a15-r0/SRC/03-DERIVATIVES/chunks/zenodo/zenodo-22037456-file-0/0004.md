17. Limitations •

Case selection is purposive and may favour systems where restoration language fits naturally.

•

The cases use different countries, scales, data structures and time horizons. •

Several variables remain conceptual vectors rather than directly estimated scalars. •

The analysis is descriptive and comparative, not causal. •

Electricity evidence is substantially forecast-based. •

Housing SRR depends on CMHC's affordability benchmark and model assumptions. •

Healthcare compensation data do not by themselves prove unsustainability or causal harm.

•

Legacy IT evidence supports maintenance burden and slow modernization but does not yet

prove

the

full

Restoration

Trap

feedback

•

No prospective out-of-sample prediction has yet been completed. •

SRD overlaps materially with resilience, recovery engineering, capacity planning, operations

research,

public

administration

and

infrastructure

asset

management.
