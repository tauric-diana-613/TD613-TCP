20.8 Research Questions •

Does measurable variation in specialization precede variation in the relevant health

outcome?

•

This question cannot be resolved from prevalence alone; it requires pathway-specific

evidence.

•

For causal purposes, the decisive issue is how the proposed pathway behaves

over

time.

•

The empirical burden is therefore to identify what changed, when it changed,

and

through

mechanism.

•

For technology, which populations or settings show the longest delay between

•

What result concerning market structure would falsify, reverse, or materially narrow
