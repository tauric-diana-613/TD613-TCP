26.5 Automation

Automation cannot be inferred from the domain label alone. A defensible automation

A longitudinal view is essential because the relevant load may precede diagnosis

26.8 Research Questions •

Does measurable variation in algorithms precede variation in the relevant health

outcome?

•

The model gains precision by separating what the system records from what the

subject

actually

experiences.

•

For causal purposes, the decisive issue is how the proposed pathway behaves

over

time.

•

This question cannot be resolved from prevalence alone; it requires pathway-specific

evidence.

•

For decision support, which populations or settings show the longest delay between

material

change

and

reliable

recognition?

•

What result concerning bias would falsify, reverse, or materially narrow the proposed

infrastructural

interpretation?
