This produces a problem of systemic visibility. A failure may begin in one layer and become consequential only after crossing

A semantic distortion may affect decision-making. A decision-making distortion may alter resource allocation.

Resource allocation may increase infrastructural stress. Infrastructure stress may degrade telemetry. Telemetry degradation may delay detection. Delayed detection may permit propagation. Propagation may then alter the epistemic environment that originally produced

the

The observable failure therefore appears fragmented even when its causal structure

SR-CEIDF begins from this condition. It asks: How does instability move across epistemic, infrastructural, diagnostic, and

The field does not restrict analysis to the institutional surface. It traces observable failure backward through the coupled layers that generated, transmitted,

It studies the substrate through which systems remain capable of producing coherent
