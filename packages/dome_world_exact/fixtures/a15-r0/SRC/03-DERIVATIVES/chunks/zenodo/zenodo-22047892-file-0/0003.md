CHAPTER 6 — INCENTIVE–INFRASTRUCTURE RUPTURE The SR Law of Incentive–Infrastructure Rupture describes a recurring structural

Infrastructure → Incentive → Interest → Fear → Defense → Isolation →

that

CHAPTER 7 — VISIBILITY, KNOWLEDGE, AND THE POLICY BLIND SYSTEM

A policy system cannot respond to conditions it cannot reliably perceive. Yet visibility

Administrative

SR defines the Blind System as a system whose operational consequences exceed

The Blind System is not necessarily ignorant. It may possess enormous quantities

This produces a distinction between information abundance and causal visibility.

A system may know many facts and still not know what is causing the pattern.

This is why SR treats visibility as a structural variable. 7.1 Visibility Lag Visibility lag is the delay between structural change and reliable recognition. It

Δ V = t_recognition − t_change where t_change is the estimated time at which a structural condition changed and

t_recognition

This is one of the framework's central claims: delayed recognition can itself generate
