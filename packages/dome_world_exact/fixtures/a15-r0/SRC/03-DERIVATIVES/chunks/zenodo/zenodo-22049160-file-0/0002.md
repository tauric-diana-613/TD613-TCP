XXXI.10 Coercion, Constrained Choice, and the SR Coercive Stack

Collapse Psychology requires a distinction between overt force and structurally

The

The SR Law of Infrastructural Coercion operationalizes this structure through four

The

ICI = (D × M × CE) / VA ICI rises as dependency, mandatory navigation, and consequence exposure increase

The

It

Illustrative applications of this index to energy, banking, and mobility systems

Collapse
