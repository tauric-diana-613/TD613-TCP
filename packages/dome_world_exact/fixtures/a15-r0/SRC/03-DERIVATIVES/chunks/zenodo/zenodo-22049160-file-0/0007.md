The Canadian narrow coercion result is especially consequential because the broader

Collapse Psychology does not claim that psychology has ignored coercion, autonomy,

Psychology

The

This concern is not external to psychology. Psychology has a long-standing literature

and

Yanchar

Fragmented models → fragmented exposure architecture → incomplete

The empirical challenge posed by Collapse Psychology is therefore not that sociology

XXXVI.8 The Stagnation Hypothesis The stronger SR criticism is formulated as a falsifiable stagnation hypothesis: when

psychologically

relevant

phenomena

are

persistently

divided

across

subfields,

exposure

domains,

and

disciplinary

boundaries,

the

field

may

while

making

slower

toward

integrated

of

real-world

burden.

Local

refinement

and

publication

are

of

cumulative

structural

understanding.

Local progress ≠ integrated explanatory progress This manuscript does not establish that psychology as a whole is stagnant. Such

a

conclusion

would

require

bibliometric,

model-comparison,

replication, and cross-domain evidence beyond the present study. What the current

evidence

establishes

is

the

reason

to

test

the

hypothesis:

the

Collapse

Psychology

repeatedly

map

onto

yet

structural

relations

are

often

unavailable

in

same-sample

designs.

The

Canadian

sociological

study

demonstrates

that

a

broader

unit

of

can

that

dependency

amplifies

the

association

between

hardship

and

distress.

XXXVI.9 Quiet Governance After Empirical Confrontation Quiet Governance does not denote a hidden controller and does not require proof

of

It

a

in

and

can

be

dependency,

required

consequential

loss,

reduced

exit

conditions,

and

without

continuous

Absence of overt command ≠ absence of structural constraint The Canadian test provides one bounded empirical doorway into this proposition.

Financial

than

Independent
