1.3 What the field does not claim •

It does not claim that all contemporary social life is collapsing. •

It does not define every hardship as structural captivity. •

It does not treat employment, debt, unpaid care, overtime, algorithms, or bureaucracy

as

inherently

pathological.

•

It does not infer institutional intent from observed burden. •

It does not claim that every domain shares one causal mechanism. •

It does not treat the “Upside Down” metaphor as a scientific variable. •

It does not replace sociology, criminology, economics, psychology, governance studies,

or

epistemology.
