SSR Stage Forensic question Surface (S) What formal condition appears intact? Contradiction (K) What independently measured outcome conflicts with the surface description? Requirement (Q) What must actually be supplied for stable participation or function? Durable capacity (D) What endogenous resources are available to meet that requirement? Dependency (Dep) How dependent is the actor or system on the relevant infrastructure or institution? Viable alternatives (Alt) What credible exit, substitution, or alternative pathways exist? Burden transfer (BT) Where does the unresolved requirement move? Compensation (C_E) Who or what absorbs the difference, and through what mechanism? Exposure (X) What risk, cost, delay, deprivation, or strain is generated? Epistemic visibility (V_E) Is the burden visible in the metrics, classifications, and institutional representations used to govern the scene? Outcome (O) What human, social, institutional, economic, criminological, or epistemic consequence is observed? Recovery (R) What changes would close the gap, reduce compensation, or restore effective access? 4.1 Competing reconstructions R_0 = individual-only explanation R_1 = single-domain structural explanation R_2 = cross-domain Structural Inversion reconstruction Collapse Society earns independent value only when the cross-domain reconstruction
