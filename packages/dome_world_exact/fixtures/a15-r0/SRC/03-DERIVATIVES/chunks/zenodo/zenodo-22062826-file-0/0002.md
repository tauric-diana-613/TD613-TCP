Five Response Branches Branch Definition Misclassification Structural harm is interpreted primarily as personal moral failure, spiritual deficiency, deserved punishment, lack of faith, or individual irresponsibility. Stabilization Religion supplies meaning, ritual, belonging, prayer, endurance, mutual aid, and emotional regulation without necessarily changing the generating structure. Legitimation Religious language normalizes, authorizes, sanctifies, or naturalizes an existing hierarchy or institution. Mobilization Religious interpretation transforms suffering into collective obligation, protest, protection, mutual aid, or justice-seeking action. Restoration Religious mobilization contributes to measurable structural repair or reduction of compensatory burden. These branches are analytically separate.
