Do not use genocide or mass suffering as rhetorical decoration. •

Do not collapse Jewish, Muslim, Christian, Indigenous, Hindu, Buddhist, secular, or other traditions

Do not infer agency-centered misclassification without independently reconstructing structural

Do not erase religious institutions’ role as both harm producers and repair actors in different histories.

Do not call stabilization inferior to restoration when people need survival before repair. •
