Appendix A — Core Lexicon Term Definition Religious Structural Interpretation Translation of structural suffering into religious meaning, moral attribution, ritual response, community practice, or mobilization. Attribution-Structure Divergence Distance between religiously attributed cause and independently reconstructed generating structure. Theological Blindness Conditional attribution mismatch under high structural causation and high agency-centered attribution. Theological Recognition Religious recognition of structural causation and corresponding moral obligation. Religious Stabilization Infrastructure Religious meaning, ritual, community, and mutual aid that preserve functioning under low control. Religious Misclassification Attributing structurally produced suffering to personal moral or spiritual deficiency. Religious Legitimation Religious authorization or normalization of an existing hierarchy or institutional arrangement. Religious Structural Mobilization Religious interpretation that generates collective action aimed at structural repair. Post-Displacement Harm Propagation Continuation of war/persecution harm into migration, reception, language, credential, discrimination, economic, and intergenerational environments. Racialized Portability Advantage Institutional mobility advantage produced by unequal portability of passport, language, credential, race, class, nationality, and recognition.
