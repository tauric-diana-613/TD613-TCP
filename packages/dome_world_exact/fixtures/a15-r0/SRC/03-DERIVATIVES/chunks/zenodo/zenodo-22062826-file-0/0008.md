49. Coding Manual for Religious Interpretation Code Definition Divine causation Explicit claim that God/deity/divine will caused or permitted the event. Demonic/evil attribution Attribution to devil, evil, demonic force, or cosmic evil. Fate/providence Suffering interpreted as destiny, plan, providence, or fate without clear blame. Personal moral attribution Suffering attributed to individual sin, failure, discipline, irresponsibility, or virtue deficiency. Collective moral attribution Suffering attributed to group sin, national guilt, colonial evil, injustice, or collective obligation. Structural attribution Explicit reference to policy, system, infrastructure, economy, law, colonialism, racism, war logistics, or institutions. Meaning without causation Religious comfort, prayer, ritual, or hope without claim about cause. Stabilizing practice Mutual aid, prayer, ritual, counseling, food, shelter, translation, community support. Legitimating practice Religious narrative normalizes or justifies hierarchy, exclusion, violence, poverty, or domination. Mobilizing practice Religious narrative activates protest, aid, advocacy, sanctuary, rescue, public testimony, or reform. Restorative outcome Evidence that religious action contributed to structural change or burden reduction.
