77.4 What synthetic recovery establishes - and what it does not •

Synthetic recovery demonstrates that the planned analyses can recover prespecified effects

under

known

simulated

conditions.

It

does

not

establish

external

validity,

human

behavior,

effect

magnitude,

or

the

truth

of

an

SR

hypothesis.

53

•

It verifies that the planned models can distinguish main effects, interactions, and nulls.

•

It permits code and model freezing before human-participant data are observed. •

It does not establish the real-world magnitude, direction, or existence of the human effects.

•

Human-participant CR-1, CR-2, and CR-3 remain prospective tests and must be reported

as

such.
