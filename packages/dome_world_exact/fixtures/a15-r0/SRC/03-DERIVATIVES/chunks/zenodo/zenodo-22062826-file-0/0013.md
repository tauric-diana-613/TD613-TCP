83. Replication Outcome Classification 57

Collapse Religion rejects the binary habit of labeling every study either “confirmed” or “disproved.”

Replications

Code Classification Interpretation F1 Valid falsification Frozen construct and protocol were preserved, manipulation succeeded, precision was adequate, and the predicted effect was absent or meaningfully opposite. F2 Valid support Frozen construct and protocol were preserved and the predicted effect was recovered with adequate precision. F3 Null / indeterminate Estimate is compatible with substantively important effects in more than one direction or precision is insufficient. F4 Manipulation failure The treatment did not reliably create the intended experimental contrast. F5 Precision / power failure The study cannot discriminate the prespecified effect from a practically important null. F6 Construct mismatch The operationalization no longer corresponds to the frozen SR construct. F7 Systemic substrate omission Relevant structural substrate was omitted while the claim extended beyond the reduced model. F8 Exposure-silo distortion Compound exposures were isolated and the resulting partial estimate was generalized beyond its legitimate boundary. F9 Protocol deviation Material confirmatory procedures changed after preregistration or data inspection without proper separation and disclosure. F10 Nonreplicable result A previously observed result fails across adequately powered, faithful independent replications. An unfavorable result is not classified as invalid merely because it contradicts SR. Methodological
