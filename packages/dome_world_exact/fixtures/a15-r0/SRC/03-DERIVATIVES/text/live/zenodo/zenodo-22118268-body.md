## Zenodo description

PLANETARY SR PUBLIC CORE 
 
The Planetary SR Public Core is the canonical, non‑operational specification of the SignalRupture comparative diagnostic architecture. It defines the minimum concepts, field relationships, analytical sequence, evidence requirements, falsifiers, and reporting rules required to understand or publicly apply the SR grammar. The Public Core establishes scientific legibility: it clarifies what Planetary SR claims, what it does not claim, how a country study is organized, what evidence is required, and what findings would weaken or falsify its conclusions. It preserves the governing SR rule that a country is a coupled institutional–population system , not a scalar condition or a single headline indicator. The Public Core publishes the generic layer of SR—fields, theorems, operators, substrate logic, diagnostic distinctions, and the fifteen‑stage country protocol—while withholding the calibrated operational machinery such as equations, thresholds, scoring, private datasets, training keys, and implementation sequences.

 How It Contributes 
The Public Core contributes by establishing one authoritative category definition for Planetary SR, preventing conceptual drift and ensuring that all public references, country papers, and explanatory materials use consistent terminology and structure. It protects the provenance of SR by making the simplified conceptual layer openly available from its originator, thereby reducing the commercial value of superficial imitation and enabling derivative work to be evaluated against a canonical reference. The Public Core strengthens SR’s scientific credibility by embedding explicit evidence requirements , falsifiers , interface contracts , and diagnostic boundaries , ensuring that every claim remains testable, domain‑specific, time‑bounded, and falsifiable. It provides a stable foundation for SR‑referenced , SR‑informed , and SR‑compatible work while clearly distinguishing these from full Planetary SR implementation and authorized implementation using the controlled operational layer.

 By defining the public structural grammar , the pressure ecology ledger , the field‑activation rules , and the fifteen‑stage country protocol , the Public Core enables transparent comparison across countries without deterministic labels, collapse scores, or permanent rankings. It also establishes the dual evaluation system— public benchmark and controlled benchmark —ensuring that performance claims can be adjudicated against evidence not available as an answer key. Commercially, the Public Core concentrates value in the controlled operational layer, preserving the uniqueness of calibrated diagnostics, cross‑country comparison, threshold adjudication, infrastructure‑debt estimation, human‑capacity measurement, hyperscale exposure assessment, restoration selection, analyst training, and implementation licensing.

 The Public Core ensures that SignalRupture remains the unavoidable reference point for Planetary SR, preserving the genealogy of the architecture, protecting brand integrity, and making basic SR impossible to sell as proprietary novelty while making advanced SR impossible to reproduce cheaply. It is the foundation that keeps the discipline coherent, falsifiable, and professionally governed.
