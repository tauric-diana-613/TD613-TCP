 Description 
 Planetary SR XVIII — Australia provides a structural diagnostic of Australia as a coupled institutional–population system, using evidence from 2000–2025 with 2026 verification. The paper identifies Australia’s continuity architecture as resource‑export capacity, migration‑led growth, housing pressure, and electricity‑system transition . Australia’s demographic profile— “27.80 million residents at end‑2025, up 1.5%, with net overseas migration contributing 301,000” (ABS, 2026)—coexists with structural pressures including “a national median house‑price‑to‑income ratio near 7.6 and household debt around 176% of disposable income” (IMF, 2026). The study distinguishes universal Medicare coverage from effective access, showing how housing costs, aged‑care load, rural/remote service gaps, Indigenous disadvantage, and skills shortages shape continuity beneath high‑income federal stability. Australia’s electricity system— “39.5% renewable and 42.7% coal in 2025” (DCCEEW, 2026)—faces transmission, storage, and coal‑retirement constraints, forming a central test of whether transition capacity becomes reliable function across states and territories.
