# PLANETARY SR XXXIII — TIME-TO-CONVERGENCE REGISTRY: Early-, Middle-, and Late-Horizon Adjudication of the Thirty Locked National Pathways

## Zenodo description

PLANETARY SR XXXIII — Time‑to‑Convergence Registry 
 Description 
 PLANETARY SR XXXIII formalizes the temporal architecture of the thirty locked national pathways by assigning each country an Early , Middle , or Late horizon for when its distinctive SR mechanism should become observable. As the paper states, “Convergence does not mean collapse. It means that the distinctive mechanism already locked for a country becomes sufficiently visible that the pathway can be distinguished from its principal alternatives.” The registry identifies for each country: the first observable signal, the expected receiver or buffer, the convergence criterion, and the timing falsifier. Early‑horizon cases (2026–2028) include systems where household compensation, informal substitution, territorial divergence, macro reorganization, or restoration appear quickly. Middle‑horizon cases (2029–2030) require cumulative institutional, demographic, infrastructural, or regional transmission. Late‑horizon cases (2031) depend on slow structural conversion such as sovereign‑capacity transformation, demographic reorganization, diversification, or external‑redundancy durability. The registry does not alter any forecast; it freezes when each pathway should become adjudicable.

 How it Contributes 
 This paper adds the temporal dimension to the SR universe, completing the triad formed with the Infrastructure Debt Test and the Infrastructural Dependency & Buffer Transfer Map . It operationalizes SR’s structural grammar by showing that mechanisms differ not only in type but in speed . The registry strengthens SR’s Systems Propagation Science by demonstrating that pressure → buffer → receiver → function sequences unfold at different temporal distances depending on national architecture. It also deepens Institutional Cognition by clarifying when institutional reflex becomes distinguishable from household or informal receivers. The timing bands create a falsifiable structure: early cases must reveal their mechanism quickly, middle cases must show cumulative transmission, and late cases must demonstrate durable structural conversion. This transforms SR from a static diagnostic into a temporal adjudication system , enabling 2031 evaluation through ordered signals, bridges, receivers, and falsifiers. By freezing the timing layer, the paper ensures that SR’s universe remains auditable, non‑retroactive, and structurally coherent across all thirty national pathways.
