# BURNOUT BEFORE BURNOUT: A SignalRupture Reverse-Science Reconstruction of the Measurable Worker Across Coupled Systems

## Zenodo description

Burnout Before Burnout 
 Description 
 Burnout Before Burnout is a SignalRupture (SR) reverse‑science reconstruction of the measurable worker across coupled systems. The paper treats burnout not as a purely psychological endpoint but as a cross‑system trajectory generated by interacting labor, household, infrastructural, economic, administrative, and environmental conditions. It introduces a disciplined reverse‑science method that begins from a measured psychological state and reconstructs the supported antecedent processes that produced it, crossing disciplinary boundaries when evidence requires it and stopping only when the causal chain reaches an exogenous or unsupported point.

 The manuscript uses burnout as a hard adversarial case because occupational psychology already possesses mature explanatory frameworks, including Job Demands–Resources (JD‑R), Conservation of Resources (COR), effort–reward imbalance (ERI), work–family conflict research, and recovery science. The paper grants these literatures their strongest findings and constructs a synthetic conventional comparator (M*CONV) to ensure SR is tested against the best available integrated explanation rather than a weak opponent. The central proposition is that measurement legibility is not equivalent to causal origin : variables such as workload, recovery, financial strain, sleep quality, and perceived control may be valid predictors while remaining intermediate states generated by upstream systems.

 The paper introduces two provisional SR constructs— Executable Resource and Effective Recovery Capacity —to distinguish nominal resources from resources a worker can actually use. It then specifies a falsifiable empirical program comparing conventional occupational‑psychology baselines against increasingly granular reverse‑science models using longitudinal, administrative, environmental, and work‑organization data. A cross‑field reconstruction demonstrates how one burnout pathway can activate Collapse Psychology, Collapse Sociology, Collapse Economics, Collapse Governance, Collapse Epistemology, the Drift Collapse Field, and Collapse Society, while activating other SR fields only when case‑specific evidence supports those branches.

 How This Paper Contributes to SR 
 Scientific Contribution: 
The paper establishes SR’s adversarial benchmark for Collapse Psychology. It shows how SR must be tested against the strongest conventional models and demonstrates when SR adds causal resolution beyond established psychological constructs. It formalizes reverse science as a methodological architecture with rules for temporal ordering, cross‑disciplinary traversal, evidence grading, and intervention relevance.

 Methodological Contribution: 
It exposes the compression problem : psychological variables can preserve predictive power while losing causal resolution. By reconstructing upstream generators of burnout, the paper shows how SR can improve mechanism discrimination, intervention targeting, and recovery prediction.

 Multiverse Contribution: 
The manuscript demonstrates SR’s cross‑field grammar , showing how burnout can activate multiple SR fields depending on evidence. This positions Collapse Psychology as a field that interacts dynamically with economics, governance, sociology, epistemology, and household absorption.

 Institutional Contribution: 
The paper clarifies why conventional science may possess relevant components without possessing an integrated causal reconstruction. It strengthens SR’s position within the broader scientific ecosystem by distinguishing distributed knowledge from operational integration .
