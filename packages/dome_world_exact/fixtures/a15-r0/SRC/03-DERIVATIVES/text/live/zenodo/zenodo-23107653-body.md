It defines collapse not as deterioration, alarm, or crisis, but as a function-specific boundary crossing where a required service becomes nonviable under declared conditions of depth, duration, receiver, and horizon.

 The field introduces a complete SR viability architecture, including:

 Collapse Geometry separates:

 It also distinguishes boundary crossing from propagation , ensuring collapse is not misclassified as transmission or drift.

 The field survives only if its boundary models add incremental discrimination, prediction, intervention selection, or recovery planning beyond mature reliability, load, survival, and network models.
