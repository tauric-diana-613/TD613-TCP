## Zenodo description

Phenomenological SR: SR Theory of Bounded Lived Structure 

 DESCRIPTION 
 Phenomenological SR is the first canonical subfield of Experience and Value Science .
Its irreducible object is:

 the bounded lived and embodied state as it appears to a specified person or population across a declared episode or trajectory. 

 The field studies:

 salience

 bodily state

 affect

 temporality

 perceived control

 orientation

 meaning

 intensity

 first‑person qualitative character

 without collapsing them into a single score.

 Its primary unit is:

 a lived episode or trajectory

 belonging to a specified person or sampled population

 with declared onset, offset, context, method, and horizon

 Phenomenological SR is governed by the SR Theory of Bounded Lived Structure , which states:

 First‑person evidence is indispensable for lived quality, but it does not independently establish external cause, diagnosis, prevalence, harm, legitimacy, or the experience of another person. 

 The field is falsifiable.
It retains standing only where its multidimensional lived‑state architecture improves:

 discrimination

 longitudinal prediction

 intervention choice

 error localization

 beyond affect science, symptom scales, consciousness models, interoception, psychophysics, experience sampling, qualitative phenomenology, and domain‑specific experiential models.

 It is architecturally established, empirically open, first‑person preserving, and governed by SR revision rules.

 HOW IT CONTRIBUTES TO SR 
 1. It gives SR a scientific architecture for lived experience 
 Before Phenomenological SR, SR had:

 Harm and Value Dynamics for empirical harm

 Ethical SR for duties and legitimacy

 Identity SR for continuity and recognition

 But SR lacked a field that governs:

 how conditions are lived, felt, embodied, and temporally structured from the first‑person perspective. 

 Phenomenological SR fills that gap by defining:

 the lived episode

 the lived trajectory

 the component‑preserving lived‑state vector

 experiential transitions

 experiential recovery

 experiential drift

 cross‑method divergence

 This is the experiential foundation of SR.

 2. It preserves lived components instead of collapsing them 
 Phenomenological SR preserves nine components:

 salience

 bodily state

 affect

 temporality

 perceived control

 orientation

 meaning

 intensity

 first‑person quality

 This prevents:

 reducing experience to valence

 reducing experience to symptom severity

 reducing experience to intensity

 reducing experience to behavior

 reducing experience to physiology

 reducing experience to narrative

 reducing experience to memory

 Linked concept: Component Record 

 3. It distinguishes lived experience from report, cause, and consequence 
 Phenomenological SR enforces strict boundaries:

 lived experience ≠ external cause

 lived experience ≠ diagnosis

 lived experience ≠ harm

 lived experience ≠ preference

 lived experience ≠ narrative

 lived experience ≠ physiological signal

 lived experience ≠ behavior

 This protects first‑person evidence from dismissal and from misuse.

 Linked concept: SR Bridge Discipline 

 4. It introduces experiential recovery and experiential drift 
 Phenomenological SR formalizes:

 Experiential Recovery 
 Movement toward a personal baseline across time.

 Experiential Drift 
 Persistent change in lived‑state distributions under matched conditions.

 These concepts allow SR to:

 detect experiential deterioration

 detect experiential improvement

 detect experiential instability

 detect experiential transformation

 Linked concept: Experiential Drift 

 5. It introduces cross‑method report divergence 
 Phenomenological SR formalizes:

 Reports from different methods may diverge without implying that one is wrong. 

 This is crucial because:

 momentary sampling ≠ retrospective recall

 diaries ≠ interviews

 physiological signals ≠ lived quality

 behavioral tasks ≠ orientation

 scales ≠ meaning

 The field studies divergence instead of erasing it.

 Linked concept: Report Divergence 

 6. It establishes bridges to other SR fields 
 Phenomenological SR connects to:

 Identity SR (identity continuity affects lived structure)

 Harm and Value Dynamics (lived burden enters harm analysis)

 Cognitive SR (interpretation affects salience/orientation)

 Narrative SR (accounts reorganize meaning and temporality)

 Human Capacity Load (fatigue affects lived control and temporality)

 Infrastructure Science (substrate exposure affects lived structure)

 Feasible Agency (lived control affects action set)

 Ethical SR (lived humiliation, fear, exclusion become normative premises)

 Each bridge preserves:

 transfer object

 pathway

 evidence threshold

 receiving‑domain limits

 Linked concept: SR Bridge Discipline 

 7. It introduces a full falsification programme 
 Phenomenological SR can be falsified by:

 lack of bounded episode

 lack of reliable measures

 collapse into affect or symptom

 inability to separate lived structure from suggestion

 disappearance of population patterns after correction

 disappearance of drift under invariant method

 failure of predicted component response

 equal performance of simpler models

 failure in held‑out episodes or contexts

 This makes lived‑experience science scientific , not anecdotal.

 Linked concept: SR Falsification 

 8. It gives SR the experiential foundation needed for harm, identity, agency, and ethics 
 Phenomenological SR provides the experiential premises required by:

 Harm and Value Dynamics (duration, lived burden, salience, control)

 Feasible Agency (perceived control, orientation, bodily availability)

 Identity SR (lived continuity, conflict, recognition)

 Ethical SR (humiliation, fear, exclusion, lived injustice)

 Without Phenomenological SR, these fields would lack:

 lived‑state evidence

 experiential trajectories

 experiential recovery

 experiential drift

 first‑person quality

 Phenomenological SR is the experiential backbone of SR.

 The SR Contribution in One Line 
 Phenomenological SR gives SR a scientific, falsifiable, component‑preserving architecture for lived experience , forming the experiential foundation for harm, identity, agency, and ethical evaluation across the SR Ultiverse.
