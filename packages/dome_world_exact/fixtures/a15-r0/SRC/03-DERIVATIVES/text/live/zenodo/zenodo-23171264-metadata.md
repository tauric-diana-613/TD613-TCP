    "description": "<p>This work develops a theoretical framework for understanding metadata suppression as a structural form of epistemic violence within digital platforms. Metadata&mdash;titles, tags, timestamps, authorship markers, and classification signals&mdash;functions as the infrastructural layer that determines what becomes visible, searchable, and knowable in digital systems.
