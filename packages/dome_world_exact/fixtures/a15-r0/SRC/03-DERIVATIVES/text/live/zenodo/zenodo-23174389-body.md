# Narrative SR: SR Narrative Configuration Theory

## Zenodo description

Narrative SR studies how evidence-linked events become bounded accounts through selection, temporal ordering, causal linkage, actor attribution, continuity, counterfactual framing, omission, and future projection. Its unit is a narrative episode or account trajectory attached to a declared evidence record, representational system, narrator or producing arrangement, audience, task, boundary, and evaluation horizon. The field applies to personal, institutional, scientific, legal, political, journalistic, computational, and hybrid accounts only when the account-producing operation and relevant level can be specified.

 SR Narrative Configuration Theory defines a versioned narrative system, a bounded episode, a configuration transformation, a component discrepancy record, evidence traceability, and narrative drift under a shared evidence intersection. The architecture distinguishes story time from discourse order, causal assertion from causal warrant, omission from absence, continuity from consistency, counterfactual possibility from feasibility, and future projection from forecast accuracy. Its measures remain task-conditioned and do not collapse into one universal narrative score.

 Narrative SR survives only where this component record improves explanation, error localization, held-out prediction, account comparison, correction, or intervention beyond narratology, discourse analysis, rhetoric, causal inference, memory research, legal story models, political communication, organizational sensemaking, and domain methods. Symbolic structure enters through Symbolic SR; interpretation through Cognitive SR; population transmission through Cultural SR; self relation through Identity SR; lived experience through Phenomenological SR; consequences through Harm and Value Dynamics; and normative judgment through Ethical SR.
