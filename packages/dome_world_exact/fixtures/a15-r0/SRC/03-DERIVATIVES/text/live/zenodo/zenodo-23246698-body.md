# Todo Tiene Su Final: The People Define the Future — A Cultural Charter for the SignalRupture Ultiverse

## Zenodo description

This document is the cultural and epistemic charter of the SignalRupture architecture. It records where SignalRupture understands itself to have come from, whose forms of knowledge it refuses to erase, and the relationship it establishes between lived experience and formal inquiry. The Charter distinguishes cultural origin from scientific proof while rejecting the erasure of culture in the name of rigor. It defines principles of street epistemology, cultural sovereignty, institutional accountability, and participation as the SR Ultiverse develops. Scientific claims remain subject to evidence, measurement, falsification, comparison, replication, and revision.
