# Infrastructure Science: Master Field Specification and SR Infrastructure Theory

## Zenodo description

Infrastructure Science is the SignalRupture master field for the study of substrate behaviour across coupled physical, digital, logistical, financial, administrative, ecological, organizational, and human operating systems. Its object is not infrastructure as a catalogue of assets. Its object is the bounded substrate configuration through which a required function becomes possible, remains coherent, accumulates debt, transmits disturbance, consumes human compensatory capacity, approaches a collapse boundary, and recovers or fails to recover. The field unifies seven internal subfields: Drift Dynamics, Collapse Geometry, Infrastructure Debt, Human Capacity Load, Propagation Geometry, Institutional Coherence, and Recovery Architecture.

 The paper defines the substrate object, formalizes SR Infrastructure Theory, and establishes a measurement and falsification programme. The theory represents a substrate as a time-varying multilayer graph with declared functions, requirements, buffers, debt states, human loads, coherence conditions, and recovery capacities. It distinguishes functional continuity from structural health, local recovery from durable repair, propagation from common shock, authority from executability, and canonical field status from empirical validation. The equations are measurement scaffolds rather than universal constants. Scalar indices are permitted only after unit compatibility, boundary definition, sensitivity analysis, and out-of-sample calibration have been demonstrated.

 Infrastructure Science sits architecturally above the SR Multiverse because it supplies the substrate geometry used by its infrastructure-dependent components. This precedence does not erase Multiverse plurality. Domain fields continue to determine what a state variable means when pressure crosses into economics, health, law, psychology, demography, culture, or another receiving domain. The field is defensive and restoration oriented. It excludes operational targeting, vulnerability exploitation, and unjustified national ranking. The Ultiverse remains dormant because the creation of one master field does not satisfy the complete activation sequence stated in the charter.
