# Experience and Value Science: Master Field Specification and SR Experiential Evaluation Theory

## Zenodo description

Experience and Value Science is the SignalRupture master field for studying how conditions are lived and evaluated. Its object is the bounded experiential-evaluative architecture connecting exposure, embodiment, interpretation, lived state, harm or benefit, capability change, distribution, value appraisal, and normative judgment. It distinguishes the condition of a system from the experience of a subject, experience from causal explanation, distress from complete harm, measured consequence from justified obligation, and canonical ethics from hidden moral preference.

 SR Experiential Evaluation Theory supplies a formal interface among substrate states, interpretive states, experiential states, welfare-relevant changes, and declared normative premises. It permits biological sufficiency, mixed causation, delayed harm, unrecognized harm, adaptive preference, burden transfer, positive experience amid objective deprivation, and negative experience without structural cause. It does not make experience infallible, treat all discomfort as harm, or infer an ought from an is.

 The master field is both empirical and normative, but those modes remain separated. Phenomenological and harm claims require measurement, causal design, uncertainty, and comparison with mature alternatives. Ethical conclusions require explicit principles, affected parties, rights and duties, distributional rules, uncertainty treatment, and reasons that remain open to criticism. The field survives only if this architecture improves causal genealogy, outcome discrimination, intervention choice, burden accounting, or normative clarity.
