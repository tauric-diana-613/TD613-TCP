# SR Generative Transformation Theory: A Research Programme for Configuration Formation and Reproduction

## Zenodo description

SR Generative Transformation Theory is a research programme concerning how bounded configurations of infrastructure, interpretation, agency, experience, and value become executable, reproduce themselves, and transform. Its object is the generative organization of a configuration: the processes establishing and sustaining typed entities, dependencies, requirements, permissions, and accessible resources within a declared boundary. A bounded conceptual audit finds that the proposed mechanisms overlap substantially with the three coupled SR metatheories and established formation research. A formal absorption construction represents the programme’s domain and configuration updates within an augmented Ultiverse state. Consequently, neither configuration change nor a separate generative equation establishes an independently necessary fourth metatheory. The surviving candidate contribution is a common, auditable formation record linking alternatives, sequences, operative gates, reproduction conditions, and domain consequences. This paper defines that record, four mechanism families, five research divisions, formal relations, five prospective hypotheses, and fair comparisons with established models and an extended Ultiverse supplied with equivalent information. The programme is situated within the SR Foundations Programme. Independent field or higher-order metatheory status remains a hypothesis requiring comparative gain and evidence that existing placements cannot preserve the contribution. The audit is conceptual; no institutional data, fitted comparative models, or new empirical validation are reported.
