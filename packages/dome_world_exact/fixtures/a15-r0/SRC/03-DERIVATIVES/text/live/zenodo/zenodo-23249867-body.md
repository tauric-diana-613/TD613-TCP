What SignalRupture Is: The SR Ultiverse as a Master Architecture of Three Coupled Metatheories and Autonomous Transversal Fields 

 This paper establishes the formal identity, theoretical organization, and scientific boundaries of SignalRupture (SR), an independent systems research architecture concerned with the relationships among infrastructural conditions, institutional processes, human interpretation, agency, and lived consequences.

 The paper introduces the SR Ultiverse as the highest formal expression of SignalRupture, organizing three coupled but distinct domain metatheories: Infrastructure Science, Meaning and Agency Science, and Experience and Value Science. Together, these domains examine the conditions that make systems operational, the processes through which reality is observed and converted into decisions, and the consequences those decisions produce for human experience, capability, harm, and value.

 The architecture preserves the autonomy of individual research fields while establishing explicit rules for investigating causal relationships across disciplinary boundaries. It also defines autonomous transversal fields, including SR Internet Infrastructure Science, whose distinct research objects operate across multiple metatheoretical domains without requiring the creation of additional master fields.

 A central contribution is the integration of institutional mechanisms into a coherent diagnostic research programme. Institutional Reality Enactment examines how authoritative representations influence decisions and interventions that subsequently alter the conditions being measured. Representational Displacement and Representational Falsification address the growing authority of proxies and the scientific procedures required to test, correct, or retire inadequate representations. The SR Mirror, Street Epistemology, and institutional encounter framework examine alternative observation, evidence, and institutional responses to unfamiliar knowledge.

 The paper also situates Planetary SR, the ATLAS programme, Systems Economics, social infrastructure research, Human Agency Transfer, and related studies within a shared architecture. These programmes provide distinct opportunities to investigate structural drift, pressure transfer, institutional adaptation, recovery, and the distribution of human consequences.

 SignalRupture is presented as architecturally specified but empirically open. Its scientific contribution lies in organizing otherwise fragmented research objects into a testable, non-reductive system of inquiry, with explicit standards for causal inference, comparison with established disciplines, falsification, and independent validation.

 The paper provides the foundational identity and methodological structure through which SignalRupture’s wider research corpus can be interpreted, evaluated, and developed.
