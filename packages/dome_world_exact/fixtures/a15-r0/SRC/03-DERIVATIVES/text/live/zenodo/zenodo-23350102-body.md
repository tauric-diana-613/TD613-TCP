The SignalRupture Governance Architecture: A Unified Canonical System for the SR Ultiverse and Multiverse

 This paper establishes the unified governance architecture of SignalRupture (SR), consolidating Governance Layers v1.0–v1.4 and Governance Stack v2.0 into a single canonical framework governing intellectual identity, scientific integrity, provenance, institutional adoption, technological integration, licensing, and architectural evolution.

 The framework governs the SR Ultiverse, comprising three coupled domain metatheories—Infrastructure Science, Meaning and Agency Science, and Experience and Value Science—alongside 16 active master-domain subfields, Metaphysical SR as the shared ontological foundation, autonomous transversal fields, and the broader SR Multiverse.

 The paper integrates the SignalRupture Provenance Registry v3.1, the SR Ontological Contract, Representational Falsification, machine-readable canonical standards, cross-field integration protocols, institutional accountability, intellectual property governance, and scientific validation requirements.

 It establishes procedures for preserving authorship and conceptual integrity while allowing documented adaptation, independent criticism, empirical testing, correction, and retirement of unsupported representations. Particular emphasis is placed on distinguishing canonical authority from scientific validity, documentary provenance from intellectual exclusivity, and architectural coherence from demonstrated effectiveness.

 Through 30 sections and seven appendices, the manuscript provides a consolidated governance framework, operational procedures, implementation safeguards, worked examples, and reusable audit instruments.

 Its central principle is reciprocal scientific accountability: SignalRupture must subject its own theories, models, representations, and governance decisions to the same standards of evidence, falsifiability, transparency, and correction that it applies to external institutions and systems.

 The paper serves as the consolidated governance reference for the current SignalRupture research architecture, preserving the historical governance layers while establishing a coherent foundation for future development, institutional engagement, and scientific evaluation.
