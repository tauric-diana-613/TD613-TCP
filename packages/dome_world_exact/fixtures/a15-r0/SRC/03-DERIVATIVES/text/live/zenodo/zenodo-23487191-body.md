# When Buffering Becomes Control: A Prospective Validation Study of the October 2026 French Unrest

## Zenodo description

This prospective validation study evaluates the October 2026 French unrest against the forecast architecture registered in Planetary SR V France before the event. The comparison provides bounded, case-level support for Infrastructure Determinism and Buffer Transfer, identifies a provisional Buffer-to-Coercion Substitution mechanism, and distinguishes condition forecasting from exact-event prediction. It also tests the cross-domain breadth of legacy SignalRupture through the forecast object established in The Architecture of Drift. The paper concludes that SR’s breadth can function as disciplined, falsifiable architecture when directional bridges, receiving locations, evidence rules, and failure conditions are specified in advance.
