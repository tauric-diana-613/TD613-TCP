ADHD, Trauma, Nutrition, and Infrastructure: Misdiagnosis, Excitotoxicity, and the Gut–Brain Axis Abstract Attention ‑ Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD) is a biologically grounded condition, yet its diagnosis

is

by

and

This

how

B6,

ADHD ‑ like

Childhood

The

and

where

ADHD

is

managed

pharmaceutically

rather

through

root ‑ cause

When

This

where

systems

prioritize

pharmaceutical

management

over

comprehensive

biological

3–17—approximately

Despite

Research

The

Trauma, Neurochemical Imbalance, and Excitotoxicity Trauma elevates cortisol and catecholamines, which can influence glutamatergic and GABAergic

signaling.

Research

can

NMDA

(Guerriero,

2015).

Trauma ‑ related

These

patterns

that

ADHD,

complicating

diagnostic

can

molecules

to

Wang

(2021)

and

Inflammation

where

trauma,

gut

disruption,

and

cognitive

symptoms

reinforce

one

another. Environmental Exposure and Infrastructure: Fluoridation as a Case Study Municipal fluoridation is framed as a public health intervention, yet it also represents an infrastructural

These

a

already

by

reinforcing

a

pharmaceutical ‑ first

This

Infrastructure

Magnesium,
