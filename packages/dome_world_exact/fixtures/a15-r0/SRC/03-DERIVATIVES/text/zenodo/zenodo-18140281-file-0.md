Introduction: Absorption as design We are told to sleep better. To eat earlier. To avoid stress. To take care of our gut. We are told Gaza is complicated. That Indigenous youth vanish without a trace. That autism is artificially induced. That grief is personal, not political. These framings appear disconnected, but they are not. They are part of a broader architecture that
