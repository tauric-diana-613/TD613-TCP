Implications Reframing workplace health as an infrastructural phenomenon has significant implications: • Occupational health policy must shift from individual interventions to structural redesign. • Organizational leadership must treat stability, clarity, and resource sufficiency as health determinants. • Clinical practitioners must recognize workplace conditions as etiological factors in patient decline. • Legal and administrative systems must be evaluated for their role in producing or legitimizing harm. Workplace health becomes a cross ‑ disciplinary diagnostic tool, not a wellness initiative. Discussion Workplace health is not a medical specialty—it is an infrastructural analysis.
