    "description": "<p>This essay introduces and defines Exact‑Match Governance, a newly observed form of platform behavior in which digital systems index content but restrict its discoverability to full‑title, exact‑match queries. Building on the author&rsquo;s established theoretical lineage&mdash;Quiet Governance, Systemic Erosion Theory, Infrastructural Exposure Theory, Slow Harm Theory, and Metadata Suppression &amp; Stylometric Resistance&mdash;the essay argues that this retrieval pattern represents a shift from overt moderation to infrastructural control. The essay positions stylometric resistance and DOI‑anchored publishing as counter‑infrastructures capable of preserving minority scholarship within an increasingly selective search ecosystem.</p>\n<p>This essay is part of the SignalRupture canon, a body of work examining contemporary systems, infrastructures, and social dynamics through a conceptual and diagnostic lens.
