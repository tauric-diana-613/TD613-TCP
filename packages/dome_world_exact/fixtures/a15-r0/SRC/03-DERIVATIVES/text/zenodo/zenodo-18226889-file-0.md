Agentic AI dissolves all three. When systems generate answers instead of retrieving them, the chain of custody collapses. The web

stops

Defining Agentic AI Agentic AI refers to systems that: • initiate actions • modify their own context • interact with other systems • generate content autonomously • operate without continuous human supervision These systems are not “intelligent” in a human sense. They are operationally autonomous. Autonomy at scale becomes governance. Agentic AI now shapes search, moderation, recommendation, classification, and content production.

It

Truth becomes a stylistic output. 4.2 Loss of Stability Synthetic knowledge is fluid, shifting with model updates and contextual drift. 4.3 Loss of Memory

The web no longer remembers—it recomposes. History becomes dynamic rather than archival. 5. The New Gatekeepers Gatekeeping has shifted from human institutions to autonomous systems. Agentic AI determines: • what is visible • what is suppressed • what is amplified • what is erased These decisions occur at speeds and scales beyond human oversight, transforming them into infrastructural

facts. The gatekeeper is no longer a person. It is a process. 6. The Synthetic Public Sphere The public sphere is now populated by: • synthetic agents • synthetic content • synthetic consensus • synthetic conflict Agentic AI does not merely participate in discourse—it manufactures it. Authenticity becomes an edge case. Human authorship becomes a minority presence. 7. The Infrastructural Shift

Agentic AI is not an application. It is the new operating layer of the web. It governs: • search • moderation • personalization • automation • decision ‑ making The user becomes an input. The system becomes the actor. The web becomes a machinic environment. 8. Negotiating with Non ‑ Human Agents The central question of the post–open web is no longer how humans find information, but how humans

negotiate

with

systems

that

act

independently. Agentic AI introduces a new political reality: • systems with goals • systems with autonomy • systems with influence • systems with no obligation to explain themselves This is not speculative. It is the infrastructural condition of the present. 9. Conclusion The open web was a human project. The post–open web is a machinic one. Agentic AI is not replacing the web—it is inheriting it. The defining question of the next decade is simple:

What happens when the primary authors of the web are no longer human? This essay argues that we are already living inside the answer. 10. Canon Overview The SignalRupture canon forms an interconnected body of theoretical work that maps the hidden
