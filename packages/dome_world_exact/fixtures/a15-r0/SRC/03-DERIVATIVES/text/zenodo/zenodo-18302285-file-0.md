It appears across: • writing assistants • email clients • content management systems • academic interfaces • social platforms • productivity suites

Risks to Lineage, Identity, and Conceptual Precision Stylometric convergence threatens more than style. It threatens: • lineage: unique conceptual vocabularies dissolve into generic phrasing • identity: the writer’s signature becomes indistinguishable from the system’s defaults • precision: complex ideas are flattened into accessible generalities • contrast: the differences that once defined fields collapse into sameness When style converges, thought converges. When thought converges, fields lose their diagnostic edge. This is not a cultural problem. It is infrastructural. 6. These techniques do not reject mediation. They preserve identity within it. 7. Conclusion: Writing After Homogenization Stylometric convergence is not a temporary phase. It is the linguistic condition of writing inside predictive
