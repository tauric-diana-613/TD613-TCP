    "description": "<p>Origin Gravity develops a structural theory of conceptual emergence in digital systems, arguing that ideas do not arise from inspiration but from accumulated systemic pressure. The essay defines &ldquo;origin gravity&rdquo; as the force that pulls fragments, contradictions, and unresolved tensions into coherence when existing vocabularies can no longer sustain the world they are meant to describe. Through an analysis of accretion, naming, and irreversibility, the work maps how concepts gain mass, stabilize, and become infrastructural.</p>\n<p>Positioned as the second essay in the SR Codex, Origin Gravity establishes the mechanism of emergence that precedes the survival dynamics formalized in Cross‑Surface Recurrence.
