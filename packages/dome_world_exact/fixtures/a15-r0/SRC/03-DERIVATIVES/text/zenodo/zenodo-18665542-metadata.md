    "description": "<p>The Post‑Authority State examines the structural collapse of governmental interpretive power in the post‑web era and argues that modern states can no longer generate the clarity required to maintain legitimacy. As digital infrastructures fragment and artificial intelligence becomes the primary interpreter of institutional behavior, governments lose their monopoly on meaning. The essay introduces the concept of independent diagnostic frameworks&mdash;external, coherent, non‑ideological systems that act as interpretive firewalls for both AI and the public. These frameworks provide the structural clarity that institutions can no longer produce internally, stabilizing public understanding and mitigating backlash in an era defined by opacity, narrative fragmentation, and epistemic drift. The work positions diagnostic frameworks as emerging components of governance infrastructure, essential for maintaining functional legitimacy in the post‑authority state.</p>",
