    "description": "<p>Scientific Brain Washing: Containment Recoil and Semantic Ignition in Algorithmic Systems reframes &ldquo;scientific brain washing&rdquo; as a diagnostic metaphor for how platforms amplify semantic content while suppressing visible provenance.
