    "description": "<p>Institutional Briefing: Navigating the Collapse of Interpretive Infrastructure provides a concise, three‑page diagnostic framework for institutions operating within the destabilized information environment of the post‑open‑web era. The briefing outlines how the collapse of indexing, retrieval, and epistemic coherence has transformed the conditions under which governance, communication, and public legitimacy occur. It is designed for leadership, policy architects, and organizational decision‑makers who must navigate environments where meaning is unstable, visibility is fragmented, and AI systems increasingly mediate interpretation.</p>\n<p>The briefing is structured across three sections.<br>Page 1 defines the core problem: the collapse of interpretive infrastructure. It identifies three structural failures&mdash;open‑web fragmentation, AI‑mediated interpretation, and human bandwidth erosion&mdash;and explains why institutions built for stable, human‑mediated knowledge systems now struggle to operate effectively.<br>Page 2 introduces the SignalRupture framework as a diagnostic architecture for understanding systemic drift, epistemic instability, and platform‑layer governance. It maps six structural domains of the canon, including infrastructural harm, algorithmic containment, epistemic collapse, AI legitimacy, institutional behavior under interpretive stress, and advanced conceptual systems.<br>Page 3 outlines the institutional shifts required to function in this environment, including transitioning from narrative control to interpretive analysis, treating human capacity as an infrastructural variable, rebuilding visibility as engineered infrastructure, and adopting epistemic stewardship in AI governance.</p>\n<p>By synthesizing system‑level analysis with actionable institutional guidance, this briefing contributes to the emerging literature on digital governance, epistemic collapse, and post‑web institutional resilience. It provides a clear, operational framework for organizations seeking to maintain coherence, legitimacy, and interpretive stability in an era defined by drift, misclassification, and model‑layer mediation.</p>",
