1. Extraction as the Organizing Principle of

Modern

Life

The system does not function by supporting human life — it functions by extracting value from it. Every

institution,

from

workplaces

to

digital

platforms,

is

designed

to

convert

human

capacities

into

usable

outputs.

People are not participants in the system. They are inputs.

2. Labor Extraction: The First Layer of the Loop

Labor is the most visible form of extraction. Workers trade: ● time ● energy ● health ● emotional regulation ● cognitive capacity …in exchange for wages that barely cover survival. The system extracts more value than it returns, ensuring workers remain dependent and unable to

exit.

3. Attention Extraction: The Digital Mine Digital platforms extract attention the way mines extract ore. Every scroll, click, and pause becomes

raw

material

for:

● advertising ● behavioral prediction ● algorithmic training ● data monetization The user is not the customer. The user is the resource.

4. Data Extraction: The Silent Harvest Data extraction transforms human behavior into a commodity. Institutions collect: ● location ● preferences ● habits ● vulnerabilities

● social networks This data is used to shape environments, influence decisions, and reinforce dependency. People become predictable not by nature, but by design.

5. Emotional Extraction: The Hidden Economy

The system extracts emotional labor through: ● customer service roles ● social media performance ● workplace expectations of positivity ● constant self ‑ presentation Emotional extraction is invisible because it is framed as personality, professionalism, or "good attitude."

Yet

it

drains

the

same

finite

resources

as

physical

labor.

6. Health Extraction: Profit Through Depletion

The system extracts health by: ● overworking people ● feeding them harmful food ● exposing them to chronic stress ● denying affordable care Once health collapses, the system extracts again through: ● pharmaceuticals ● medical debt ● long ‑ term treatment cycles The body becomes a site of continuous extraction.

7. Time Extraction: The Most Valuable Resource

Time is the foundation of autonomy, yet it is the resource most aggressively mined. The system extracts

time

through:

● long work hours ● commutes ● digital distraction ● bureaucratic processes ● survival tasks A person without time cannot resist extraction. They can only endure it.

8. The Loop: Extraction Creates Dependency,

Dependency

Enables

Extraction

The extraction loop sustains itself by ensuring that: ● extraction produces precarity ● precarity produces dependency ● dependency enables further extraction This is not a cycle of failure. It is a cycle of design. The system extracts until people break — then extracts from the breakage.
