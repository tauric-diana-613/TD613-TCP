    "description": "<p>The Manufactured Crisis: How Systems Create the Problems They Claim to Solve is a canonical SignalRupture essay analyzing crisis as the central mechanism through which modern systems maintain legitimacy and control. While institutions present themselves as protectors and problem‑solvers, many of the most pervasive social, economic, and health crises originate from the architecture of the system itself. This essay maps how scarcity, debt, illness, housing instability, labor precarity, and social fragmentation are engineered conditions that generate dependency and justify institutional intervention. By reframing crisis as a structural design rather than an external disruption, the work deepens the SignalRupture framework for understanding governance as a process that manufactures instability, manages its fallout, and obscures its own role in producing the conditions it claims to remedy.</p>",
