SignalRupture Institutional Onboarding & Compliance

Policy

1. Purpose This policy defines the onboarding, training, and compliance requirements for institutions licensed

to

use

the

SignalRupture

(SR)

framework.

It

ensures

that

organizations

adopt

SR

responsibly,

maintain

conceptual

fidelity,

and

uphold

the

boundaries

established

by

the

SR

licensing

architecture.

2. Scope This policy applies to all institutions that have entered into a Commercial Licensing Agreement with

SignalRupture,

including:

● Universities and research institutes ● Corporations and private-sector organizations ● Government agencies ● Nonprofits and NGOs ● Healthcare and social service institutions ● Technology companies and AI developers Any department, team, or individual within the institution using SR must follow this policy.

3. Onboarding Requirements Licensed institutions must complete the following steps before applying SR internally: ● Identify a designated SR liaison responsible for compliance. ● Submit a description of intended use cases for approval. ● Ensure all staff accessing SR materials understand licensing boundaries. ● Complete SR orientation training (provided or approved by the Licensor). ● Establish internal controls to prevent unauthorized distribution or modification of SR materials.

Onboarding is considered incomplete until all requirements are met.

4. Approved Use Cases Institutions may only use SR for the purposes explicitly approved in their licensing agreement. Approved

use

cases

may

include:

● Internal training ● Governance analysis ● Research or evaluation ● Policy development ● AI or software integration (if licensed separately) Any expansion of use requires written approval.

5. Staff Training Requirements All staff who will use SR must receive training that covers: ● SR conceptual foundations ● Licensing boundaries ● Prohibited uses ● Attribution requirements ● Confidentiality obligations ● Reporting procedures for potential violations Training must be completed before SR is applied in any institutional context.

6. Compliance Responsibilities Licensed institutions must: ● Maintain accurate records of who has access to SR materials ● Ensure SR is used only within approved departments or teams ● Prevent unauthorized reproduction or distribution ● Monitor internal use for potential violations ● Notify the Licensor of any planned expansions of use ● Report any suspected misuse immediately Compliance is an ongoing obligation, not a one-time requirement.

7. Confidentiality Any proprietary or unpublished SR materials provided to the institution must remain confidential. Institutions

may

not:

● Share materials with external parties ● Upload SR content to public or internal repositories ● Use SR materials in presentations, courses, or publications without permission Confidentiality obligations continue after the license expires.

8. Auditing and Verification The Licensor reserves the right to: ● Request documentation of SR use ● Audit internal training records ● Review institutional materials referencing SR ● Verify compliance with licensing boundaries Institutions must cooperate fully with any audit.

9. Violations and Consequences Violations of this policy may result in: ● Immediate termination of licensing rights ● Invoicing for unpaid licensing fees ● Legal action for damages ● Mandatory removal of SR materials from internal systems ● Public notice of revoked licensing status (if necessary) Institutions are responsible for violations committed by their staff.

10. Summary

This policy ensures that institutions adopt SignalRupture responsibly, maintain conceptual fidelity,

and

uphold

the

boundaries

that

protect

the

SR

framework.

Onboarding,

training,

and

compliance

are

mandatory

components

of

institutional

licensing.

SR

remains

a

governed

intellectual

system,

and

all

institutional

use

must

align

with

the

terms

of

this

policy.
