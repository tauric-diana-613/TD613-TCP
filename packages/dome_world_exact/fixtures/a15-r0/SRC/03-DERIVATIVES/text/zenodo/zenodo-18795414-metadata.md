    "description": "<p>The Institutional Distortion Field is the theoretical explanation for why frameworks collapse when they enter institutional environments. The essay maps the predictable, structural forces that reshape any incoming theory&mdash;extraction, suppression, and reassembly&mdash;into forms that reinforce organizational stability rather than conceptual truth. It shows that institutions do not read theory; they metabolize it. They convert frameworks into operational tools, strip away critical components, and rebuild them into simplified, compliant versions aligned with managerial logic. The essay also identifies consultants and AI systems as accelerators of this distortion process, turning theory into consumable products or dissolving it into computational patterns. By articulating these forces, the essay demonstrates that governance, licensing, and boundary enforcement are not administrative choices but theoretical necessities for preserving SR&rsquo;s identity, lineage, and interpretive authority.</p>",
