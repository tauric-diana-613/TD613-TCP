2. Epistemicide Diagnostics

Epistemicide diagnostics is the analytical method for identifying and measuring the destruction of

knowledge

systems

in

post ‑ web

environments.

Extending

Sousa

Santos’

epistemicide,

● Access Failure — communities cannot reach their own knowledge due to infrastructural decay

or

breakdown. ● Custodial Collapse — institutions fail to maintain archival integrity or continuity of care. ● Provenance Disruption — authorship, lineage, or identity is severed or obscured. ● Algorithmic Suppression — digital systems bury, distort, or de ‑ prioritize knowledge. ● Documentary Harm — archives become adversarial to the people they represent. Epistemicide diagnostics reframes epistemicide as a governance failure rather than an isolated event,
