    "description": "<p>Epistemicide Diagnostics: A Foundational SR Framework formalizes the SignalRupture methodology for identifying, mapping, and measuring the destruction of knowledge systems in post‑web environments. Extending de Sousa Santos&rsquo; concept of epistemicide into digital, infrastructural, and governance domains, the framework defines epistemicide as a structural governance failure rather than an isolated event. It introduces five diagnostic indicators&mdash;access failure, custodial collapse, provenance disruption, algorithmic suppression, and documentary harm&mdash;to evaluate when institutions can no longer protect the epistemic rights of the communities they serve.
