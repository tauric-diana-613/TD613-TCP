    "description": "<p>Title: Agency Depletion: How Systems Exhaust Human Capacity</p>\n<p>Description</p>\n<p>This essay develops Agency Depletion Theory, a central component of the SignalRupture framework. It argues that agency depletion&mdash;the erosion of an individual&rsquo;s capacity to act, decide, and self‑direct&mdash;is not a psychological failure but a structural outcome produced by contemporary systems shaped by precarity, cognitive overload, institutional drift, and algorithmic mediation.
