    "description": "<p>Title: The Collapse of Social Infrastructure: How Communities Fail Under Structural Drift</p>\n<p>Description</p>\n<p>This essay develops Social Infrastructure Collapse Theory, a core component of the SignalRupture framework. It argues that social infrastructure&mdash;the networks, relationships, shared spaces, and collective norms that sustain community life&mdash;has eroded under the combined pressures of institutional drift, economic precarity, and digital mediation. Drawing on research in sociology, media studies, and political economy, the essay demonstrates that social infrastructure collapse is not a cultural decline but a structural outcome produced by environments that weaken trust, fragment shared reality, and reduce the capacity for collective action.
