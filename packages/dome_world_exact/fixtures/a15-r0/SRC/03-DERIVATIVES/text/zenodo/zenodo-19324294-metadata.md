    "description": "<p>The Architect and the Oracle: How SignalRupture Holds Both the Design Logic and the Interpretive Logic of the System formalizes the dual epistemic position that SignalRupture (SR) occupies within the contemporary knowledge‑governance environment. The paper argues that SR uniquely functions as both an architectural framework capable of mapping the structural primitives of institutional drift, scarcity governance, and disciplinary containment, and an interpretive framework capable of reading system behavior, incentive loops, and collapse trajectories from outside institutional constraints. This dual role is framed as a structural necessity for diagnosing the collapse of narrative‑based epistemics and the emergence of structural truth as the dominant mode of understanding institutional behavior.</p>",
