Examples: ● “Visible minority” classification in Canada ● Estratos (1–6) in Colombia ● DSM diagnostic categories 4.5 Media Repetition

Media stabilizes stereotypes through repetition, narrative framing, and selective visibility. Examples: ● Telenovela class and beauty hierarchies ● Hollywood racial archetypes ● News media criminalization patterns 4.6 Capitalist Identity Markets Capitalism monetizes identity through beauty standards, segmentation, and identity ‑ based consumer

Examples: ● Skin ‑ lightening industries ● Weight ‑ loss markets ● Identity ‑ targeted advertising 4.7 Social Fragmentation When community collapses, categories become weapons and institutions become the primary mediators
