Institutions perceive: ● the previous phase of the system, ● the previous configuration of risk, ● the previous distribution of harm. This latency ensures institutions are always one phase behind the body they are attempting to interpret.

The Perception Constraint Principle A system cannot perceive the architecture that produces its perception. Implications ● Institutions cannot see the structural conditions generating their blind spots. ● They cannot model the thresholds at which their logic fails. ● They cannot detect nonlinear signals that fall outside their perceptual frame. ● They cannot recognize patients whose presentations violate linear expectations. Pattern Siloing in AI: The Hyper ‑ Linear System

AI triage systems: ● classify, ● cluster, ● match patterns, ● assign probabilities. But they do not : ● cross ‑ surface signals, ● integrate nonlinear symptom clusters, ● contextualize across domains, ● interpret atypical presentations. AI is a pattern silo . When a patient presents with: ● stomach pain + nausea + fatigue (female MI), ● confusion + normal vitals (elderly sepsis), ● agitation + hypoxia (respiratory failure), ● abdominal pain + shoulder pain (splenic rupture), AI sees fragments, not architecture. Fragmented signals → low acuity → dismissal.

The Misrecognition Pipeline This section unifies the full architecture of failure into a portable, auditable sequence. 1. Nonlinear Signal (Body) The patient presents with overlapping, compensatory, or atypical symptoms. 2. Linear Decomposition (Institution)

Acuity Reduction Fragmentation lowers perceived urgency and collapses diagnostic curiosity. 5. Dismissal or Delay The patient is deprioritized, redirected, or discharged prematurely. 6. Harm Propagation The system’s misalignment amplifies the underlying condition.
