    "description": "<p>The Methodological Architecture of SignalRupture: Breadth as Structural Inquiry formalizes the epistemic and methodological foundation of the SignalRupture (SR) paradigm. The paper argues that traditional siloed methodologies&mdash;disciplinary, empirical, computational, or ethnographic&mdash;are structurally incapable of detecting cross‑domain drift, incentive contradictions, and systemic pattern recurrence. SR requires a methodological stance grounded in breadth, not as generalism or intellectual temperament, but as a structural necessity for perceiving institutional behavior across domains, infrastructures, and historical periods.</p>\n<p>The essay defines breadth as a disciplined analytic mode supported by meta‑research, cross‑domain synthesis, and structural triangulation. It establishes the evidentiary standards of SR, clarifies the role of narrative‑structural mismatch, and delineates the limits of the framework itself. As digital environments increasingly narrow cognition and fragment attention, the paper positions breadth as a form of methodological autonomy&mdash;preserving the capacity to perceive structural truth in a systemically constrained world.</p>",
