4. Methods 4.1 Study Design Longitudinal institutional case files were analyzed across seven governance domains: ● child welfare ● corrections ● disability adjudication ● psychiatric hospitalization ● emergency medicine ● addictions governance ● school discipline Cases were selected based on procedural duration and multi-stage administrative involvement. Each case contained: ● initial behavioural narratives (GA1/GA2) ● frontline documentation ● administrative summaries (GA3) ● oversight reviews (GA4) ● longitudinal procedural records (GA5) The study focused on representational transformation across time rather than adjudicating objective

behavioural

truth.

4.2 Operational Definition of Behavioural Reality Within SR, behavioural reality does not imply perfectly objective or observer-independent truth. Instead,

it

refers

to

the

highest-fidelity

available

behavioural

account

located

at

the

lowest

governance

altitudes

(GA1/GA2),

where

complexity

remains

least

compressed.

GTDI therefore measures divergence from originating behavioural representation, not divergence

from

absolute

truth.

4.3 Temporal Ground-Truth Discrepancy Index (GTDI) GTDI_t = 1 - \frac{I_{c,t}+I_{t,t}+I_{r,t}}{F_c+F_t+F_r} Where: ● t indexes procedural time ● I_c represents contextual integrity ● I_t represents temporal fidelity ● I_r represents relational fidelity ● F represents originating behavioural fidelity markers Higher GTDI values indicate greater representational divergence. 4.4 Drift Slope (Δ GTDI) \Delta GTDI = GTDI_{t+1} - GTDI_t Positive values indicate increasing representational drift across procedural stages. 4.5 Measurement Validity GTDI measures representational divergence rather than objective behavioural truth. The metric evaluates

how

institutional

representations

transform

across

procedural

time

relative

to

originating

behavioural

accounts.

5. Governance Altitude Model — Temporal Extension

Institutional Drift extends the Governance Altitude framework temporally. GA1 — Behavioural Reality Original lived complexity and behavioural context. GA2 — Operational Mediation Initial frontline interpretation and documentation.

GA3 — Administrative Compression First major procedural abstraction event. GA4 — Oversight Stabilization Secondary abstraction through review, audit, and supervisory interpretation. GA5 — Temporal Drift Layer Recursive institutional re-encoding across procedural time. GA_1 \rightarrow GA_2 \rightarrow GA_3 \rightarrow GA_4 \rightarrow GA_5 Each transition represents a representational compression and re-encoding event.

6. Results 6.1 Directional Drift Across Procedural Time Across all observed domains, GTDI scores demonstrated a strong directional tendency toward increase

GTDI_{t_0} < GTDI_{t_1} < GTDI_{t_2} Although individual fluctuations occurred, aggregate trajectories consistently moved toward higher

6.2 Average Drift Magnitude Average GTDI increase per procedural layer: ● Child welfare: +0.11 ● Corrections: +0.14 ● Disability adjudication: +0.09 ● Psychiatry: +0.13 High procedural density systems demonstrated the greatest divergence rates.

6.3 Drift Acceleration Systems characterized by intensive audit dependency, frequent transfer processes, and high oversight

6.4 Drift Saturation After approximately four to six major abstraction layers, GTDI growth frequently approached stabilization

7. Predictive SR Propositions — Temporal Proposition 1 The longer a case remains institutionally active, the greater the probability of increased GTDI. Proposition 2 Systems with greater procedural layering exhibit faster representational drift. Proposition 3 Oversight systems accelerate drift through recursive re-encoding of prior summaries. Proposition 4 High-liability governance systems, particularly corrections and psychiatry, exhibit steeper average

Proposition 5 Representational divergence approaches stabilization once abstraction density reaches saturation

12. Limitations ● Drift measurement depends upon available documentation quality. ● Some institutional systems contain incomplete longitudinal records. ● GTDI cannot fully capture unrecorded behavioural nuance. ● Behavioural origin itself may already contain observational distortion. ● The study evaluates representational divergence rather than absolute truth conditions.
