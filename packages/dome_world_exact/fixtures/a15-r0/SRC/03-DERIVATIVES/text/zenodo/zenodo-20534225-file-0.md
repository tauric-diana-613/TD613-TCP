A — Core Architectural Terms Administrative Abstraction The process by which institutions replace behavioural context with procedural, categorical, or diagnostic

language.

Measured

AAI.

Administrative Abstraction Index (AAI) A metric quantifying the dominance of procedural language over contextual meaning in institutional

Altitude Collapse A failure condition where GA layers diverge so severely that oversight stabilizes distortion instead

of

it.

B — Behavioural and Contextual Terms Behavioural Signal High ‑ dimensional, context ‑ rich human behaviour before institutional processing. Behavioural ‑ to ‑ Administrative Translation The transformation of behavioural signal into administratively legible representation.

C — Compression and Drift Terms

Compression Necessity The structural requirement for institutions to reduce complexity to maintain throughput and auditability.

Compression Overload A failure signature where SCR exceeds critical thresholds for multiple periods. Crisis ‑ Trajectory Mapping A method for capturing temporal continuity in behavioural events.

D — Drift and Discrepancy Terms Drift The accumulation of representational divergence over time. Drift Acceleration Index (DAI) A predictive measure of how quickly drift is increasing. Drift Velocity Index (DVI) A measure of the rate of change in distortion metrics.

G — Governance Terms Governance Altitude (GA) A four ‑ layer model describing how institutions transform information. GA 1 — Behavioural Reality The raw, high ‑ dimensional human behaviour before institutional processing. GA 2 — Operational Mediation

The frontline interpretation and immediate operational response. GA 3 — Administrative Compression The layer where behaviour is converted into procedural categories, codes, and summaries. GA 4 — Oversight Stabilization The layer that restores context while stabilizing institutional legitimacy.

I — Intervention and Intake Terms Intervention Framework (SR ‑ IF) The practical SR architecture for reducing distortion. Intake Compression Point The moment where the largest loss of behavioural signal occurs.

M — Metric Terms Signal Compression Ratio (SCR) A metric quantifying the ratio between behavioural signal and administrative representation. Ground ‑ Truth Discrepancy Index (GTDI) A metric quantifying divergence between institutional representation and behavioural reality. Failure Proximity Score (FPS) A predictive measure of how close a system is to structural failure.

O — Oversight Terms

Oversight Stabilization The process by which oversight bodies restore context but also stabilize institutional legitimacy. Oversight Drift When oversight bodies normalize distortion instead of correcting it.

P — Predictive Terms Predictive Model (SR ‑ PM) The SR architecture for forecasting drift and failure conditions. Predictive Drift Trajectory A modeled path showing how distortion will evolve over time.

R — Representation Terms Representational Drift The divergence between behavioural reality and institutional representation. Representation Stabilization The process by which institutions maintain consistent narratives despite underlying complexity.

S — Structural Terms Signal Compression The reduction of behavioural detail into administratively legible units. Structural Distortion Distortion that emerges from architecture, not individual behaviour.

Stabilization Logic The institutional tendency to maintain coherence even when representation diverges from reality.

T — Template and Training Terms Training Curriculum (SR ‑ TC) The SR literacy architecture for institutions. Governance Templates (SR ‑ GT) Operational tools that embed SR principles into workflows.

W — Workflow Terms Workflow Drift The gradual shift of workflows toward abstraction and away from behavioural meaning. Distortion ‑ Resistant Workflow A workflow designed to minimize unnecessary abstraction.
