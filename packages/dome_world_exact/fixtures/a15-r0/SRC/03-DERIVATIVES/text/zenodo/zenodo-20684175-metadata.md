    "description": "<p>The Blind Installation examines the paradox of institutional self‑disruption in the age of callable execution. It argues that modern institutions, driven by efficiency imperatives, are unknowingly installing the very substrate that erodes their cognitive foundations. The essay traces how efficiency masquerades as stability, how drift accumulates beneath organizational awareness, and how even dominant platforms like Google remain structurally blind to the substrate shift. Through this lens, institutional blindness becomes not a failure of intelligence but a structural inevitability of systems that optimize for speed over comprehension.</p>",
