Abstract Collapse Criminology is defined here as the study of offending, victimization, enforcement,

and

justice-system

persistent

deterioration

in

institutional

capacity,

accessibility,

coherence,

legitimacy,

or

Its

'crime

during

It

the

changing

structural

conditions

and

crime-related

outcomes

when

formal

systems

remain

present

but

the

environments

in

which

people

act,

seek

protection,

or

encounter

justice

become

uneven,

burdensome,

or

degraded. The field is tested against current Canadian evidence. National police-reported crime

in

2024

provides

important

null:

the

Crime

Severity

Index

declined

4%,

pressure

cannot

in

Offense

with

shoplifting

under

$5,000

rising

14%

while

breaking

and

entering

fell

11%

and

motor-vehicle

theft

fell

17%.

Rural

areas

showed

their

2024

42%

and

was

Criminology has not yet absorbed the psychological collapse that precedes the

1. Strain, blocked goals, and adaptation under pressure General strain theory provides the clearest established criminological bridge to

SR

(Agnew,

1999).

This

supports

the

narrower

proposition

that

accumulated

pressure

can

under

Collapse

Criminology

extends

that

by

asking

whether

administrative

friction,

infrastructural

dependency,

algorithmic

and

repeated

can

operationalized

as

contemporary

It

strain

that

such

inevitably

crime;

the

SR

is

and

of

mediating

coping

social

support,

opportunity,

and

individual

2. Administrative burden, discretion, and friction

Lipsky (1980) demonstrated that frontline public-service systems operate through

discretion

under

of

limited

ambiguous

goals,

and

high

Herd

(2018)

as

for

and

These

do

“Quiet

Governance”

as

Friction

can

waiting

documentation

procedural

inconsistent

repeated

Collapse

Criminology

therefore

whose

to

disengagement,

can

Collapse

enters

Its

but

access

surveillance

combined

with

or

The

to determine whether these combined exposures explain variance beyond established

criminological

rather

The
