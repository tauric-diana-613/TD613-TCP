Preface Collapse Sociology begins from a narrower empirical problem: social and institutional

can

before

institutional

failure

Erosion

The

therefore

recognizable

forms

persist

while

the

capacities

and

labour

required

to

reproduce

those

Established sociology already studies institutional change, organizational decline, social

reproduction,

anomie,

precarity,

administrative

social

Its

social

and

institutional

under

measurable

of

endogenous

capacity

and

rising

compensatory

Collapse Sociology studies: ●

institutions that keep their shape but lose their function ●

identities that fragment faster than they can be rebuilt ●

narratives that dissolve into metrics and algorithms ●

communities that exist without cohesion ●

governance that operates through friction instead of force ●

meaning that collapses into simulation ●

trust that evaporates into risk ●

social roles that drift without anchor This is not a claim that all social change is dissolution. Collapse Sociology isolates

a

narrower

class

of

transitions

in

which

continuity

of

form

coexists

with

declining

reproduction

capacity,

externalized

maintenance

burden,

or

unresolved

Collapse Sociology is proposed as an emerging sociological research field, not as

It

distinct

beyond

1. Institutional Erosion, Democratic Hollowing, and Structural Transformation

Collapse Sociology begins from the proposition that institutional continuity does not

Organizations

Crouch’s theory of post-democracy is particularly relevant. Rather than arguing that

Crouch

to

and

and

(Crouch,

2004).

His

provides

important

for

the

SR

between

institutional

form

and

institutional

The

work

is

real;

Post-Democracy ,

rather

than

The

Hollowing

of

Western

Democracy ,

is

the

correct

source.

Brown (2015) develops a related argument from a different theoretical direction, examining

how

can

reshape

citizenship,

and

public

by

into

through

and

Bauman’s

and

arrangements (Bauman, 2000). Polity’s description of Liquid Modernity confirms Bauman’s

“solid”

Collapse Sociology extends this literature by treating institutional erosion as a dynamic

structural

Its

but

declines

changing

Sennett’s The Corrosion of Character analyzes how flexible capitalism, unstable employment

The

Rosa’s theory of social acceleration adds a temporal mechanism. Modern societies

Columbia

Collapse Sociology synthesizes these strands into the concept of social-role drift :

3. Meaning, Narrative, and Symbolic Fragmentation Collapse Sociology also draws from scholarship concerned with the transformation

Baudrillard’s theory of simulation examines environments in which signs, representations,

Fisher’s

These works should not be represented as empirical demonstrations that “meaning

Collapse Sociology therefore defines narrative erosion as an empirical research problem

It

6. Administrative Burden, Street-Level Governance, and Institutional Friction

Collapse Sociology’s concept of administrative erosion also has a substantial antecedent

Lipsky’s Street-Level Bureaucracy established that frontline public-service workers

Russell

Graeber’s The Utopia of Rules provides a broader critique of bureaucracy, procedural

Collapse Sociology extends these traditions by defining administrative friction as

Processing

The relevant SR proposition is therefore not simply that “friction is governance.” It is

can

and

to

and

\leftrightarrow

\leftrightarrow
