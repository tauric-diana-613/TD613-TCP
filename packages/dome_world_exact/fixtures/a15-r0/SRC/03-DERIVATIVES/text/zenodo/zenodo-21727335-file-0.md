Preface Collapse Epistemology begins with a blunt truth: knowledge can erode before institutions

The

Traditional epistemology asks what knowledge is, what justifies belief, and what

Collapse

what

It

platform

institutional

recursive

synthetic

•

truth that becomes unstable •

meaning that becomes metricized •

narratives that dissolve into fragments •

interpretation increasingly mediated by algorithms

•

legitimacy reorganized through visibility •

knowledge converted into affective and classificatory signals •

authority performed without equivalent epistemic trust •

understanding displaced by prediction and categorization This is not an epistemology of dissolution because truth literally disappears. It

is

dissolution

because

Collapse Epistemology is proposed here as an emerging interdisciplinary epistemic

Its

Author’s Note: On Structural Inevitability SR retains a structural orientation, but the language of inevitability requires precision.

Structures

and

Accordingly,

from

The SR claim is not that the future can be known in advance. The claim is that

increasingly

probable

when countervailing mechanisms weaken. Collapse Epistemology therefore treats

institutional

The canonical SR formulation remains: collapse is a process before it is an event.

The
