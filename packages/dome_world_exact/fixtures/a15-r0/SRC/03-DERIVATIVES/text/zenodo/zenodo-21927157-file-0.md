Introduction — Anthropology After Stability Anthropology has always studied humans in social and cultural worlds, and the

Globalization,

Appadurai

(1996)

SR Anthropology begins from this transformed disciplinary terrain rather than

Its

The

•

XII.4 Infrastructural Dependency Dependency should combine exposure with substitutability. High use is not necessarily

ID_i = U_i × (1 - R_i) where U_i is normalized reliance on infrastructure and R_i is redundancy/substitutability.

The

when

visibility

or

and
