2. Canonical definition Collapse Economics is the scientific study of divergence between measured economic activity

and

the

evolving

human,

material,

ecological,

and

institutional

capacity

of

a

society.

Its canonical proposition is: Measured expansion does not establish societal recovery when loss stocks remain

elevated,

recovery

is

or

3. What the field is not •

It is not the claim that GDP is useless. •

It is not the claim that every medical, rebuilding, or emergency expenditure is socially

undesirable.

•

It is not the claim that disasters reliably increase GDP. •

It is not a new label for recession analysis. •

It is not degrowth by another name, although it overlaps with debates about ecological

limits.

•

It is not a moral index disguised as national accounting. •

It is not an assertion that one composite number can replace all existing statistics.

The field instead separates three questions that public debate routinely fuses: How much market

production

occurred?

What

losses

accumulated?

How

much

of

the

affected

population,

environment,

and

asset

base

recovered?

V. Hypotheses and Falsifiability 19. Core hypotheses Code Hypothesis Minimum test

H1 Positive GDP growth frequently coexists with rising loss in at least one independently measured domain. Panel of output and loss indicators with preregistered directionality. H2 National aggregation understates the social salience of spatially concentrated shocks. Regional/national exposure ratios and affected-population outcomes. H3 Compensatory sectors can expand during persistent harm without implying recovery. Sector output, harm incidence, and recovery-duration series. H4 Loss-adjusted measures improve prediction of later capacity or well-being beyond GDP alone. Out-of-sample model comparison. H5 Support withdrawal before human recovery increases insecurity or delays recovery. Policy discontinuity or quasi-experimental design. H6 Reliance on GDP alone produces diagnostic stagnation as loss complexity increases. Forecast-error trend and comparison with multidimensional models. H7 The share of burden-mediated production weakens GDP as a signal of net productive expansion. Composition estimates and out-of-sample signal comparison. H8 Human-buffer indicators rise before visible institutional failure in systems that permit compensation. Longitudinal timing of overtime, debt, unpaid labour, turnover and service failure. 20. Conditions that would weaken the field •

Loss indicators do not systematically diverge from GDP after appropriate trend adjustment.

•

Multidimensional loss measures add no explanatory or predictive value beyond established

macroeconomic

and

well-being

frameworks.

•

Apparent divergence disappears when per-capita, distributional, price, demographic,

and

are

•

Compensatory-activity classifications cannot be measured reliably or replicated. •

The proposed constructs fail discriminant validity and merely rename inclusive wealth,

green

or

•

Claims are made universal even though mechanisms appear only in selected crises or

institutional

settings. Collapse Economics survives only if it identifies relations that are measurable, temporally ordered

where

claimed,

and

useful

beyond

rhetorical

criticism

of

XII. Domain Architecture of Collapse Economics 52. Collapse labour economics Collapse labour economics studies situations in which employment and participation remain

secure

their

Collapse labour economics predicts that participation becomes a zombie measure when entry

sign( Δ Y_t) ≠ sign( Δ NAC_t) is admissible whenever Δ L_t, Δ W_t, or Δ U_t ≠ 0 The empirical content lies in prevalence and consequence. Collapse Economics predicts that

If

Δ Capability_t = Benefit(Response_t) - NewLoss_t - ResidualLoss_(t-1) The proposition forbids labeling all compensatory activity waste. Its purpose is to require outcome

Treatment

The

and

ObservedOutput_t = CoreCapacity_t + HumanBuffer_t - ServiceLoss_t The proposition predicts nonlinear failure: output can remain stable while the buffer grows,

GDP diagnostic divergence Positive GDP classification coexists with preregistered deterioration beyond the expected false-alert rate. Divergence is rare, transient, or disappears using per-capita and distributional alternatives. Incremental validity Integrated models improve out-of-sample prediction of loss or recovery over conventional baselines. No reproducible predictive gain after complexity penalties and vintage-correct evaluation. Human Buffer sequence Buffer indicators lead later service failure or institutional strain at preregistered horizons. Effects are contemporaneous only, reverse in time, or vanish after demand and composition controls. Compensatory dependence A rising response share coincides with persistent loss and weak net-capacity growth. Response reliably reduces loss and raises durable capability proportionately. Model stagnation Validated supplementary indicators remain weakly integrated into consequential decisions. Broader measures are demonstrably integrated, timely, and decision-relevant. Null and mixed results must remain part of the architecture. Collapse Economics does not require

every

or

Collapse Economics therefore calls for governance of indicator meaning. Every headline measure

or

can

XVIII. Discussion

The Canadian results clarify the field’s strongest defensible claim. GDP is not invalid. It is

insufficient

for

the

social

task

assigned

to

it.

Wildfires

show

how

national

scale

compresses

local

catastrophe.

Infrastructure

shows

how

current

flows

can

coexist

with

a

deteriorating

stock.

Opioid

mortality

shows

how

measured

activity

and

harm

can

rise

together.

COVID-19

shows

how

output

recovery,

policy

withdrawal,

and

human

recovery

follow

different

timelines.

The analysis also sets limits. A correlation between deaths and health-sector output does not

mean

deaths

produced

prosperity.

A

renewal

gap

does

not

by

itself

quantify

future

collapse.

A

national

wildfire

share

or

in

satisfaction

does

not

fully

post-pandemic

health.

Collapse

Economics

becomes

only

by

preserving

these

boundaries.

The field’s contribution is architectural. It joins production, loss, distribution, stock condition,

compensatory

activity,

and

recovery

duration

in

one

testable

system.

Existing

disciplines

contain

each

component,

but

institutional

analysis

rarely

treats

their

divergence

as

the

primary

object.

The

result

is

a

theory

of

how

economies

can

appear

functional

while

the

capacities

beneath

them

are

being

consumed.

This architecture also changes policy interpretation. More medical spending during illness

is

not

evidence

of

success

or

failure

by

itself.

It

may

be

a

necessary

response

to

harm.

The

relevant

is

whether

the

response

reduces

the

loss

stock,

restores

capability,

and

distributes

protection

fairly.

More

after

fire

may

output

while

and

The

is

not

to

rebuilding.

It

is

to

stop

rebuilding

activity

with

completed

recovery.

GDP Diagnostic Confounding supplies the missing bridge between aggregate measurement

and

the

Eroded

Subject.

An

can

preserve

because

workers

and

increase

buffering

effort.

That

appears

as

credit-financed

consumption,

additional

treatment,

and

The

remains

precisely

the

the

condition

is

under

XIX. Limitations The empirical cases are descriptive and heterogeneous. They establish coexistence and mismatch,

not

a

common

causal

coefficient.

Several

series

have

limited

geographic

coverage,

changing

definitions,

or

short

time

spans.

The

infrastructure

test

relies

on

a

reported-need

snapshot

and

a

two-point

condition

comparison.

The

wildfire

exposure

ratio

compares

scales

but

not

commensurate

welfare

losses.

COVID

evidence

combines

national

output,

policy

dates,

and

selected

well-being

indicators

rather

than

individual

longitudinal

records.

The proposed equations are measurement architectures, not natural laws. Their weights, normalization,

thresholds,

and

recovery

parameters

must

be

estimated

or

transparently

chosen.

Composite

indices

may

recreate

the

very

compression

the

field

criticizes;

must

subordinate

to

Collapse Economics also risks conceptual overreach. “Collapse” can become unfalsifiable

if

every

or

inequality

is

The

field

therefore

requires

persistence,

cross-domain

significance,

impaired

recovery,

or

erosion.

Ordinary

trade-offs,

temporary

with

rapid

recovery,

and

properly

bounded

GDP

should

not

be

relabeled

collapse.

XX. Conclusion

A nation is not healthy because transactions continue. A society is not recovered because output

returns

to

trend.

An

economy

is

not

strong

merely

because

the

activities

required

to

survive

its

losses

are

measurable.

Collapse Economics begins where the production account ends. It studies the deaths not reversed,

the

illness

not

healed,

the

people

not

rehoused,

the

forests

not

restored,

the

infrastructure

not

renewed,

the

security

not

regained,

and

the

institutional

capacity

spent

to

preserve

normal

appearance.

It

the

that

prevent

worse

because

a

loss

without

a

recovery

ledger

would

be

equally

incomplete.

The field does not abolish GDP. It restores GDP to its proper place. Production must be measured.

So

must

loss.

So

must

distribution.

So

must

the

condition

of

the

stocks

that

make

future

life

possible.

So

must

the

time

required

for

people

and

systems

to

recover.

The economy can expand while the nation contracts. Collapse Economics exists to measure

the

difference.
