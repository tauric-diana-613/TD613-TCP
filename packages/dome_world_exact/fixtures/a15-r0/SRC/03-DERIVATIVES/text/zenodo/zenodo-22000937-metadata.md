    "description": "<h1><strong>System Inheritance Studies &mdash; Description</strong></h1>\n<p>System Inheritance Studies (SIS) is a SignalRupture field that examines how historical institutional structure becomes encoded in administrative data, learned by models, activated through institutional action, and returned as future evidence. As your manuscript states, modern AI &ldquo;<em>does not enter a neutral world</em>&rdquo;; it enters systems already shaped by housing policy, policing, healthcare access, school finance, and accumulated wealth.</p>\n<p>SIS formalizes four core processes:</p>\n<ul>\n<li>\n<p><strong>Inherited representation</strong> &mdash; historical structure remains detectable in present-day data even after explicit categories are removed.</p>\n</li>\n<li>\n<p><strong>Proxy reconstruction</strong> &mdash; protected or historical states can be predicted from &ldquo;neutral&rdquo; features.</p>\n</li>\n<li>\n<p><strong>Amplification</strong> &mdash; model or institutional action increases disparity relative to a baseline.</p>\n</li>\n<li>\n<p><strong>Recursive inheritance</strong> &mdash; system-induced outcomes become training data for successor systems.</p>\n</li>\n</ul>\n<p>The field defines measurable constructs such as the <strong>Inheritance Recoverability Index</strong>, <strong>Model Amplification</strong>, <strong>Deployment Amplification Ratio</strong>, <strong>Exposure Concentration Ratio</strong>, <strong>Recursive Gain</strong>, and <strong>Genealogical Contribution</strong>. It also introduces a strict evidentiary ladder (E0&ndash;E5) to prevent rhetorical inflation and force causal discipline.</p>\n<p>Unlike conceptual fairness theory, SIS is <strong>empirical</strong>. The manuscript includes four demonstrations:</p>\n<ul>\n<li>\n<p>historical redlining &rarr; contemporary tract conditions</p>\n</li>\n<li>\n<p>predictive policing &rarr; selective observation</p>\n</li>\n<li>\n<p>mortgage underwriting &rarr; inherited credit structure</p>\n</li>\n<li>\n<p>school risk classification &rarr; administrative labels as produced outcomes</p>\n</li>\n</ul>\n<p>These are not claims of discrimination; they are <strong>tests of inheritance channels</strong>.</p>\n<div>\n</div>\n<h1><strong>How It Contributes to the SR Canon</strong></h1>\n<p>SIS is one of the most important SR fields because it provides the <strong>genealogy layer</strong> that other SR fields depend on.</p>\n<h3><strong>1. It gives SR a universal transmission architecture.</strong></h3>\n<p>SIS formalizes the full chain:</p>\n<blockquote>\n<p><strong>Historical structure &rarr; records &rarr; features &rarr; model &rarr; institutional action &rarr; changed environment &rarr; future records</strong></p>\n</blockquote>\n<p>This becomes the backbone for:</p>\n<ul>\n<li>\n<p><strong>EAO</strong> (Epistemic Architecture Obsolescence)</p>\n</li>\n<li>\n<p><strong>Algorithm Field</strong></p>\n</li>\n<li>\n<p><strong>Collapse Epistemology</strong></p>\n</li>\n<li>\n<p><strong>System Propagation Science</strong></p>\n</li>\n<li>\n<p><strong>CCIS</strong></p>\n</li>\n</ul>\n<p>SIS explains <em>where the data came from</em> before any model touches it.</p>\n<h3><strong>2. It separates reproduction, amplification, and recursion.</strong></h3>\n<p>Most fairness literature collapses these into &ldquo;bias.&rdquo;<br>SIS forces analysts to specify:</p>\n<ul>\n<li>\n<p>Did the model <strong>reproduce</strong> the inherited structure?</p>\n</li>\n<li>\n<p>Did it <strong>amplify</strong> it?</p>\n</li>\n<li>\n<p>Did deployment <strong>change</strong> the environment?</p>\n</li>\n<li>\n<p>Did retraining <strong>inherit</strong> the change?</p>\n</li>\n</ul>\n<p>This is a structural breakthrough.</p>\n<h3><strong>3. It introduces a cross-domain measurement standard.</strong></h3>\n<p>SIS provides operators that can be used across:</p>\n<ul>\n<li>\n<p>policing</p>\n</li>\n<li>\n<p>housing</p>\n</li>\n<li>\n<p>credit</p>\n</li>\n<li>\n<p>education</p>\n</li>\n<li>\n<p>healthcare</p>\n</li>\n<li>\n<p>generative AI</p>\n</li>\n<li>\n<p>urban systems</p>\n</li>\n</ul>\n<p>This makes SR the only metatheory with <strong>portable causal operators</strong>.</p>\n<h3><strong>4. It provides empirical demonstrations without overclaiming.</strong></h3>\n<p>Your manuscript is disciplined:</p>\n<ul>\n<li>\n<p>It shows persistence.</p>\n</li>\n<li>\n<p>It shows recoverability.</p>\n</li>\n<li>\n<p>It shows exposure concentration.</p>\n</li>\n<li>\n<p>It shows temporal association.</p>\n</li>\n<li>\n<p>It shows simulation-based amplification.</p>\n</li>\n</ul>\n<p>But it <strong>never</strong> claims causation without identification.<br>This is rare and scientifically strong.</p>\n<h3><strong>5. It gives SR a falsifiable theory of institutional genealogy.</strong></h3>\n<p>SIS is not &ldquo;bias studies.&rdquo;<br>It is a theory of <strong>how institutions transmit themselves</strong> through data pipelines and model generations.</p>\n<p>It explains why removing protected attributes does not remove inherited structure.</p>\n<p>It explains why retraining can reinforce the past.</p>\n<p>It explains why institutions can automate their own genealogy.</p>",
