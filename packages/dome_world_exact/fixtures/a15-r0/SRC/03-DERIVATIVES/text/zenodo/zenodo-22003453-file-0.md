SC Observable Components Component Operational meaning Example indicator Dependent-system breadth How many distinct systems materially rely on substrate S? transport, heating, industry, AI/data centres Dependency intensity How much of each system's operation requires S? share of transport energy electrified; share of compute tied to grid Criticality What happens if S becomes scarce or unavailable? service interruption, production loss, safety risk Substitutability Can demand shift quickly to another substrate? backup fuels, distributed generation, alternate modes Temporal synchrony Do dependent systems require S at the same time? peak electricity demand, simultaneous heat/compute load
