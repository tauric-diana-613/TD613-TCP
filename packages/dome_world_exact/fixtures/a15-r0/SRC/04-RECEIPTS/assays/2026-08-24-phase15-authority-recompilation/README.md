## Typed coverage receipt

Coverage is not one yes/no column. Read both:

- `audit_status`: `IMPLEMENTED`, `PARTIAL`, `BLOCKED`, `SCHEMA_ONLY`, or `HUMAN_GATED`;
- `reachability`: `SCHEMA_ONLY`, `SEEDED`, `BOUNDED_EXECUTED`, `BLOCKED_BY_SOURCE`, or `HUMAN_GATED`.

`source_status_raw` retains the original descriptive wording, `implemented_in` names concrete outputs, and `blocker_ids` resolve through the entity index. `SCHEMA_ONLY` means the instrument exists but was not executed; `SEEDED` means candidates exist but the trail is not fully adjudicated; `HUMAN_GATED` preserves a deliberate boundary. Every row retains `phase_2_run: false`.
