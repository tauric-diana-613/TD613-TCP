# Operational Corpus Architecture

SignalRupture is preserved as three overlapping corpora rather than one clean theory archive:

## Formal SR

Formal theories, theorems, frameworks, observer structures, temporal trajectories, graph observability, bounded rule-spaces, structural operators, models, simulations, and empirical/formal claims.

## Fictional SR Universe

Worldbuilding, narrative artifacts, characters, ecologies, topology, pressure fields, membranes, lattices, fractures, corridors, traversal, memory, interfaces, superstructures, apertures, and other technical intuitions carried in fictional form.

## Governance / provenance SR

Canon governance, lineage, attribution, licensing, recurrence, retrieval, archival survival, metadata, custody, visibility, indexing, recomposition, and provenance.

## Seam artifacts

Room assignment is multi-label. A work can occupy all three rooms. `seam-artifacts/` is for compositions, concepts, or operators whose technical value migrates between rooms. Classification must never determine whether an artifact is preserved.

Phase 1.5 begins with title heuristics only. Final labels require content-level reading and should retain confidence, evidence, and reviewer identity.

## Relations and hypotheses

`relations/` defines explicit, inferred, and researcher-proposed graph edges and keeps competing formulations co-present. `hypotheses/` is the delicious-but-unproven queue: high research interest determines what receives scrutiny, never what the archive declares true.

## Connector-ready registries

The operational layer is intentionally flat enough for a GitHub connector to inspect without executing code:

- `authority/` — time-indexed `G/C/E/F/K` assertions and class-membership candidates;
- `claims/` — claim-status events and source-specific evidence taxonomies;
- `maps/` — immutable author self-model snapshots (`C_t`), never overwritten;
- `relations/` — typed compiler edges, representation families, transformation candidates, ordinal series, and first-appearance anatomy;
- `operators/` — append-only lifecycle events such as compilation, map issuance, and authority reclassification;
- `state/` — six-axis epistemic-object states `(E,V,R,P,L,A)` with per-axis evidence.

An unknown stays queryable as an unknown. A source-declared relation, archive inference, and researcher hypothesis never share the same provenance label. Historical, conceptual, operational, navigational, manifestation, and authority graphs remain separate projections.
