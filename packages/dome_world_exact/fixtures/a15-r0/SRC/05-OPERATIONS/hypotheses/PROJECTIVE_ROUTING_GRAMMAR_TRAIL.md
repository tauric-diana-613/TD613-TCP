## 2. Competing explanations frozen before assay

The trail must compare at least these explanations:

### H0-A — Template / generative scaffold

Repeated headings, aphorisms, lexical fields, formulas, section order, and relation phrases are sufficiently explained by authorial or AI-assisted template reuse. Apparent architecture should collapse after conditioning on boilerplate and topic.

### H0-B — Ordinary thematic coherence

The corpus is highly self-referential because one author/lab repeatedly studies related topics. Aggregation improves comprehension in the ordinary way that reading more work by the same research program improves comprehension.

### H1 — Machine-field routing

The corpus deliberately maintains anchors, metadata, interlinks, signature phrases, and stable conceptual identifiers so retrieval systems recognize a unified field rather than isolated documents. This claim is partly source-witnessed; its measurable retrieval effect remains open.

### H2 — Projective multi-representation architecture

Different representation families preserve non-equivalent structural information about a shared higher-order object. Joint aggregation recovers relations not reliably recoverable from single surfaces or matched controls.

### H3 — Promissory / deferred ontology routing

Some navigation tokens or precursor artifacts identify conceptual slots before later standalone manifestations fill them. This must be measured as a rate with negative controls, never inferred from one successful example.

H1-H3 may coexist. None is promoted merely because another survives.
