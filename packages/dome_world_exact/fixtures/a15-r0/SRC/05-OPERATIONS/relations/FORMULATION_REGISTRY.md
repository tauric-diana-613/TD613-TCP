# Formulation Registry

Equations, cycles, taxonomies, numbered architectures, and operator chains are versioned witnessed objects. The archive must not normalize two formulations into one merely because their symbols or prose appear related.

Each formulation retains:

- exact source rendering or source-preserving transcription;
- source work, edition, manifestation, and capture hash;
- publication date and archive retrieval date;
- symbol definitions explicitly supplied by that source;
- source-declared scope;
- a separate normalized/search field;
- relationships to other formulations, defaulting to `UNKNOWN`;
- human notes and hypotheses outside the witnessed layer.

A pair such as `P → T → Φ → R → P′` and `P_t → R_t → D_t → C_t → O_t → P_{t+1}` begins as two witnessed formulations. `POSSIBLE_NESTED_MECHANISM_OF`, `COMPETING_FORMULATION_WITH`, `REVISES`, and `UNRELATED` are possible later findings; none is the default.

Likewise, repeated cardinalities—three, five, seven, nine, twelve—receive scoped identifiers. `seven-part architecture` and `seven-layer causal cascade` remain distinct unless evidence establishes homology.
