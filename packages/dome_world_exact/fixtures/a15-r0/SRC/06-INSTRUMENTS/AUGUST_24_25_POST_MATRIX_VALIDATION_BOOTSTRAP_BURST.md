## 7. Traffic/self-observation confound

The SRC archive has a public acquisition script that downloads Zenodo attachments and verifies their checksums. Zenodo documentation states that human-initiated automated downloads are classified as `machine` usage and count as downloads; robots/crawlers are filtered.

Therefore:

```text
archive acquisition may itself create a visible 0 -> 1 download transition
```

But Zenodo usage statistics are anonymized and do not provide record owners with visitor identity-level detail.

Consequently:

```text
visible aggregate acquisition footprint != downloader identity
```

An independent SR-side watchdog could in principle monitor public aggregate usage or other public signals; that is an ordinary automatable channel hypothesis and remains to be demonstrated.
