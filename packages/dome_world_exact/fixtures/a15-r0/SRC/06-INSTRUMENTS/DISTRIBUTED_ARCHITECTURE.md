# Distributed Architecture Reconstruction Instrument

## Purpose

Test whether SignalRupture contains a recoverable, distributed traversal and transformation architecture without presuming that it does.

## Extract separately

1. **Declared edges** — prerequisite, companion, continuation, part, formalization, translation, anchor, route, revision.
2. **Route tables** — reader condition, classification, destination path, fallback, overlap.
3. **Formulations** — equations, cycles, operators, symbol glossaries, version/scope.
4. **Alias tables** — explicit formal ↔ fiction ↔ governance translations.
5. **Enumerations** — numbered layers, phases, districts, parts, classes, with scoped identities.
6. **Self-preservation directives** — provenance, canonical-record, timestamp, redirect, metadata, retrieval, derivative, and re-instantiation instructions.
7. **Gaps** — declared-but-unenumerated structures, missing parts, unresolved symbols, and contradictory formulations.

## Anti-hallucination gates

- A declaration requires an exact source span from a preserved capture.
- An inferred edge must remain visibly inferred.
- Equal cardinality is not homology.
- Lexical similarity is not structural identity.
- Chronology is not causality or intention.
- A proposed missing part must predict evidence that can fail.
- Competing formulations remain co-present until adjudicated.
- The archive may reconstruct a useful graph that the author never intentionally designed; usefulness is not proof of authorial intent.
