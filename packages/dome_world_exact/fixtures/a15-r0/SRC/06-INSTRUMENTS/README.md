# Instrument Specifications

This directory defines future instruments. It does not contain source artifacts and does not silently turn machine suggestions into scholarship.

## Planned instruments

### Recurrence map

Tracks concepts across works, editions, platforms, and the three corpus rooms. Results must distinguish literal term recurrence from structural-relation recurrence.

### Terminology drift

Shows when a label changes while a relation, operator, or narrative mechanism persists. Outputs are candidates until evidenced with source spans.

### Citation and reference graph

Separates formal citations, DOI links, internal series links, platform backlinks, declared continuations, and archive-inferred relationships.

### Concept lineage

Supports queries across formal SR, fictional SR Universe, and governance/provenance SR without forcing single-room classification. A concept page should expose source spans, chronology, room-specific roles, nearby concepts, and confidence.

### Disappearance monitor

Periodically rechecks known manifestations and appends observed state transitions. It preserves negative evidence without assigning cause. A vanished page remains `UNAVAILABLE` unless separate lineage evidence supports a stronger claim.

### Versioned epistemic system

Models preservation and authority as different dimensions, distinguishes latent from retrospective architecture, and treats later field manuscripts and Canon Maps as testable compiler/checkpoint candidates rather than settled ontology. See `VERSIONED_EPISTEMIC_SYSTEM.md`.

### Representation and route assay

Separates platform translation (`T_surface`) from epistemic-register lift (`T_epistemic`), preserves typed companion cycles, and tests route-conditioned endpoints without collapsing path history. See `REPRESENTATION_AND_ROUTE_ASSAY.md`.

### Self-perception and governance assay

Compares the author's time-indexed self-model `C_t` with a cutoff-bounded archive reconstruction `G_t`, and evaluates governance rules case by case rather than manufacturing a global coherence score. See `SELF_PERCEPTION_AND_GOVERNANCE_ASSAY.md`.

### Surface-withdrawal assay

Simulates removal of one platform and reports identity, text, lineage, relations, chronology, and authority recoverability separately. It never converts unavailability into a causal suppression claim. See `SURFACE_WITHDRAWAL_ASSAY.md`.

## Human-gate rule

Automated extraction may propose titles, entities, concepts, similarities, and edges. Only evidence-backed rules or human adjudication may merge works, assign lineage, or promote a provenance conclusion.
