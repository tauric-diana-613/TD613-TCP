# Representation and Route Assay

## Companion topology

Build companion, foundation, formalization, application, and navigation relations as directed typed edges. `COMPANION_TO` is not automatically symmetric. A reciprocal pair requires two independently evidenced statements. If two predicates point in the same semantic direction, preserve the mutual references without manufacturing an inverse edge.

Cycle queries run separately on historical, conceptual, operational, and navigational projections. A cycle assembled from mixed graph types is an analysis error.

## Representation-lift hunt

Candidate pairs include narrative or intuitive precursors followed by theorem, law, formal, empirical, or operational representations. A pair enters the serious hunt only when a source declares the relationship or a human-adjudicated content comparison supports it.

For every pair, compare:

1. stable conceptual object;
2. semantic core;
3. claim scope;
4. causal strength;
5. universality;
6. variables and operators;
7. measurement rules;
8. falsifiers and counterexamples;
9. caveats and boundary conditions;
10. authority status.

Possible outcomes include faithful translation, claim narrowing, claim broadening, claim inflation, added measurement, added falsifier, and unresolved.

## Navigation graph

Extract only source-declared navigation:

- entry points and audiences;
- `START HERE` conditions;
- `read next`;
- consult;
- explicit hyperlinks;
- routes and fallbacks.

Conceptual relations do not become navigation edges automatically. Retained metadata may witness a router without revealing its destination table; such branches remain unresolved.

## Route-conditioned endpoint test

For two witnessed paths reaching the same endpoint, compare acquired neighboring concepts, source order, claim statuses, authority assertions, and reachable next steps. Preserve both states until equality is demonstrated.

Do not label the phenomenon holonomy without a separately justified mathematical model.
