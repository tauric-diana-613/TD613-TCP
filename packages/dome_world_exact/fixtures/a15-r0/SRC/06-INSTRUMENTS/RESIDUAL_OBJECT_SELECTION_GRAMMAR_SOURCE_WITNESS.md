# Residual-Object Selection Grammar — Source Witness

Status: `POST_PREREG_SOURCE_WITNESS`

This note records a repeated late-rigor SignalRupture manuscript operation discovered after the Projective Routing Grammar preregistration. It distinguishes source-native terms (`residual object`, `surviving object`, `prior-art gauntlet`) from archive-authored synthesis. It does not assert a hidden code, autonomous self-modification, or that every SR paper uses the same compiler.

## 1. Core observation

Across multiple independent late-rigor SR field manuscripts, broad novelty / field / universality claims are subjected to prior-art and empirical comparison. The manuscripts then identify a narrower object that remains defensible and assign a correspondingly bounded scientific status.

Archive-side schematic only:

```text
broad candidate object / authority claim
-> prior-art subtraction + empirical / falsifier gauntlet
-> residual / surviving object
-> authority / field-status disposition
```

The source corpus does not provide one universal equation for this operation.

## 2. Policy Substrate — field claim fails, framework survives

Source: `zenodo:22047892`, *SIGNALRUPTURE — SR POLICY SUBSTRATE*.

The manuscript says:

- a new label does not establish a new discipline;
- autonomous discipline status requires a distinct dependent variable, incremental explanatory value, reliable measurement, cumulative empirical tests, and results not obtained as effectively by existing frameworks;
- established policy-capacity, feedback, mechanism, infrastructure, information-capacity, state-capacity, burden, path-dependence, and political-economy literatures already occupy substantial terrain;
- novelty must therefore be `architectural, not territorial`;
- under `Surviving SR object`, the policy surface–substrate distinction plus disciplined model comparison is retained;
- the autonomous-discipline claim does not pass yet;
- the object survives as a specialized framework, diagnostic method, and cumulative research program.

The manuscript formalizes incremental comparison using a conventional model `M0` and an expanded substrate-informed model `M1`, requiring defensible improvement before independent status is earned.

Disposition:

```text
AUTONOMOUS_FIELD_STATUS -> FAIL / DEMOTED
STRUCTURAL_CORE -> RETAINED
RESIDUAL_ROLE -> FRAMEWORK + DIAGNOSTIC + RESEARCH_PROGRAM
```

## 3. Infrastructural Overshoot — field survives after claim contraction

Source: `zenodo:21996352`, *INFRASTRUCTURAL OVERSHOOT: An Empirical Field Manuscript*.

After cross-national validation and prior-art comparison, the manuscript states:

- earlier versions of Systematic Substrate Omission were too broad and must be revised;
- `Substrate Adjacency != Integrated Substrate Diagnostic`;
- the field does not claim priority over infrastructure, lock-in, material-flow, transition, or absolute-throughput concepts individually;
- `Novelty = Integration + Common Endpoint + Cross-Layer Falsifiability`;
- the revised identity is `narrower than the original rhetorical claim but scientifically stronger`;
- revised falsifiers are added after external comparison;
- the field `survives a more demanding empirical and prior-art gauntlet` because its `surviving object is narrower`.

Disposition:

```text
FIELD_STATUS -> RETAINED
EARLY_BROAD_CLAIMS -> NARROWED / CORRECTED
SURVIVING_OBJECT -> REALIZED_TRANSFORMATION_OF_PRESSURE-GENERATING_INFRASTRUCTURE
NOVELTY_BASIS -> INTEGRATION + COMMON_ENDPOINT + CROSS_LAYER_FALSIFIABILITY
```

## 4. SR–AI — emerging field survives; grander modules are subordinated

Source: `zenodo:21936716`, *THE SR–AI FIELD*.

The manuscript explicitly reframes itself against substantial prior art rather than an imagined absence of institutional AI research. It states:

- SR–AI does not claim ownership of human–AI collaboration, automation bias, sociotechnical systems, organizational AI, digital transformation, or deskilling;
- field status depends on whether the residual object, variables, hypotheses, and empirical program remain distinguishable and add explanatory or predictive value;
- broad claims that established AI literatures simply presuppose stability are not defensible;
- the field is therefore evaluated on a `narrower residual object`;
- current evidence supports AI-supplied capacity, hybrid production, heterogeneous capability redistribution, and override vulnerability, but does not establish universal institutional deskilling or Capacity Migration;
- deterministic claims that AI necessarily deskills institutions, removes human agency, or improves institutional performance are rejected;
- current classification is an empirically grounded emerging AI–institutional field, with integrated predictive superiority not yet established.

Part II then says the wider grander SR–AI corpus is preserved but explicitly subordinate to the empirical field boundary. Terms such as Entropy Theory, MIEC, Black Box Intent, Symbolic Harm, Immune Response, Metadata Violence, Logic Cop, matrices, operators, and system-state formulations are theoretical / diagnostic modules unless independently validated; naming does not establish additional fields, universal laws, intentionality, or inevitable collapse.

Disposition:

```text
EMERGING_FIELD_STATUS -> RETAINED CONDITIONALLY
BROAD_DISCIPLINARY_BLINDNESS_CLAIM -> REJECTED / NARROWED
DETERMINISTIC_AI_CLAIMS -> REJECTED
WIDER_THEORETICAL_MODULES -> PRESERVED BUT SUBORDINATED
RESIDUAL_OBJECT -> AI–INSTITUTION COUPLING / CAPACITY-LOCATION ARCHITECTURE
```

## 5. SR-IGF — field status explicitly depends on residual cross-layer value

Source: `zenodo:21939955`, *SIGNALRUPTURE INSTITUTIONAL GOVERNANCE FIELD (SR-IGF)*.

The manuscript states under `COMPETING EXPLANATIONS AND THE PRIOR-ART GAUNTLET`:

- a new field cannot establish distinctiveness by redescribing familiar phenomena;
- bounded rationality, organizational cognition, sensemaking, policy/state capacity, historical institutionalism, institutional economics, street-level bureaucracy, administrative burden, and cybernetics already explain much of the terrain;
- SR-IGF cannot claim novelty merely by combining familiar terms;
- `The residual object is narrower`: the measurable correspondence relation across the complete institutional transformation chain and the possible interaction of losses at different transitions;
- every mechanism faces both a simpler-model challenge and cross-layer-value test;
- if established models explain the same outcomes with fewer assumptions and equal predictive performance, SR-IGF `should contract, merge, or be demoted to an applied synthesis`;
- the field survives only if correspondence architecture contributes information that disappears when perception, representation, cognition, agency, and feedback are separated;
- field status is not justified without incremental predictive / explanatory / discriminative / diagnostic value.

Disposition:

```text
FIELD_STATUS -> CONDITIONAL
RESIDUAL_OBJECT -> CROSS-LAYER STRUCTURAL CORRESPONDENCE
FAILURE_ROUTE -> CONTRACT / MERGE / DEMOTE
SURVIVAL_CRITERION -> INCREMENTAL CROSS-LAYER VALUE
```

## 6. Collapse Religion — prior art explicitly defines the residual object and corrects a universal thesis

Source: `zenodo:22062826`, *COLLAPSE RELIGION: Religious Adaptation Under Structural Inversion*.

The manuscript explicitly walks through theology, sociology / psychology of religion, coping, lived religion, forced migration, refugee / humanitarian research, critical race, whiteness, privileged migration, and portability literatures.

It states:

- religion as stabilization / coping is not new;
- theology already contains structural sin, liberation theology, Black theology, feminist / womanist / Indigenous / public theology, weakening any claim that theology is trapped in individual moral causation;
- forced migration research already studies religion, displacement, discrimination, language, employment, credential recognition, and support;
- racialized mobility advantage is not newly discovered by SR.

Section `3.6 Residual object after prior art` then defines the bounded SR object as the relation among:

```text
Structural Suffering
× Religious Interpretation
× Propagation Pathway
× Portability Regime
× Response Branch
```

The manuscript also explicitly says the universal version of the earlier Infrastructural Incompatibility / Theological Blindness thesis fails. Religion must be treated as variable and positioned; the claim is replaced by a conditional attribution-mismatch formulation.

Disposition:

```text
UNIVERSAL_THEOLOGICAL_BLINDNESS -> FAILS
RESIDUAL_OBJECT -> BOUNDED CROSS-DOMAIN RELATION
FIELD_STATUS -> EMERGING / COMPONENT-TESTED; INTEGRATED MODEL NOT YET VALIDATED
```

## 9. Stronger model: self-recompiling field with selection, not self-coronation

Taken together with Canon Map recompilation and Legacy Classification, the late-rigor corpus supports a candidate field lifecycle:

```text
propose object / field / theorem
-> preserve documentary lineage
-> confront prior art + empirical constraints
-> identify residual object
-> narrow / retain / merge / demote authority as earned
-> keep superseded or legacy material auditable
```

The key empirical feature is **non-deterministic disposition**:

- Policy Substrate loses autonomous-field status;
- Infrastructural Overshoot retains field status after narrowing;
- SR–AI retains emerging-field status while subordinating older modules;
- SR-IGF states an explicit future contract/merge/demote rule;
- Collapse Religion retains an emerging field while replacing a universal thesis with a conditional one.

Therefore the gauntlet does not merely ratify every SR object.
