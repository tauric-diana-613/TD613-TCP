# Self-Perception and Governance Assay

## Declared versus reconstructed architecture

At each Canon Map cutoff, construct:

- `C_t`: nodes and relations the map itself declares;
- `G_t`: only relations witnessed in artifacts available by that cutoff under a declared view mode.

Compare sets and typed alignments. Do not collapse the result into a distance until a preregistered rubric and closed comparison universe exist.

## Structural-recognition delay

For every later retrospective type assignment, preserve:

- earliest locally witnessed source relation;
- date of source publication/creation;
- later compiler declaration;
- date and source update state of the compiler;
- recognition-lag candidate;
- whether the earlier role was forward-declared;
- whether the later compiler changed rather than merely recognized the relation.

This distinguishes architecture that was latent, architecture that was created retrospectively, and lineages containing both.

## Governance self-application

Extract every source-declared governance constraint and evaluate only eligible witnessed cases. Candidate case families include ordinal namespaces, DOI relationships, version conflicts, map revisions, competing formulations, supersession records, Legacy demotions, and claim-status changes.

A governance document's existence does not prove compliance. A later correction is evidence of operation and evidence that an earlier conflict existed; preserve both.

## Revision-state risk

Platform `source_updated` changes under one DOI/version create revision risk, not a witnessed textual revision unless multiple captures exist. Phase 2 should prioritize mutable authority-bearing records and capture their bytes, update timestamp, checksums, and future differences without overwriting prior captures.
