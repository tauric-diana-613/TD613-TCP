# Surface-Withdrawal Assay

## Purpose

Test how much of a resolved work remains reconstructible after logically removing one publication surface from the evidence set.

## Dimensions

- identity;
- text, with fidelity level;
- lineage;
- conceptual/operational relations;
- chronology;
- authority.

Each dimension is independently evidenced and may be complete, partial, none, or not assessed. No average score is permitted by default.

## Safety and epistemic rules

- simulation only: never delete captures or manifestations;
- never append an actual `UNAVAILABLE` event;
- after-withdrawal evidence may not rely on the withdrawn surface;
- a DOI may preserve identity while full text is lost;
- a full text may survive while its platform chronology or authority declaration is lost;
- metadata descriptions cannot establish complete text recovery;
- authority recovery links to time-indexed authority assertions rather than a boolean;
- the input manifest/crosswalk snapshot must be hashed.

## Phase 1.5 status

The contract is installed. A corpus-wide result would be misleading before work identity and full-text capture are sufficiently resolved. Phase 2 acquisition is required before promotion beyond small bounded examples.
