# Versioned Epistemic System Instrument

## Research object

This instrument tests whether the preserved SignalRupture corpus behaves as a versioned epistemic system without presuming that the behavior was intentionally designed.

The working cycle is a hypothesis, not a canon fact:

```text
publication
  -> cross-surface manifestation
  -> relation formation
  -> retrospective compilation
  -> field formation
  -> empirical tightening
  -> authority redistribution
  -> new canon self-model
  -> repeat
```

Every step must be capable of failing independently. The archive must remain able to conclude that one lineage was planned, another was compiled after the fact, and a third never formed a coherent architecture.

## Governing separation

```text
source assertion
!= archive normalization
!= archive inference
!= derived current view
```

Source publication time, platform creation time, platform update time, capture time, archive observation time, authority assertion time, and effective time are separate clocks. DOI identity and a platform `version` label do not prove byte stability.

## Authority state

For a work, formulation, field, or class at time `t`, authority is represented as:

```text
A_t(o) = (G, C, E, F, K)
```

- `G`: genealogical retention;
- `C`: canonical authority;
- `E`: empirical authority;
- `F`: field-defining authority;
- `K`: controlling formulation.

Unknown and unstated values remain explicit. A class-level Legacy policy cannot silently assign individual works. Preservation never implies authority persistence, and later publication never implies control.

Authority retrieval is append-only across time and scope. A query must return every witnessed authority slice that bears on the subject or corpus-level role. It may produce a derived current view only when a witnessed `CURRENT_CONTROLS` or `SUPERSEDES_SCOPE` path establishes it. Without such a path, overlapping January, April, July, and August anchors remain jointly visible and continuity is reported as unresolved; “latest wins” is not an archive operator.

## Epistemic-object state

For a work or concept:

```text
X_t(o) = (E, V, R, P, L, A)
```

- `E`: existence/custody;
- `V`: visibility;
- `R`: retrievability;
- `P`: provenance integrity;
- `L`: lineage coherence;
- `A`: authority resolution, linked to the full five-axis authority assertion.

The instrument enforces:

```text
E does not imply V
V does not imply R
R does not imply P
P does not imply L
L does not imply A
```

There is no composite state score.

## Latent and retrospective architecture

Two hypotheses remain independently testable:

- `H_L_LATENT_ARCHITECTURE`: roles, sequence, or later works were anticipated contemporaneously or forward-declared.
- `H_R_RETROSPECTIVE_ARCHITECTURE`: later documents assigned roles, compiled fields, reorganized maps, or redistributed authority after the source artifacts already existed.

They are not exclusive. One evidence item may support both, but it receives two separately scoped hypothesis-evidence records rather than one grand verdict.

## Bounded local compiler observations

The current vault witnesses compilation at more than one developmental moment:

- 2025-12-30: STAR compiles four same-day lineage works after one successor was forward-declared;
- 2026-01-12/13: Grand Unified Theory consolidates a core harm lineage, the Authorship Capsule promotes it as a canonical reference, and the Codex compiles a seventy-essay machine-legible discipline;
- 2026-08: later field manuscripts compile earlier surfaces into measurable fields, while Legacy and the unified Governance Stack redistribute authority and resolve conflicts.

This weakens any model in which retrospective compilation begins only with the late field manuscripts. It does not establish one stationary compiler law: the early passes emphasize coherence and machine legibility, while later passes increasingly add measurement, falsifiers, evidence boundaries, and authority changes. Phase-conditioned compiler behavior is therefore a live alternative to a single corpus-wide operator.

## Supplemental compiler and representation observations

Two local-only supplements—`99-ADMIN/phase15_additional_family_findings.json` and `99-ADMIN/phase15_additional_compiler_findings.json`—add concrete bounded cases. Their records are deduplicated into the canonical registries; the supplemental files are inputs, not alternate authorities.

- **Society Blueprint:** five afternoon modules forward-declare roles in the later Blueprint, then the evening compiler claims an eleven-infrastructure governing architecture. One AI module calls AI a harm amplifier rather than a new pillar; the compiler later includes AI among core infrastructures. This is evidence for predeclared modularity and retrospective retyping in one lineage, not proof that “harm amplifier,” “pillar,” and “core infrastructure” share a type system.
- **High-Arousal Content:** v1.1 explicitly says the core argument remains unchanged while adding the first diagnostic metric and calling itself a maturation of the original work. This is a strong formulation-maturation receipt, while body-level preservation remains untested.
- **Conspiracy Shield:** Paper I defines the concept, Paper II claims empirical measurement, Paper III claims generative reproduction, and an umbrella synthesis adds forecasting. The four-stage pipeline is source-declared and chronologically witnessed; data, code, independence, and forecast performance are not locally preserved.
- **Zombie representations:** Zombie Society and Zombie Systems form a reciprocal qualitative/quantitative pair with concrete conceptual edges. A separate Zombie Metrics trilogy was predeclared before the locally retained Parts II and III, but Part I remains unresolved and is never synthesized from a plausible title.
- **Ground Truth Distortion:** the same metric tuple and ten-domain design move from theoretical architecture to empirical presentation to theorem over three days. This is a high-correspondence recompilation candidate; the three surfaces must not be counted as independent confirmations.
- **Policy Substrate:** one record declares an autonomous field, while a later record classifies the same named construct as a specialized framework and research program with empirical gates. Separate concept DOIs and a late in-place update prevent a clean edition or supersession claim.
- **Provenance contamination:** a Logic Cop companion record carries a title/description subject mismatch. The archive preserves the contradiction and the companion predicates without transplanting the description to a more convenient record.

Together these cases sharpen the working cycle: some architectures are forward-declared, some are assembled retrospectively, some change representational register, and some redistribute authority without an explicit supersession chain.

## Typed graphs

The corpus is represented as a multigraph with distinct projections:

- `HISTORICAL`: publication, edition, version, genealogy, and supersession;
- `CONCEPTUAL`: conceptual dependence, complementarity, formalization, and structural recurrence;
- `OPERATIONAL`: measurement, application, compiler roles, and field integration;
- `NAVIGATIONAL`: entry conditions, `read next`, consult, and reader-facing routes;
- `MANIFESTATION`: work/edition/platform/capture relations;
- `AUTHORITY`: canon, control, Legacy, scope, and rehabilitation.

Typed cycles are allowed. A reciprocal conceptual relation is not a historical cycle. A navigation edge cannot be treated as conceptual dependence merely because the reader traverses it.

The executable receipt is `graph-assay-observation/v2`. Each `TYPED_CYCLE` payload must name its `node_ids`, `graph_projections`, and `ordered_edge_ids`; each edge ID resolves to a concrete `relation-assertion/v2` row in `05-OPERATIONS/relations/typed-edges.jsonl`. Optional predicate triples summarize the relation but cannot replace the edge records. Projection-specific `orders` prevent publication order, conceptual precedence, and reader navigation from becoming one synthetic chronology.

## Self-perception assay

Let:

```text
C_t = SR's declared self-model at time t
G_t = archive-reconstructed witnessed graph using a declared cutoff and view mode
```

The archive compares explicit node and edge sets rather than inventing a scalar distance:

- witnessed but unmapped relations;
- declared but not yet supported structures;
- later retrospective type assignments;
- objects not enumerated in a later map;
- candidate recognition lags;
- label, scope, and edge-type mismatches.

`C_t` is not truth. `G_t` is not authorial intent. Retrospective edges cannot leak into a `CONTEMPORANEOUS_ONLY` reconstruction. A hindsight-augmented reconstruction must say so.

## Claim-ceiling and self-application assay

Within a resolved claim lineage, record transitions among:

```text
DECLARED_LAW
CANDIDATE_MECHANISM
SUPPORTED
PARTIAL
NOT_ESTABLISHED
REJECTED_UNIVERSAL
LEGACY
CONTESTED
```

Lexical markers generate candidates only. Every promoted event requires a stable claim atom, scope, source status phrase, evidence-schema namespace, measurement definitions, falsifiers, counterexamples, competing explanations, and prior events where applicable.

Governance coherence is adjudicated rule by rule. A mismatch is not an error unless the source rule exists, applies to the case, is violated by witnessed evidence, and is not explained by a declared revision. Preserve eligible, evaluated, satisfied, violated, and indeterminate sets; do not manufacture a global coherence percentage before the denominator is closed.

## Representation operators

Two operators remain distinct:

```text
T_surface: manifestation register on one platform -> manifestation register on another platform
T_epistemic: narrative/conceptual representation -> formal/theorem/empirical representation
```

For each transformation, compare semantic core, scope, causality, universality, variables, falsifiers, and caveats. Formalization may preserve, narrow, broaden, or inflate a claim.

A future commutation assay requires four witnessed corners. It tests `T_surface` then `T_epistemic` against `T_epistemic` then `T_surface` by dimension. Missing corners are `NOT_TESTABLE`; there is no overall commutation score.

The stylometric publishing family supplies a concrete partial square: a conceptual theory, an operational method, a theory–method architecture, and a Substack “unified edition” candidate. One corner and full-body identity evidence remain absent, so every commutation dimension stays `NOT_TESTABLE`. This is a useful instrument fixture, not a promoted commutation result.

The representation registry also preserves a crucial evidence-control distinction: a claimed conceptual → empirical → theorem sequence may be one intellectual object re-presented under rising authority language. Until bodies, datasets, and lineage are resolved, a theorem surface cannot be counted as independent empirical support for its precursor.

## Route-conditioned retrieval

The navigation graph records router, entry condition, audience/namespace, explicit destination, overlap, fallback, and unresolved endpoints. Multiple entry points are not conflicts when their audiences or scopes differ.

For two routes terminating at the same concept, the archive retains separate route-conditioned epistemic states until path independence is tested. The same endpoint does not automatically imply the same acquired concepts, priors, authority context, or interpretation.

## Surface-withdrawal assay

Surface withdrawal is a logical simulation only. It never deletes a file and never creates a real availability event.

For each removed surface and resolved work, compare baseline and after-withdrawal recoverability across:

```text
identity, text, lineage, relations, chronology, authority
```

Text fidelity distinguishes byte-exact, full-text-equivalent, normalized derivative, excerpt/summary, none, and unknown. Evidence from the withdrawn surface cannot support the after-withdrawal result. The dimensions are never averaged.

## First-appearance provenance

Each structural signature is preregistered as roles, ordered relations, and required components. Results may be:

- lexical only;
- partial structural;
- full structural.

The archive may report `CANDIDATE_EARLIEST_WITHIN_SCANNED_SCOPE`. It may not report a global first unless the search universe is bounded, hashed, complete, and accompanied by an explicit earlier-counterexample search receipt.

## Coverage and gate contract

The coverage ledger records two independent typed axes:

```text
audit_status = IMPLEMENTED | PARTIAL | BLOCKED | SCHEMA_ONLY | HUMAN_GATED
reachability = SCHEMA_ONLY | SEEDED | BOUNDED_EXECUTED | BLOCKED_BY_SOURCE | HUMAN_GATED
```

`source_status_raw` preserves the pre-normalized wording, `implemented_in` identifies concrete outputs, and `blocker_ids` resolve through the entity index. A schema-only instrument is not a failed experiment; a seeded trail is not an adjudicated result; a human gate is not a source outage. All current rows retain `phase_2_run: false`, and no documentation statement opens that gate.
