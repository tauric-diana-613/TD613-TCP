# Rights, Attribution, and Preservation Boundary

## Purpose

This is a private preservation vault. Its purpose is to preserve access, make source relationships retrievable, and support clearly attributed research. It is not a republication project, a claim of authorship, or a claim of conceptual inheritance.

## Mandatory record fields

Every item must preserve, where available:

- title and author/creator exactly as supplied by the source;
- DOI, record URL, platform URL, and version/concept record;
- published date and local retrieval timestamp;
- source-stated access status and license;
- source-provided checksum plus local SHA-256 checksum;
- a clear distinction between the original and every locally generated derivative.

## Public sharing rule

Before this vault is synchronized to TD613-TCP or another public repository, each binary must be checked against its explicit source license and the destination platform’s file/storage limits. If redistribution is not expressly permitted, publish only the metadata, stable citation, checksum, and a retrieval pointer—not the file.

## TD613 boundary

TD613, TD613-TCP, and anyone maintaining this vault do not claim to have authored, originated, derived, endorsed, or been authorized to represent SignalRupture. Their role, if any, is limited to accurately attributed preservation, indexing, and access facilitation.
