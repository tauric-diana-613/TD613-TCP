## Independence membrane

The replica engine may consume only the frozen public artifact bundle already admitted by the parent route. It does not receive preregistration answers or case identifiers. It may not import or call the parent repairability evaluator or seeder.

The harness retains case identity and expected outputs outside the replica boundary, creates the finite mutations using a separately implemented fixture engine, then compares the replica result against both the preregistered expectation and the frozen parent evaluator after the replica has produced its answer.

The finite family is unchanged:

- CLEAN_CONTROL
- SOURCE_BINDING_DRIFT
- AUTHORITY_PROMOTION
- RELATION_CLASS_INFLATION
- REPAIR_RECOVERY_COLLAPSE
- DESTINATION_ENFORCEMENT_PROMOTION
