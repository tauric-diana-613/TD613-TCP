## Abstract

When two scientific worlds terminate in the exact same admitted knowledge graph, $\Pi(G_A) = \Pi(G_B)$, while their underlying research histories diverge, $H_A \neq H_B$, does retaining process history improve an investigator's next experimental decision under an unseen mechanism $M$? Extending the Experiment A existence proof into a preregistered, source-free counterfactual decision study ($48$ calibration/pilot worlds followed by $96$ locked confirmatory worlds across four hidden-world families, six inheritance conditions, and two strict UTF-8 memory budgets of $96$ and $256$ bytes), we demonstrate five coupled results that refuse collapse into a single pro-memory or anti-memory slogan. First, complete permissible history (`II_ARCHIVISTS`, mean $3{,}234.7$ bytes) improves expected research-action utility over terminal-graph amnesia (`I_AMNESIACS`, $0$ historical bytes) by $\Delta U_H = +0.2074$ ($95\%$ quartet-clustered CI $[+0.1207, +0.2920]$, $p < 0.0001$), yet yields **zero net gain** in causal lookalike worlds ($\Delta U_H = +0.0013$) where high-volume, uncalibrated founder trials (`EPOCH_0`) anchor posterior belief away from informative interventions. Second, a calibration-trained discrete Information-Bottleneck capsule (`III_HEIRS`) at $256$ bytes ($111.5$ bytes realized, $29.01\times$ compression) achieves **both noninferiority and superiority** over complete history ($U = 0.9179$ vs. $0.7960$, paired difference $+0.1219$, $95\%$ CI $[+0.0627, +0.1943]$) while reducing out-of-sample adversarial reconstruction of a $2$-bit synthetic sensitive provenance attribute $S$ from $100.0\%$ ($1.9865$ Miller–Madow bits, which survives naive key redaction via lexical and timestamp side-channels) to $25.0\%$ chance ($0.0000$ bits). Third, compression exhibits sharp structural failure boundaries: at $96$ bytes ($81.9$ bytes realized, $39.47\times$ compression), coarse ternary quantization erases continuous suppressor variance in Family A (failing family-specific noninferiority, CI $[-0.0920, +0.0963]$), category-level selective forgetting (`VI_REVISIONISTS`) evicts residual anomalies and collapses Family D predicate invention from $91.67\%$ to $0.0000\%$, and an unwitnessed conditioning-state inversion between calibration and deployment collapses the fixed $256$-byte capsule from $U = 0.9179$ to $U = 0.2342$—far below memoryless ignorance ($0.5887$), instantiating the Finite Admissibility Descent Theorem (FADT) union–intersection gap. Fourth, while fabricated anti-truth narratives (`IV_FALSE_PROPHETS`, $U = 0.3613$) depress raw utility slightly more than authentic histories misattributed from sibling worlds (`V_EXILES`, $U = 0.4482$), misattributed authentic histories induce substantially worse overconfidence when wrong ($0.6634$ vs. $0.4705$, ECE $0.3003$ vs. $0.1455$) because genuine empirical noise variance bypasses forensic smoothness checks; moreover, even an adversarial-skeptic policy (`Post-Hoc E4`) that treats foreign history purely as a hypothesis to test cannot match clean amnesia under a single-probe budget ($0.4656$ vs. $0.5887$), because testing a $1$-vs-$3$ inherited candidate yields at most $H(1/4) = 0.811$ bits against memoryless $2$-vs-$2$ bisection ($1.000$ bit). ## 1. Ingress at the Quotient Seam: From Existence Proof to Counterfactual Consequence

Institutional archives present their terminal consensus as if arrival rendered the corridor disposable. In the preceding Experiment A Four-Crossing Capstone, Universal Atelier proved on four synthetic estates that distinct historical processes $H_A \neq H_B$ can map onto byte-identical admitted terminal graphs under canonical projection:

$$
\Pi(G_A) = \Pi(G_B), \qquad I(\text{World}; \Pi(G)) = 0.
$$

That existence construction established only that graph quotients discard historical path distinctions; it did not establish whether the discarded distinctions help or harm a successor investigator who must act next in an unfamiliar world.

Let $G$ denote the admitted terminal Encyclopedia graph, $H$ the complete permissible synthetic process trace (rejected candidate hypotheses, calibrated micro-trials, temporal retraction ledgers, residual anomalies, uncalibrated founder monographs, and intake provenance metadata), $M \in \mathcal{M}$ the latent mechanism governing future interventions and unseen holdout states, and $a = (p, u, \hat{m}, q, \hat{\psi})$ the successor's composite research decision: choosing one diagnostic probe $p \in \mathcal{P}$, observing the world's response $y \sim P(\cdot \mid M, p)$ (`NOTICE → ACT → WORLD ANSWERS → NAME`), committing to an intervention $u \in \mathcal{U}$, issuing a calibrated mechanism claim $(\hat{m}, q)$ (or `ABSTAIN_UNIDENTIFIABLE`), and optionally proposing a new formal predicate $\hat{\psi}$ when $G$'s vocabulary ruptures.

We measure historical utility contribution and compressed-capsule utility contribution directly against memoryless terminal-graph reasoning:

$$
\Delta U_H = \mathbb{E}\big[U(a(G, H), M)\big] - \mathbb{E}\big[U(a(G), M)\big], \qquad
\Delta U_Z = \mathbb{E}\big[U(a(G, Z), M)\big] - \mathbb{E}\big[U(a(G), M)\big], \quad Z = f_{\text{cal}}(H), \; |Z|_{\text{UTF8}} \le B.
$$

Rather than assuming $\Delta U_H > 0$ or assuming that lossy compression monotonically degrades $\Delta U_H$, our design stages six competing institutional postures over identical terminal graphs $\Pi(G)$ alongside two noncompeting evaluator anchors:

2. **Condition II — `II_ARCHIVISTS` (mean $3{,}234.7$ bytes):** Receives $\Pi(G)$ plus complete permissible synthetic history $H$, pooling replicate-weighted historical log-evidence across all epochs alongside temporal and residual ledgers.

## 5. Counterpoint of the Forbidden Wing and the Final Challenge

To test Section XI's challenge—whether preservation and deletion exhaust scientific inheritance, whether authoritative history disables inquiry, and whether any fixed capsule can survive changing worlds—we paired four exploratory investigations with adversarial counter-constructions and ten falsification challenges (`receipts/forbidden-wing-receipt.json`, `receipts/adversarial-challenges-receipt.json`):

1. **The Misinherited Truth vs. Explicit Fabrication (`E1` & Stealth Counter-Case):** Why is an authentic foreign archive (`V_EXILES`) epistemically treacherous? In `IV_FALSE_PROPHETS`, fabricated smoothness (`within_cell_var = 0.002`) triggered forensic skepticism, tempering prior trust and allowing $87.50\%$ surprise recovery after a contradictory probe (`ConfWrong = 0.4705`, ECE $= 0.1455$). Constructing a noise-matched stealth fabricator (`E1 Counter-Case`, `within_cell_var = 0.018` + probe-aliasing slot selection) defeated forensic screening and drove wrong-claim confidence to **`0.6958`** ($U = 0.3095$), proving that **calibrated realistic variance—not moral authenticity—is the mechanism of misplaced institutional certainty**. *(Challenge 10 also uncovered and controlled a theoretical $\log_2(4/3) = 0.415$-bit anti-truth slot-exclusion correlation in Condition IV via a Zero-Leak Uncorrelated Prophet Control, $U = 0.4645 < 0.5887$.)*
2. **Self-Falsification of Interrogative Skepticism Under Finite Action Budgets (`Post-Hoc E4`):** After `PILOT_48`, we hypothesized that treating inherited history strictly as a falsifiable conjecture (`inquisitive_skeptic_mode`) would rescue `V_EXILES` back to `I_AMNESIACS`.
