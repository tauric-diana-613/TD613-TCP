### II. NKS experimental discipline
Stephen Wolfram’s methodology emphasizes systematically running many comparatively simple programs and examining raw behavior rather than selecting only a few anticipated patterns. This design preserves complete state histories and failure cases, and treats beautiful visualizations as hypotheses until the update rules are operational. See [NKS: simple programs](https://www.wolframscience.com/nks/p23--how-do-simple-programs-behave/) and [NKS: how discoveries were made](https://www.wolframscience.com/nks/p111--how-the-discoveries-in-this-chapter-were-made/).

We **do not** identify state confluence with causal invariance. Piskunov supplies explicit counterexamples: [Confluence and Causal Invariance](https://bulletins.wolframphysics.org/2020/11/confluence-and-causal-invariance/). We **do not** infer computational irreducibility from a visually complex trace or finite enumeration.
