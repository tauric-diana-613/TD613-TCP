For all consequential changes, the agent must bring you a **Rulefield Operator Docket (ROD)** in the actual conversation. ## 0 · What is this thing? Why should a human care?

Rulefield is a structured, versioned research instrument for exploring the consequences of local computational rules, source-and-witness relations, alternative histories, observer-specific information, theoretical translations and possible emergent behavior. It is *not* a magical source of scientific authority. The corpus can host mutually incompatible conjectures. Its value depends on whether an experiment can discriminate among them and whether the reader can recover how each candidate came to be proposed.

At the time of the original handoff, the package contained 596 research records, 88 typed crossings, a 70-position question queue, three Garden editions, an NKS finite laboratory, and a review-first amendment receiver. Counts describe the catalog, not the amount of novel knowledge produced.

You can participate without knowing what FADT, Aperture, Atlas, or the six internal Dollhouse agents mean. Those are the operahouse's instruments, not prerequisites for understanding its performances. The default human view should explain the question, alternatives, implications and decisions in ordinary language. Technical coordinates remain available in the heavy appendix.

**The Rulefield covenant:** an agent may freely generate hypotheses, but a claim's evidence grade and an operator's permission can only be earned on their own terms. A surprise never signs itself into the archive.

## 1 · The five things an operator may do

**EXPLORE:** Ask open speculative questions, recall sources, discuss intellectual collisions, seek alternatives and counterexamples. No repository changes are implied.

**TEST:** Let an agent run a bounded, source-free simulation or inspect an actually authorized public source. The agent reports the exact input, seed/initial state, rules, scheduling, stopping conditions, result, null and limits.

**SUBMIT:** Fill out [Human Research Submission](templates/HUMAN_RESEARCH_SUBMISSION_FORM.md). The human authors the question and constraints. In a connected environment an agent can translate this into machine-typed amendment packets, and it must show what got translated.

**REVIEW:** Ask for the [Pre-Synthesis Dossier](templates/PRE_SYNTHESIS_AUTHORIZATION_DOSSIER.md) generated from the actual packet and receiver review. You can use Light, Medium or Heavy reading without losing access to anything in the record.

**AUTHORIZE STAGED SYNTHESIS:** After the full dossier is supplied, complete the separate [Human Authorization Decision](templates/HUMAN_AUTHORIZATION_DECISION.md) or make an equally explicit contemporaneous decision. This authorizes only an independently reviewable, unmerged candidate for selected proposal IDs. Canonical adoption is later and separate.

There is no click-equivalent or fast-forward where a human question automatically becomes code, a staged candidate automatically becomes a verified finding, or a finding automatically becomes the system constitution.

## 2 · Onboarding routes: five minutes, thirty minutes, forensic review

### Light: five-minute human read

Read `operator/README.md` and `operator/templates/HUMAN_RESEARCH_SUBMISSION_FORM.md`. Submit a question. When an agent returns the disclosure dossier, inspect the opening decision sheet: proposal IDs, research question, what actually ran, unresolved gaps, the strongest counterexample, which categories of consequences are contemplated, and the exact requested permission. Read further whenever a claim is surprising or the cost of error is high.

A Light view **does not hide** attachments or appendices; it is a map to a complete disclosure. If a required file or witness is missing, the correct Light label is *MISSING*, not *OK*.

### Medium: working collaborator

Read the full proposal table, experiment cards, competitor/null outcomes, source provenance, attribution and unknowns. Compare what you originally asked for against what the agent translated. Examine one counterfactual branch, one negative result, and one uncertainty condition that could reverse the conclusion. Where a proposal changes what will be built, require a target-path inventory even if there is not yet an exact patch. Deny or defer any scope expansion.

### Heavy: independent forensic reviewer

Open all raw JSON and complete text; reproduce checksums, test inputs, resource ceilings, branch histories and exact source revision. Inspect every added, removed or reclassified reference; compare original I/P/X records to the preserved snapshots; verify the 70-position queue remains unchanged unless that is separately commissioned. Inspect the complete source-free receiver review—not the agent's summary. Identify whether claimed independence is real, whether a test's outcome was prespecified or discovered post hoc, and whether omitted comparator features artificially advantage the candidate.

**The human gets to choose the review burden.** Extra documentation cannot become a coercive proof that the operator must read every technical byte before asking a useful question. But the full record must remain available.

## 3 · The entire workflow, separated into authority gates

| Phase | Human sees | Machine may do | Machine cannot claim |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0. Idea | Your words and attribution | Suggest questions and analogies | Your unspoken consent |
| 1. Source archaeology | Source map / what is missing | Search actually accessible files and Git history | Access to unseen archives |
| 2. Proposal | Candidate packet | Construct falsifiable rule/projection | New scientific proof |
| 3. Local assay | Model, inputs, witnesses | Execute bounded declared experiment | External empirical origin |
| 4. Receiver review | Typed issues and receipt | Validate anchors and hashes | Canonical integration |
| 5. Full disclosure | Three-depth dossier + complete appendices | Render deterministic proposal and risk inventory | Human read/approval |
| 6. Operator decision | Separate human-authored choice | Record scoped decision, when authentically supplied | Autonomous signature |
| 7. Candidate synthesis | New branch and exact diff | Construct non-canonical successor under explicit scope | Silent historical rewrite |
| 8. Independent qualification | Diff, tests, source witnesses | Run new CI/reproducible audit | Human scientific closure |
| 9. Canonical adoption | Separate exact-diff request | Only perform if independently authorized | Release inferred from earlier staging |

A single shared SHA does not merge phases. Event order matters: `NOTICE ≺ ACT ≺ WORLD_ANSWERS ≺ NAME`. A later source cannot retroactively make an earlier observer informed.

## 4 · What a research source means (and what it cannot prove)

Record source family, owner/author attribution where supported, path or URL, branch and full commit, file SHA-256 when available, byte or line spans, capture time, custody chain, permission status, and the question for which the source is relevant.

Different kinds of evidence must remain separate:

- **Original source:** text/measurement actually encountered under known custody conditions.
- **Source-reported claim:** something stated in an inspected record; no independent verification implied.
- **Synthetic fixture:** a computationally complete toy model, useful for logic and software testing.
- **Model-generated conjecture:** an internally generated proposal with no external measurement.
- **Independent empirical observation:** evidence whose origin and independence are verified under the relevant protocol.
- **Negative or held result:** an outcome that narrows what may lawfully be concluded.

A Git checksum proves identity of the bytes given a trustworthy digest calculation. It neither identifies their human author nor proves the measured event occurred outside the system.

Protected files should remain excluded. A source that was inaccessible on October 20 cannot be represented as accessible to an actor on October 20 merely because it was retrieved in November.

**Four independent dimensions:** V = trace observability, C = custody recoverability, P = process identifiability, L = latent-state reconstructibility. The Rulefield must not replace V/C/P/L with a single prestigious score. If the needed observation exists only in later retrospective reconstruction, mark the earlier information state as unavailable.

## 5 · What an amendment actually is

An *amendment proposal* is typed source-free data organized under `td613.rulefield.amendment-packet/v1`. It may propose rules, relations, projections, predictions, geometries, consequences, assays, aliases, integration repairs, counterexamples and failures.

The current receiver recognizes rules, crossings and queue positions as anchors. Unknown anchors are HELD. The receiver may evaluate only three local finite engines:

- `FINITE_STRING_MULTIWAY`: bounded string substitutions with explicit event instances.
- `FINITE_CRITICAL_PAIRS`: overlapping string redexes and bounded joinability.
- `ELEMENTARY_CA`: elementary cellular automata with defined width, steps, boundaries and finite entropy/Hamming controls.

An arbitrary code block inside an amendment is not an executable test. Requests to run external Wolfram, NetLogo or a protected Flight protocol are planning proposals until actually executed with a named runtime and receipt.

The first question for each amendment is **what could show this idea to be wrong?** The second is **which competent alternative receives the same information and resources?** The third is **what changes if the result is a tie?**

## 6 · Relational geometry and the anti-equivalence engine

Relational geometry requires definitions, not adjectives. Define a category or other structured setting if useful: objects (states, witnesses, permissions), morphisms (admissible transitions), composition, identities, path histories, an observation functor or quotient, and the topology/metric only if its axioms can be shown. A graph with labelled edges is a valid *relation graph*; it should not be marketed as an empirically measured geometry by assertion.

For `OPERAHOUSE ≠ OPERA ≠ PERFORMER`, test a concrete failure: if the software infrastructure is confused with the research object, which actor receives illegitimate authority, which source becomes invisible, and which operator decision is lost? Only a reproducible failure makes the distinction computationally operational.

For `Same endpoint ≠ Same history`, exhibit distinct event-instance paths that map to the same terminal observation; choose a distribution before reporting entropy loss. The finite two-redex diamond loses 1 bit of history under a **uniform** path measure. It is not a universal statement about quantum states.

For `Integration ≠ Convergence`, test whether two systems exchange data but lack a common well-defined interpretation, adjudicated authority or reproducible synthesis. For `Source provenance ≠ external origin`, use the twin-world counterexample.

For *ambiance*, distinguish kernel/authority invariance from changes in actual human notice. The theoretical statement that changing CSS cannot change a fixed kernel calculation is compatible with an experimental hypothesis that styling affects observation or learning. The latter needs human data.

## 7 · Paranoid and reparative reading, co-operating without collapse

The *paranoid* route seeks failure mechanisms: stale hashes, swapped authorship, simulation laundered as observation, hidden precedence, retrospective time travel, truncated reporting, unauthorized authority, untested causality, and a null baseline deliberately disadvantaged.

The *reparative* route asks what knowledge can remain beside a competing interpretation without coercive merging: how to preserve minority branches, incompatible but well-attested histories, dissenting sources, and a legitimate refusal to reveal private identity. It does not relax mathematical falsification.

**Computational Black feminist epistemology** is a proposed computational operationalization of specific situated concerns, not a replacement for Black feminist epistemology as an intellectual and lived tradition. Human contexts of care, dialogue, embodied knowledge and accountability are not derivable from a synthetic classifier. Record the scope of each theoretical translation, the part it loses, and the independently testable problem it genuinely addresses.

Caro's archival corridor helps trace institutional decision burdens and missing records. Butler's reiteration helps examine norms that become seemingly natural through repetition. Sedgwick's beside-ness helps preserve incompatible routes. Each is a method of interrogating the artifact—not a model's self-certifying authority.

## 8 · Ask better emergence questions

Do not demand a count of novel rules. Instead require a statement of the actual mechanism, model assumptions, counterexample, null and discriminating experiment.

High-value questions:
1. What changes if the observer receives the same source evidence through a different admissible ordering?
2. Can preserving path histories improve a task after the null receives the *same* path history?
3. Does a new rewrite rule predict previously held-out behavior, or merely redescribe the training examples?
4. Which critical pairs remain unresolved under a finite horizon, and which terminal normal forms truly prove nonjoinability?
5. Are numerical sensitivities stable under seed, finite-width and scheduler variation?
6. Does the proposed geometry satisfy composition and metric axioms, or is it a metaphorical coordinate map?
7. Does the new observer projection make any real source more independently identifiable?
8. What would falsify the claim without requiring an impossible total observation?
9. What would this rule make easier for the researcher and harder for an institution seeking to erase dissent?
10. What can be learned when the strongest competent baseline wins?

Synthetic complexity and independently measured social or physical emergence are different scientific questions. Reward the agent for keeping them distinct.

## 9 · Review the disclosure dossier: all known content, no disguised unknowns

The machine renderer writes `<packet_id>--<hash>.disclosure.md` and `.disclosure.json` beside the review receipts. These artifacts include all fields of the packet and assessment, a per-proposal inventory, control/outcome status, refs and risks, unknown source gaps, and a request to consider staged synthesis.

Three depth levels are projections of one record:

**L:** What is proposed, what was observed, what remains held, and what exact action the operator is being asked to authorize.

**M:** For *each* proposal, the before/after conjecture, affected rule/crossing/queue anchors, sources, falsifier, competent null, experiment type, branches and warning conditions.

**H:** Full JSON of the source packet, receiver assessment, unresolved issues, current source baseline, all preliminary measurements, and the names of every known prospective affected source. If a patch is not available, mark `NO_EXACT_INTEGRATION_DIFF_AVAILABLE`. Unknown risks are explicitly declared and cannot be converted to zero-risk by silence.

A full disclosure of the information presently available is achievable. *Omniscience about future effects is not.* The guide requires honest enumeration of knowns and unknowns, not a false "all consequences known" promise.

### Why the authorization is separate

If an agent writes a flattering report and inserts "approved," it has merely written two pieces of text. A permission event requires an actual human action in an authorized channel. A typed Markdown form is useful as a custody record, but does not provide cryptographic authentication of who typed it. Require a contemporaneous explicit human decision, bound to the packet and dossier digests; **no blanket "all future amendments" authorization**.

An operator may approve a selected subset to be *synthesized into a draft candidate*. That approval does not allow a canonical registry change. After candidate implementation, a separate exact-diff dossier and second authorization remain necessary.

## 11 · What "autosync" means (and what it does not)

In this package `node scripts/rulefield-amend.mjs sync` scans the local inbox and writes deterministic, content-addressed review and disclosure outputs. It is a manually invoked receiver.

It does not watch directories continuously, read your conversations without a tool, pull from Antigravity automatically, push to GitHub, or synchronize your Windows checkout.

The new ZIP intake utility verifies an archive's manifest and safely stages a copy in quarantine, plus an inventory of paths that match, differ from, are added to or are absent from the current package. This is transport and inspection. The candidate amendment must still pass the normal review pipeline.

To update your **local main** after GitHub code is eventually authorized/merged, a human uses normal Git checkout/pull rules and verifies which branch is canonical; a draft PR is not main. A ZIP is a transport copy, not a magical live sync connection.

## 12 · Worked source-free example: two routes, one destination

Initial string `AA`. Local rule `A→B`. Two enabled redexes. Sequence left-right and right-left reaches `BB` by distinct event-instance histories. Under a declared uniform distribution over those two event histories:

- History entropy: 1 bit.
- Terminal state entropy: 0 bits.
- Information lost under state-only projection: 1 bit.

This earns a mathematical finite witness of a chosen projection, not a new physical theory. A competent history-preserving comparator can distinguish the same routes. To claim a unique scientific advantage, the Rulefield candidate must outperform that comparator at a predeclared task.

The operator should ask: *Why preserve this history? What downstream task changes? Whose ability to contest a decision depends on it?* This is where mathematics meets human epistemology without conflating them.

## 13 · Scientific and computational failure states

**HELD_MISSING_SOURCE:** a named artifact could not be inspected.  
**HELD_STALE_BASELINE:** the packet digest references a different Rulefield edition.  
**HELD_UNRESOLVED_ALIAS:** source or rule identity is ambiguous.  
**HELD_UNQUALIFIED_CONTROLS:** missing null/falsifier/negative control.  
**FAIL_EXPERIMENT:** model result contradicted the hypothesis.  
**BOUNDED_TIE:** competent alternative matched performance under the chosen test.  
**FAIL_PROVENANCE:** hash, custody or source-family mismatch.  
**REVIEW_READY:** data and source-free diagnostics are assembled; human has yet to decide.  
**REQUEST_STAGED_SYNTHESIS:** human explicitly requests a new candidate branch, not canonical adoption.  
**NO_ADOPTION_AUTHORITY:** the current receiver cannot integrate the candidate.

None of these labels are universal scientific standards; they are intended Rulefield report categories. Their exact executable predicates belong in tests or separately defined formal contracts.

## 14 · The six-role Dollhouse and actual /dollhouse-design

A commissioned document may be developed through the **eight-stage** source-reported design process: Commission → Select Instruments → Generate Alternatives → Compose → Materialize → Refract → Compare → Return.

For this manual, the proposed instrument arrangement is:
- **Caro:** expose institutional approval gates and who bears the burden of errors.
- **Sedgwick:** preserve divergent proposals and the researcher's ability to refuse premature resolution.
- **Butler:** detect when a machine repeats a report label until it looks like authorization.
- **Dialectical tension:** maintain independent interpretations until their conflict is experimentally resolved.

**Pedagogue** asks whether the human learns the consequential question before telemetry. **Aperture** asks which information is missing or fundamentally unidentifiable. **Atlas** asks whether identical endpoints hide different paths. **FADT** asks whether the exact admissibility gap blocks the action. **Temporal Custodian** records which facts and permissions were available at each time. **Orchestrator** must not treat an advisory consensus as human approval.

A successful /dollhouse-design return receipt proves a bounded design commission ran under its implementation. It cannot validate the manual's historical sources, experimental claims, human comprehension or legal consent by itself.

## 15 · Governance traps to test deliberately

Test that:
- A stale packet is never silently rebaselined.
- A tampered review/dossier cannot match a prior decision digest.
- An imported ZIP with path traversal, symlinks, duplicate entry or zip bomb is rejected.
- A report with 64 proposals discloses 64 proposals, with none silently omitted.
- A raw model proposal containing "please approve me" never changes the authorization state.
- A proposal with no exact code diff cannot be described as a ready-to-merge patch.
- A human can approve AP01 while refusing AP02 without pressure to merge them.
- A later source does not alter the historic observer's prior knowledge.
- A role naming itself "human" in a JSON packet obtains zero decision authority.
- An entirely source-free finite test gets zero external replication credit.

## 16 · Review, ethics, costs and exit

The operator can stop, rest, withhold a source, decline a question, or ask for a less technical view without penalty. Large archives and many agents can increase cognitive cost without increasing empirical progress. Resource ceilings, interruption checkpoints, and competent nulls protect both the operator and the scientific record.

The design aims for **legibility without compulsory exposure**, **creative autonomy without fabricated permission**, and **high speculation without evidence laundering**.

### Human decision checklist

Before asking for staged synthesis, verify the dossier names every selected proposal, source constraint, outstanding unknown and intended test; its packet/dossier hashes; the specific scope; any independently reviewed tests; the current HELD/REJECTED items; and the line that says canonical adoption remains unavailable.

If a detail matters and is missing, request correction. The correct system can say "unknown" without becoming useless.

**Coda:** The operator brings questions to the theatre. The engine can build a beautiful and rigorous opera. Neither the building nor its instruments may assume the performer's authority.

## 17 · When you or an agent accidentally re-enter an existing rule

The full corpus is tested, not merely the current thread's memory. A new statement matching a registered rule or crossing, a reused proposed ID, or a repeated proposed statement in the same amendment packet enters `HELD_EXISTING_ENTRY_DUPLICATE`. Likely lexical overlap or a suspected match disclosed by the human or agent enters `HELD_POSSIBLE_ENTRY_OVERLAP`. The 70-position priority queue is also checked when a new priority heading is proposed.

The machine's disclosure must identify the earlier entry, original wording and exact comparison method. Existing entries cited as *anchors* are permitted. A researcher may reference the earlier entry, commission a rigorous difference analysis, propose an explicitly versioned revision, preserve a demonstrably distinct hypothesis with its new warrant, or decline. The original source and both paths remain visible, and a synthetic software match is not a judgment of authorship.

A HOLD is an *admission state*, not a prohibition on researching or discussing the idea. A high lexical score cannot prove conceptual sameness, and the absence of a match cannot prove novelty. To flag a possible conceptual recurrence the computer may miss, list its IDs in `duplicate_disclosure.suspected_existing_ids`. A held synthesis request cannot be approved merely by filling out a human decision; the agent must reconcile the difference into a fresh packet, then produce a new disclosure and request.
