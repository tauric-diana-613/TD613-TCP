# 𝌋 Frozen Nomenclature Contract — Version 1

**Version:** `td613.rulefield.nomenclature-lock/v1` · **State:** FROZEN_V1_COMPATIBILITY_CONTRACT

This is a source-free compatibility freeze of **terms, identifier conventions and decision semantics**, not proof that the operator approved the academic titles or any scientific hypotheses. Later revisions must publish a versioned migration, not silently rewrite existing records.

## Stable identifiers

- **RF-001…RF-080** — submitted *field* IDs, not executable rules.
- **Q01…Q70** — inherited priority coordinates; frozen original ordering unless separately authorized.
- **RF-OD-0001** — this first human decision docket.
- **RF-SUB-0001** — future human submission identifiers.
- **RF-DEC-0001** — prospective human decision reference; never minted to assert a decision that never happened.
- **RF-AP-0001** — example proposed amendment packet ID; historical AP01/legacy IDs are preserved unrenamed.

`Rulefield ≠ Rule ≠ Priority` is an authority/type invariant, not a novel physics theorem.

## Frozen decision vocabulary

`APPROVE_PROVISIONAL_RESEARCH`, `APPROVE_SELECTED_FOR_FURTHER_REVIEW`, `REQUEST_REVISIONS`, `HOLD_SELECTION`, `DECLINE_PROPOSAL`.

These **review outcomes cannot merge, deploy, authorize protected Flight or admit an executable rule into the canonical registry**. The separate source-free staged-synthesis and exact-diff authorization pathway remains in force.

## Naming safety

The first issue of a docket is content-bound to its source revision, original expressions and computed digest. A corrected item means a **new docket edition/digest**, retaining the old one. Human decisions reference exact digest and selected proposal IDs. New agents may render a compact or interactive review but must always expose the complete original and all 80 candidates through the durable attached document.

**This contract freezes names and semantics for v1; it freezes no human consent.**
