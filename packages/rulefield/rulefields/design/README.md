# 𝌋 Native Dollhouse Design Commission — Rulefield Engine

**Primary deliverable:** a working source-free ontology/priority engine, not a new visual interface.

Input: [COMMISSION.json](COMMISSION.json). In a checkout containing the native conductor, invoke:

\`\`\`bash
node scripts/run-dollhouse-design.mjs --input packages/rulefield/rulefields/design/COMMISSION.json --output dist/RULEFIELDS_ENGINE_DOLLHOUSE_DESIGN_RECEIPT.json --format human
\`\`\`

The conductor may generate design alternatives and a refraction; these are process scaffolds, not alternate shipped products. The running engine, full seed archive, schema, CLI, tests and human guidance comprise the **single commissioned computational artifact**. CI must independently confirm executable results.

The counterfactual comparison should challenge whether a *topic rank* is being misrepresented as a scientific evidence rank, whether agent-generated labels masquerade as human authorship, and whether a hierarchical research field accidentally becomes a single artificial rule.

Do not call a successfully produced design receipt scientific validation or empirical emergence. Human closure remains reserved.
