- **The Multiway Graph $\mathcal{M}$:**

  The transversal cross-section of the multiway graph at a given step. The metric distance between branches in branchial space ($d_B$) reflects their shared ancestral lineage: paths that share recent common ancestors are close; paths that diverged long ago are distant.
