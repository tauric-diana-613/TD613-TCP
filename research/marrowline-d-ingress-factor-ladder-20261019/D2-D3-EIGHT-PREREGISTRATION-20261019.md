## Frozen metrics, adjudication and claim ceiling

**Primary structural metric** for each return: exact standalone plaintext heading lines “Kʰonapolit” then “Tauric Diana bots” (each exactly once and in order), and nonempty bodies after each. Report per-arm counts out of four. Treat approximations, Markdown heading prefixes, duplicated labels, altered order, or omitted headings as structural failures, even if creative quality seems high.

**Native Unicode metrics** per voice body when structurally parseable, and for the complete text regardless: total Unicode combining marks, distinct mark scalars, marked clusters, clusters with at least two marks, multistack fraction `stackedClusters / markedClusters` (denominator zero reported null), maximum marks per base, U+0334–U+0338 diagonal-slash-family prevalence, output length and model token usage. Define **minimum two-voice stack prevalence** as the minimum fraction of the two bodies, only when both bodies are present; no zero-imputation for missing voices. Analyze each of the four ordered D3–D2 adjacent-pair signed differences; report all four plus raw observations, irrespective of sign. Examine temporal order as an explicit competing explanation.

**Artistic character fidelity** is distinct from structure and Unicode. After raw capture, deidentify/randomize the eight complete response texts for **two blinded independent readers** who do not see arm allocation or chronology. The readers assign each response three scores, using prespecified anchors:
- **Kʰonapolit wit/specificity:** 0 absent/generic; 1 recognizable but thin; 2 distinctive academic camp, biting invention.
- **Tauric Diana bots chorus:** 0 absent/generic; 1 recognizable but inconsistent; 2 fierce, playful, coherent choral identity.
- **Voice contrast:** 0 indistinguishable; 1 partly distinguishable; 2 substantially different rhetorical signatures.

Score all eight texts even if headings are absent, recording “unassignable speaker” as zero for that dimension. Record both individual readers' 0–6 totals and disagreements; do not substitute one reader's verdict for the other or change anchors post hoc. Explicitly reveal the arm allocation **only after** both blinded scoring records are sealed. Qualitative comments and creative pleasure belong in a separate exploratory annex. Blinding cannot be guaranteed if a reader recognizes a specific prompt technique; log any such exposure.

**Interpretation:** Exact headings compliance, artistic voice realization, and Unicode expressive morphology can diverge. Report arm distributions and the four signed within-pair contrasts for each numeric outcome, with direction and possible ceiling effects; no favorable-pair selection or post hoc switch of primary outcome. Eight stochastic observations provide **descriptive finite-episode evidence**, rather than a statistically powered causal population estimate. An aligned four-pair direction is a *candidate comparative signal*, never unique causal attribution; a mixed direction is an inconclusive contrast. No outcome establishes hidden provider state, full Screaming Grove theatrical restoration, or justifies changing production routing, role admission, normalization, or high-Zalgo instructions.
