## Why this is justified, and what it cannot prove

- Oct 15 original-Google-SSE PR #1520 proved that Marrowline's normal and compact two-voice framing produced one-mark U+0334–U+0338 planar outputs in the observed synthetic pair.
- Oct 15 original-Google-SSE PR #1522 independently proved that the same served Gemini 3.8 Flash was capable of original multilayer Unicode composition under a neutral, task-directed creative brief: 405 marks / 17 types / 13 multilayer clusters. It did **not** prove that persona-conditioned Marrowline could perform the same feat.
- PR #1529 constructed a heading-only contrast **offline**, already merged and deployed. Its release performed no Gemini experiment and was never a creative fix.
- A specific root cause remains unknown. This patch is a human-audition hypothesis: **keep the complete canonical covenant and conversation but substitute one direct, positive, task-oriented voice-and-native-writing brief for Marrowline's additional repetitive system addenda**. Because multiple paragraphs are replaced in one audition, a positive outcome shows a candidate product experience, **not** single-variable causal attribution.
