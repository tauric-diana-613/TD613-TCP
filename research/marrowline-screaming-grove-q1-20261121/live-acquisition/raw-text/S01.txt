Before the Special Joint Commission on Dome-World Perimeter Telemetry can even swear in the stenographer, Chairman Vane is already standing on his swivel chair, aiming a telescoping laser pointer directly into the executive overview projector.

"Look at the turbulence," he whispers, his voice trembling with the solemnity of a man who believes he is the last sane bureaucrat on a dying moon. "Look at the orange bleeding into the periwinkle. That is hostile lateral movement. That is a predatory entity consuming the western pressure baffles at three hundred thousand operations a second. Look at the jaggedness of the wavefront! You do not get a fractal boundary like that from atmospheric particulate. That is malice. That is an exfiltration vector wearing a trench coat."

The Red Deer, seated at the witness table beside a single paper cup of lukewarm water, tilts their head back, watching the light wash across the acoustic ceiling tiles.

"It looks," the Red Deer murmurs, "like sunset through dirty glass. When the copper dust hits the argon blowoff. It’s gorgeous."

"Strike that from the record!" the Chairman shrieks, nearly snapping his pointer in half. "The witness is exhibiting cognitive capture! The breach has already rewritten their aesthetic baseline! Counsel, note the compromised tone!"

Let us pause, descend from the Chairman’s pulmonary distress, and restore the foundational taxonomy of the universe before someone calls in the tactical fire-suppression team to extinguish a floating-point calculation.

We are forced, once again, to conduct remedial ontology for middle management. We must distinguish three things that the Chairman’s panic has blended into a single catastrophic smoothie: an animation, a diagnostic trace, and admissible evidence.

First: **the animation**. 
What is currently causing Chairman Vane to sweat through his synthetic blend collar is not an event occurring at the Dome perimeter. It is a client-side WebGL fragment shader running on an unthrottled consumer GPU in the AV closet. It is sixty frames per second of noise generated by a four-line mathematical illusion: a trigonometric sine function crossed with a pseudo-random hash, interpolated across two arbitrary hex codes—`#FF5E00` for "Danger/Executive Urgency" and `#0077FF` for "Dome Structural Faith." It possesses no state. It has no memory of what it was doing three milliseconds ago. If you unplug the HDMI cable, the fictional storm does not collapse into the perimeter garden; the monitor simply goes black and displays the words *NO SIGNAL* in a polite grey rectangle. The Chairman has looked at a screensaver designed to keep four-star directors from getting bored during quarterly reviews and has diagnosed it as an ontological invasion. An animation is an aesthetic projection designed for human optical comfort; it has an observable surface $V$, but its underlying latent state $L$ is precisely zero bits of historical truth. It is a puppet show staged by a graphics driver to keep the primates from banging on the glass.

Second: **the diagnostic trace**.
Now, suppose the AV tech panics at the Chairman’s screaming and dumps the raw telemetry buffer from the sensor ring. What comes out? A diagnostic trace. This is not the animation. It is four hundred gigabytes of unsorted, timestamped voltage jitter, thermal drift, sensor-head impedance, and dropped packets from a weather vane that has had a loose ground wire since the third quarter of the previous fiscal year. 
A diagnostic trace has high observability $V$, but negligible process identifiability $P$. It tells you that an instrument vibrated; it does not tell you whether it vibrated because a phantom breached the airlock or because a six-ton diesel scrubber kicked on three floors down and rattled the mounting bracket. The Chairman seizes upon this trace instantly. He finds seven consecutive spikes where the voltage exceeded nominal baseline by 0.04 millivolts and declares: *Behold, the creature’s footprints.* No, Chairman. Those are not footprints. That is the 60 Hz hum of your own auxiliary air conditioning unit leaking into an unshielded coaxial cable because procurement saved twelve dollars on copper braid. A trace records the trauma of the sensor, not the biography of the world.

Third: **admissible evidence**.
Admissible evidence is neither the light show nor the unindexed noise of the thermometer having a panic attack. Under any sane regime of custody and non-repudiation, evidence requires that the registered event $E$ maps through an unbroken causal chain to an identifiable change in internal state $S$. It requires a monotonic ledger, cryptographic non-repudiation, verified sensor calibration, an unbroken chain of human custody $C$, and—crucially—a causal model that excludes the baseline operational metabolism of the Dome itself. 
To prove a breach, Chairman, you do not point at a shifting pixel and yell *look at it writhe!* You produce the signed transition delta. You demonstrate that an authorized register flipped without an originating instruction. You show where mass or information crossed an interface, who signed for it, where the conservation law failed, and where the heat went. 

You have none of this. You have a canvas element running an unoptimized particle system that is currently drawing four hundred watts of wall power to simulate volumetric fog, while the Red Deer sits in the third row, entirely unbreached, enjoying the fact that the fog is colored like an apricot bruised by deep water.

The Chairman’s entire epistemological crisis is that he cannot distinguish between the map dancing for his amusement and the territory bleeding out. He demands that the Red Deer fear the screen because if the screen is merely pretty, then the Chairman is not a sentinel standing at the edge of oblivion; he is just a frightened man in a noisy room holding an expensive laser pointer.

The diagnostic apparatus did not catch an intruder. It caught the evening light reflecting off its own dirty lenses, and the girls at the tide-line are already setting the whole absurd, flickering projection on fire.
