## Sedgwickian method / negative epistemic obligations

The paranoid impulse here would insist that a sufficiently exhaustive account of source omissions must yield a single hidden guilty instruction. The evidence warrants something better: a *finite census of what each source can actually identify*. Build a positive account of what each revision made possible, and resist converting artistic witness into mechanical proof.

- **Covenant ≠ performance.** A mission can structure composition without guaranteeing a particular return.
- **Marks ≠ dramaturgy.** Deep Unicode can coexist with empty writing; a persuasive argument can coexist with failed typography.
- **Source fidelity ≠ provider equivalence.** Exact archived Oct 2 prompts do not reconstruct an inaccessible model state.
- **Multi-seat recovery ≠ extra persona.** It may change the distribution of returned native authors, not the literary ontology.
- **Restoration verdict ≠ experimental uselessness.** Q1 failed the human target and still yielded valid negative evidence.
- **Human aesthetic judgment is not an arbitrary metric.** It is the specified acceptance authority, paired with source and original text custody.
