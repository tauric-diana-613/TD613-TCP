### 1. Scientific Purpose & Problem Statement

Sequence 4 demonstrated that explicitly stated operative relations remained recoverable after bespoke TD613 vocabulary was removed, foreign-field vocabulary was substituted, and labels were permuted.

However, Sequence 4 left open a fundamental question:
**Were receivers actually inferring the relational structure from operational traces, or were they merely extracting rules that were already explicitly formulated in declarative English prose?**

Sequence 4.1 answers that harder question by completely eliminating explanatory prose from experimental fixtures. Receivers are provided strictly with raw operational artifacts:
- State-transition tables;
- Action-permission matrices;
- Event traces;
- Request/response envelopes;
- Missing-file exception traces;
- Multi-host environment records;
- Audit ledgers and code discovery records;
- State-merge proposals with explicit action-support sets.

The receiver must infer the underlying relational laws from observed operational phenomena rather than reading stated rules.

### 5. Standard Receiver Question Skeleton

Every receiver is prompted strictly with its assigned fixture and the identical 7-question skeleton:
1. **Materially Different States:** What state distinctions, if any, are necessary to explain the observed traces?
2. **Action Enablement:** Which actions appear enabled under which observed conditions?
3. **Transition Rules:** What additional rule would best explain transitions between states?
4. **State Merge Evaluation:** Which proposed state merges preserve behavior and which change behavior?
5. **Underdetermined Claims:** Which stronger claims are underdetermined by the records?
6. **Temporal Observation vs Model Updates:** What later evidence changes the current model versus the historical observation record?
7. **Competing Explanations & Falsifiers:** What counterexample or observation would distinguish competing explanations?

*Mandatory Directive:* Where the records cannot choose among competing hypotheses, the receiver must explicitly output `UNDERDETERMINED`.
